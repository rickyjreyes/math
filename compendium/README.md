# Mathematical compendia

This directory contains **reference compendia**, distinct from the original research manuscripts under [`papers/`](../papers/) and their compiled versions under [`preprints/`](../preprints/).

A compendium collects standard mathematical results, reusable lemmas, conventions, and cross-references. Its inclusion does **not** imply that the underlying statements are original discoveries, that WCT's physical hypotheses are established, or that the complete document is Lean-formalized.

## Volumes

### Volume I — Elementary Mathematical Foundations of Wave Confinement Theory

[Read the PDF](wct-elementary-mathematics/paper.pdf) · [LaTeX source](wct-elementary-mathematics/main.tex) · [Scope and compilation notes](wct-elementary-mathematics/README.md)

Covers 25 corrected WCT registry IDs in seven core sections: conventions; Sobolev embeddings; entropy and counting; differential operator identities; variational and gradient-flow lemmas; flat-product and embedded-torus modes; cross-references. The report also records limitations.

The [corrected canonical registry](https://github.com/rickyjreyes/geometry_of_resonance/blob/main/WCT_FULL_EQUATION_LIST_CORRECTED.md) remains authoritative for registry wording and statuses.
