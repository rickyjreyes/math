# GC28: explicit infinite-dimensional zero-fiber witness for the original phase-current map

**Date:** November 4, 2026  
**Type:** Analytical exact-operator partial result, **not** a Lean formalization or a generic-fiber classification.  
**Original target:** [GC28 canonical problem](../reyes-problem-repository/problems/06-inverse-observation-control/GC28.md), using the [GC08 operator](../reyes-problem-repository/problems/02-confinement-curvature/GC08.md).  
**Audit lineage:** [Negative-result source-and-scope audit](NEGATIVE_RESULT_SCOPE_AUDIT_2026-11-02.md).

## Frozen model

Fix `ε > 0` and the flat torus `T² = (R / 2πZ)²`. For a smooth complex field `u : T² → C`, use exactly the GC08 phase-current operator

```math
K_{\varepsilon}(u)
=\nabla\cdot\left(
\frac{\operatorname{Im}(\overline{u}\nabla u)}
{\varepsilon+|u|^2}
\right).
```

Define the equivalence relation generated by constant complex phases and torus translations,

```math
v(x)=e^{i\theta}u(x-y),\qquad
\theta\in\mathbb R,\ y\in\mathbb T^2.
```

The regularizer makes the denominator strictly positive, so no zero-set exclusion is needed. The examples below are, in fact, everywhere strictly positive.

## Theorem (exact GC28 zero-fiber obstruction)

1. If `f ∈ C∞(T²,R)`, then `K_ε(f) ≡ 0`. Thus the zero fiber contains the entire real smooth subspace, including its open cone of strictly positive functions.
2. For each integer `N ≥ 1`, set
   ```math
   U_N=\left(0,\frac{1}{4N}\right)^N,\qquad
   f_a(x_1,x_2)=1+\sum_{j=1}^N a_j\cos(jx_1),
   \quad a\in U_N.
   ```
   All `f_a` are smooth, nonconstant, strictly positive, and have `K_ε(f_a)=0`. If `f_a` and `f_b` differ only by constant phase and translation, then `a=b`.
3. Consequently, `K_ε` is noninjective even modulo constant phase and translation, and its zero fiber contains families of inequivalent smooth, nowhere-zero fields of arbitrarily high finite parameter dimension. In particular, the zero fiber is infinite-dimensional as a subset of `C∞(T²,C)`.

## Proof

For real `f`, both `f` and `∇f` are real, so `Im(conj(f)∇f)=0` pointwise. Taking its divergence gives `K_ε(f)=0`, for every positive `ε`.

For `a∈U_N`, `Σ_j a_j < 1/4`, hence `f_a(x)>3/4`. The function `f_a` is strictly positive, so an equality

```math
f_b(x)=e^{i\theta} f_a(x-y)
```

forces `e^{iθ}=1`. For each `1≤j≤N`, the complex Fourier coefficient of `f_a` at `(j,0)` is `a_j/2`. Translation by `y` multiplies this coefficient by a unit-modulus factor `e^{-ijy_1}` and hence preserves its absolute value. Equality after translation implies `a_j/2=b_j/2` for every `j`, so `a=b`. Thus the entire open `N`-parameter family survives the phase/translation quotient. Since `N` was arbitrary, there is no finite dimension cap on these explicit zero-fiber families. □

**Simplest witness:** `u≡1` and `v(x)=1+(1/2)cos(x_1)` satisfy `K_ε(u)=K_ε(v)=0` for every `ε>0`. One is constant and the other nonconstant, so they cannot be equivalent under the permitted group.

## Exact scope and limits

- **Established analytically:** noninjectivity for the *original* GC28/GC08 torus operator; an infinite-dimensional zero fiber, even using smooth strictly positive fields; explicit quotient-distinguishing Fourier invariants.
- **Not established:** whether `K_ε^{-1}(g)` is infinite-dimensional for **generic** `g`, a classification of all fibers, or any Sobolev stability/inverse estimate. The constant-phase real fields here form a special class, not a genericity argument.
- **Not claimed:** exact GC28 Lean verification, a new PDE evolution theorem, a universal WCT non-identifiability theorem, or equivalence to M6B's different Laplacian-quotient operator on a Dirichlet square.
- **Repository policy:** retain the GC28 candidate status and every original registry entry, source theorem, manuscript and historical certificate. A formal GC28 status transition would require the ordinary review contract and may demand generic-fiber classification.

The proof uses only the original operator formula and elementary torus Fourier coefficients; it does **not** rely on any unproved correspondence between GC28 and M6B.
