## Executive mathematical conclusions

1. The literal, corrected, fixed-flat, nonzero-field S004 scalar action has a nondegenerate acceleration Hessian, additional unconstrained canonical degrees of freedom, and a Hamiltonian affine in an independent canonical momentum. Those obstructions are real for the stated formulation. They do **not** exclude degenerate higher-derivative actions, derivative-dependent transformations with new constraint structure, dynamical metric/gauge/foliation completions, or independent first-order-time wave models without further analysis.
2. The S004 ultraviolet theorem is a genuine constant-coefficient **linearized** Sobolev Cauchy obstruction on specified nonzero homogeneous on-shell backgrounds. It is not a universal theorem of nonlinear wave lifetime, existence of localized states, or all physical backgrounds.
3. The R18 no-primary-finite-wavenumber result applies to a strict **static homogeneous** instability-onset mechanism. Conditional finite-frequency minima, rotating backgrounds, nonlinear wavelength selection, and spatially inhomogeneous configurations are different questions.
4. The F01 constructive results for strict binding, constrained minimizers, global H² flow, and orbital stability concern a specified fourth-order cubic–quintic Schrödinger Hamiltonian with **first-order time evolution**. They are not logically contradicted by the separate S004 **fourth-order time** obstructions.
5. **One demonstrable source-to-problem mismatch:** GC08's original phase-current divergence operator is *not* the M6B Laplacian-quotient operator. The valid M6B Lean nonuniqueness theorem cannot be called a Lean formalization of the exact GC08 counterexample. The original GC08 page itself gives a separate, elementary analytical counterexample, so this correction does not justify reversing the existing partial GC status.

## Audit method and status meaning

The audit compared repository paths and Lean theorem declarations against corresponding manuscript statements and foundational model definitions. Status labels below refer to **mathematical interpretation in the named target**, not blanket acceptance or rejection of WCT.

- **VALID_AND_APPLICABLE:** the mathematical argument applies to the *explicitly named original representative* and its hypotheses.
- **VALID_BUT_RESTRICTED:** mathematical argument established for the formal representative, but the general claim would require extra premises.
- **VALID_BUT_MISAPPLIED:** a correct result is being carried across a non-equivalent model boundary.
- **FORMALIZATION_MISMATCH:** theorem being attributed to a problem does not express that problem's exact claim.
- **INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE:** the original model, analytical correspondence, or machine verification needed for a stronger verdict has not been established.

The existence and contents of the named Lean declarations were checked by direct source retrieval. **This review did not run lake build, the axiom audit, or the historical CI suites.** Existing [October 27 formalization review](https://github.com/rickyjreyes/math/blob/main/docs/FORMALIZATION_REVIEW_2026-10-27.md), [October 30 analytic certification](https://github.com/rickyjreyes/math/blob/main/docs/ANALYTIC_CERTIFICATION_2026-10-30.md), and E68/E69 mapping records report stronger earlier checks; they remain historical assertions rather than independently reproduced checks here. A source-level verdict should not be read as a newly executed axiom certificate.

### S004 canonical correspondence, explicit check

The [original corrected action](https://github.com/rickyjreyes/geometry_of_resonance/blob/main/WCT_CORRECTED_VARIATIONAL_CLOSURE_DRAFT.md) in a fixed inertial foliation has

$
\mathcal L_{\rm orig}=\partial_\mu\bar\psi\,\partial^\mu\psi-V(\rho)
+F(\rho)\bigl[\kappa|\Box\psi|^2+\theta|\Delta\psi|^2
+\gamma\operatorname{Re}(\Box\psi\,\overline{\Delta\psi})\bigr].
$

With $\Box\psi=-\psi_{\tau\tau}+\Delta\psi$, the sign-reversed canonical density has acceleration Hessian $H_{aa}=-2\kappa F(\rho)I_2$. Since $D(\rho)=\rho+\varepsilon^2e^{-2\alpha\rho}>0$ and $F(\rho)=\rho/D(\rho)^2>0$ for $\rho>0$, $\det H_{aa}=4\kappa^2F(\rho)^2>0$. The unboundedness is not conjectural within this regular fixed-background model. By contrast, at $\rho=0$ the acceleration coefficient vanishes, changing the rank: a separate constant-rank and PDE analysis is required. A **new proposed action** may evade these hypotheses, but no repair is certified by pointing to that possibility.

### Exact GC08 correspondence correction

The [canonical GC08 problem](https://github.com/rickyjreyes/math/blob/main/reyes-problem-repository/problems/02-confinement-curvature/GC08.md) defines

$
K_\varepsilon(u)=\nabla\cdot\frac{\operatorname{Im}(\bar u\nabla u)}{\varepsilon+|u|^2}
$

and asks whether a class of **real-valued**, phase/translation/rotation-invariant local operators agreeing on constant-amplitude plane waves is unique. The page supplies the distinct operators

$
C_\lambda(u)=K_\varepsilon(u)+\lambda\Delta(|u|^2).
$

For constant-amplitude plane waves the extra term vanishes; for variable amplitude it need not. This is a valid analytical nonuniqueness counterexample for the stated unrestricted GC08 class. Its remaining classification under added axioms is open.

The M6B theorem instead concerns

$
\mathcal N_\varepsilon[u]=-(\Delta u)\bar u/(|u|^2+\varepsilon^2e^{-2\alpha|u|^2}).
$

Its admissibility axioms, output space, field dependence, and regularizer differ. **The Lean M6B theorem is not a formalization of C_λ or the exact GC08 claim.** GC08's existing partial status need not be changed on this evidence; its Lean-verification label must be described precisely. See [GC08 formal review](https://github.com/rickyjreyes/math/blob/main/reyes-problem-repository/formal-reviews/GC08.md).

## Correspondence with constructive theorems: no contradiction identified

The F01 variational/NLS equation is i u_t=(Δ+k²)²u−β|u|²u+γ|u|⁴u on a specific two-dimensional whole-space phase space. Its strict binding, minimizer existence, global H² dynamics, and stability of the **set of minimizers** are mathematically distinct from the S004 action's second-time-derivative scalar field and fourth-time-order Euler–Lagrange evolution. Different equations, domains, conserved charges, and admissible phase spaces explain the apparent tension. No contradiction justifying alteration of the constructive Lean declarations was found in this source review.

Likewise, exact-shell L² exclusion (B04) does not contradict linear concentration into arbitrarily narrow **finite-width** shells (B07). E68 H¹ failure in dimensions other than 2 does not contradict E68's corrected H² estimate in dimensions 3 and 4. The E69 ε→0 failure does not contradict its positive-ε theorem. M6B stationary inverse noninjectivity does not imply nonunique time-dependent flow.
