# GC01: Which nonlinear waves remain orbitally stable?

**Plain-language question:** When a coherent wave is disturbed, which shapes survive indefinitely, and which do not?

**Status:** Candidate classification problem. Closest-literature references identified; novelty and complete open status **not established**. Existing variational orbital-stability results cover a narrower class.

[Back to the catalogue](../README.md) · [Original statement](../FORMAL_PROBLEMS.md#gc01-universal-orbital-stability) · [Screening rubric](../METHOD.md)

## Freeze the mathematical object

Let $k\ge 0$ and $\beta,\gamma>0$. Study the flow on $\mathbb R^2$

```math
i\partial_t u=(\Delta+k^2)^2u-\beta|u|^2u+\gamma|u|^4u,
\qquad u(0)\in H^2(\mathbb R^2).
```

A nonzero standing-wave profile $\phi\in H^2(\mathbb R^2)$ with frequency $\omega\in\mathbb R$ solves

```math
(\Delta+k^2)^2\phi+\omega\phi-\beta|\phi|^2\phi+\gamma|\phi|^4\phi=0.
```

Define its orbit under phase and translation by

```math
\mathcal O_\phi=\{e^{i\theta}\phi(\cdot-y):\theta\in\mathbb R,\ y\in\mathbb R^2\}.
```

**Orbital stability** means: for every $\varepsilon>0$ there is $\delta>0$ such that $\mathrm{dist}_{H^2}(u_0,\mathcal O_\phi)<\delta$ implies $\sup_{t\ge0}\mathrm{dist}_{H^2}(u(t),\mathcal O_\phi)<\varepsilon$ for the global evolution. A full theorem must establish that evolution under the stated hypotheses.

## Exact research question

For a fixed admissible parameter triple $(k,\beta,\gamma)$, classify the stationary profiles $\phi$ satisfying orbital stability, **including non-minimizing and excited states**. Establish the strongest valid spectral/variational criterion, with clearly stated nondegeneracy and spectral assumptions, and exhibit counterexamples to overbroad criteria.

This is a **classification task**, not an assertion that every standing wave is stable.

## Known context and possible obstructions

- Classical variational stability arguments concern constrained minimizing sets, not automatically all individual excited states.
- The general Hamiltonian stability framework of Grillakis, Shatah and Strauss already covers broad classes of solitary waves. A new statement must clearly separate its additional content.
- Zero modes from phase and translation, other kernel vectors, possible embedded eigenvalues and negative Hessian directions complicate claims of a simple if-and-only-if spectral test.
- The repository's [orbital stability paper](../../papers/orbital-stability-fourth-order-cubic-quintic/main.tex) and [Lean module](../../lean/MathProofs/OrbitalStability.lean) are related foundations; they are not evidence of the requested full classification.

## First falsifiable milestones

1. Determine whether every constrained minimizer covered by the existing theorem satisfies this exact orbit definition and hypotheses.
2. Construct or exclude one non-minimizing standing wave in a frozen parameter regime; rigorously compute the number of negative constrained directions.
3. Test a proposed stability criterion against phase/translation zero modes and a degenerate stationary branch.
4. Only after an analytic theorem is proved, identify the precise Lean 4 statement and its hypotheses.

**Resolution:** A theorem giving a fully specified classification on a stated family of parameters/profiles, including proofs of both directions where claimed, or a counterexample that requires the classification claim to be sharpened. Mathematical verification is not novelty certification.
