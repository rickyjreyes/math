# GC06: When can a wave confine itself?

**Plain-language question:** Can a self-interacting wave minimize its energy without an external trapping potential, and exactly at which masses?

**Status:** Candidate parameter-classification problem; repository contains relevant partial existence/minimization work. No claim that the entire question is newly open.

[Back to the catalogue](../README.md) · [Original statement](../FORMAL_PROBLEMS.md#gc06-self-confinement-criteria) · [Screening rubric](../METHOD.md)

## Freeze the model

For real $k\ge0$, $\beta,\gamma>0$ and $m>0$, define on $H^2(\mathbb R^2)$:

```math
E_{k,\beta,\gamma}(u)
=\int_{\mathbb R^2}\left(
|(\Delta+k^2)u|^2-\frac{\beta}{2}|u|^4
+\frac{\gamma}{3}|u|^6
\right)\,dx.
```

```math
I_{k,\beta,\gamma}(m)
=\inf\left\{E_{k,\beta,\gamma}(u):
u\in H^2(\mathbb R^2),\ \|u\|_{L^2}^2=m\right\}.
```

An **attained infimum** means a function $u_m$ on the constraint set satisfies $E(u_m)=I(m)$. Compactness must be understood modulo translations; phase symmetry may also be factored out.

## Exact research question

Classify the set of quadruples $(k,\beta,\gamma,m)$ for which the infimum is attained. For every parameter range with nonattainment, identify the decisive mechanism (vanishing, dichotomy, escape, or another specified defect). Determine where strict binding is sufficient, necessary, or neither under the frozen model.

This problem **does not** assert that localized minimizers exist at every mass.

## Evidence and failure modes to investigate

- Translation invariance prevents naive compactness on $\mathbb R^2$; concentration-compactness and mass splitting are mandatory checks.
- Near-zero-mass behavior, finite-frequency Fourier-shell concentration, and competing mass distributions may obstruct attainment.
- Compare the exact energy and assumptions with existing [whole-space minimizers](../../papers/whole-space-fourth-order-cubic-quintic-minimizers/main.tex), [strict subadditivity](../../papers/strict-subadditivity-fourth-order-cubic-quintic/main.tex), and [Lean minimizer infrastructure](../../lean/MathProofs/WholeSpaceMinimizers.lean).
- A numerical localized profile is not a proof of minimizing, attainment, or strict binding.

## First falsifiable milestones

1. Freeze one parameter slice $(k,\beta,\gamma)$ and compute available sharp analytic bounds on $I(m)$.
2. Identify all masses in that slice already covered by the repository's proven theorems.
3. Construct explicit separated bump or frequency-shell minimizing **sequences** for unclassified regimes and test whether they refute compactness.
4. Prove a new mass interval or one explicit nonattainment case without enlarging the assumptions silently.
