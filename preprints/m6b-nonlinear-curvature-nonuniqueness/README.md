# M6B — Nonuniqueness of regularized nonlinear curvature

**Status:** Exact analytic counterexamples, independently of any numerical computation. Not Lean-formalized and not peer-reviewed.

[PDF](paper.pdf) · [LaTeX source](../../papers/m6b-nonlinear-curvature-nonuniqueness/main.tex) · [M6B registry](https://github.com/rickyjreyes/geometry_of_resonance/blob/main/WCT_FULL_EQUATION_LIST_CORRECTED.md#m6b-nonlinear-curvature-operator)

The manuscript proves:

1. Positivity, regularity at nodes, global U(1) invariance, and the unregularized limit do **not** uniquely select a regularized nonlinear curvature closure. An explicit continuum of distinct closures is constructed.
2. For the canonical operator on (0,π)² with homogeneous Dirichlet conditions, the two explicit fields c(sin(x)sin(2y) ± i sin(2x)sin(y)) have the same curvature image and any prescribed positive mass, yet are not related by a constant phase.

**Limit:** This does not assert nonuniqueness of solutions with identical initial data for the M7 time-dependent evolution, nor does it prove existence for arbitrary forcing. Those problems require separate well-posedness and derivation analyses.
