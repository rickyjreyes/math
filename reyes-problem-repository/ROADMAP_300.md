# Mathematical problems: the 300-question catalogue

**Browse by research subject.** Every entry links to its own problem page with its mathematical statement or research target. The **first 65** are existing formal mathematical questions; **GC66–GC300** now have linked **provisional scope-v1 dossiers**, but still require model-specific mathematical review and literature comparison before any can be treated as a verified open problem.

**[Overview](./README.md) · [✅ Completed exact-scope Lean results](./VERIFIED_LEAN_RESULTS.md) · [Papers](../CONTENTS.md) · [Contribute](./CONTRIBUTING.md) · [Technical records](./docs/README.md)**

**Scoped dossier v1:** The GC66–GC300 pages in [the scoped dossier collection](scoped/README.md) add explicit benchmark models, quantities to freeze, existing audit cautions, and first falsifiable tests. They are **not** independently reviewed, automatically nonvacuous or certified open. The historical question pages remain unchanged.

**Mathematical review catalogue:** [All 300 individually linked research review pages](formal-reviews/README.md) preserve the original statements and add model notes, a resolution test, subject-level prior-art starting points, strictly scoped milestones and status recommendations. **These are not 300 independently certified novel open problems or full independent literature audits.**

**Difficulty*:** provisional research/proof burden on a 1–10 ordinal scale; not mathematical significance, novelty, proof completion, or a claim that a question is known open. Scores are editorial estimates with low confidence (approximately ±2 points), assuming the model is made precise. [Rubric and limitations](DIFFICULTY_RUBRIC.md).

**Read the statuses carefully:** **Candidate (formal)** means the original problem is specified but no full GC resolution is accepted. **Draft (scope v1; unreviewed)** denotes a research sketch with a proposed benchmark model and first test, **not** a frozen, literature-verified unsolved problem. **🟡 Partially resolved** indicates an established narrow negative or positive result, with the larger question still open. A **✅ theorem linked** in the evidence column refers only to the theorem's *precise mathematical scope*, not to completion of that GC question.

There are 12 subjects with 25 questions each. The [original 65 statements](./FORMAL_PROBLEMS.md), [five detailed research dossiers](./README.md#five-problems-to-start-with), and [completed-results index](./VERIFIED_LEAN_RESULTS.md) provide the technical starting points.

**Illustrated F01 theorem chain:** [existence, binding, and orbital stability](../docs/figures/research-posts/README.md) (conceptual figures only). The [GC01](problems/01-wave-dynamics/GC01.md), [GC06](problems/02-confinement-curvature/GC06.md), and [GC16](problems/04-variational-solitons/GC16.md) pages link the illustrations to **narrower completed theorems**; the corresponding general problem statuses below are unchanged.
