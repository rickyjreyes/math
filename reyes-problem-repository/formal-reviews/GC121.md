## Canonical mathematical statement

**Model freeze.** Use $E_W$, $M(u)=\|u\|_2^2$, $I_0(m)=\inf_{M=m}E_W$ on $H^2(\mathbb R^2;\mathbb C)$. For each existing real positive $H^4$ minimizer $\phi\in\mathcal M_m$, with multiplier $\omega$, use the source linearized blocks $L_+=(\Delta+1)^2-3\phi^2+5\phi^4-\omega$ and $L_-=(\Delta+1)^2-\phi^2+\phi^4-\omega$ on $H^4$.

**Precise mathematical target / proven narrower result.** For every mass $m>0$ admitting such a minimizer, determine whether $\ker_{L^2}L_+=\operatorname{span}\{\partial_1\phi,\partial_2\phi\}$ and $\ker_{L^2}L_-=\operatorname{span}\{\phi\}$. Symmetries imply the displayed inclusions; the novel issue is excluding *additional* kernel directions at masses and branch points.

**Original scope text (preserved for comparison):**

For $m>0$, let $\mathcal M_m$ be all minimizers of $I_0(m)=\inf_{M(u)=m}E_W(u)$ in $H^2(\mathbb R^2)$. For every real, positive $\phi\in\mathcal M_m\cap H^4$ with multiplier $\omega$, determine whether $\ker_{L^2}L_+=\operatorname{span}\{\partial_1\phi,\partial_2\phi\}$ and $\ker_{L^2}L_-=\operatorname{span}\{\phi\}$.

## Resolution criterion

Prove nonzero real positive minimizer existence for the claimed masses, domain/self-adjointness of the Hessian blocks, and both equalities; a counterexample is any additional normalizable zero mode or a mass at which a qualified claim fails.

**Proof standard:** Give all quantifiers, functional spaces, parameter ranges, symmetries, boundary conditions, and the appropriate affirmative proof or explicit admissible negative witness. Lean 4 verification is valuable when a new exact theorem or checker is claimed, but is not implied by this editorial page.
