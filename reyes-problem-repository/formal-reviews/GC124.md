## Canonical mathematical statement

**Model freeze.** Fix $T_L^2=(\mathbb R/L\mathbb Z)^2$, $L=8\pi$, $m>0$, $u\in H^2(T_L^2;\mathbb C)$, $M(u)=m$, and $E_L(u)=\int(|(\Delta+1)u|^2-\frac12|u|^4+\frac13|u|^6)$. Keep the original stripe class $u=f(x_1)$ and fourfold-invariant localized class with $\int_{B_{L/4}(0)}|u|^2\ge0.9m$.

**Precise mathematical target / proven narrower result.** For each $m>0$ determine the sign of $D(m)=\inf_{\mathcal A_{\rm str}(m)}E_L-\inf_{\mathcal A_{\rm loc}(m)}E_L$, classifying $D>0,D=0,D<0$ on $(0,\infty)$. Both infima must use the same torus and mass; a nonzero infinite-plane periodic stripe has infinite $L^2$ mass.

**Original scope text (preserved for comparison):**

On $\mathbb T_L^2=(\mathbb R/L\mathbb Z)^2$ with $L=8\pi$ and mass $m>0$, define $E_L(u)=\int(|(\Delta+1)u|^2-\tfrac12|u|^4+\tfrac13|u|^6)$. Let $\mathcal A_{str} =\{u(x_1,x_2)=f(x_1)\in H^2:M=m\}$, and $\mathcal A_{loc}=\{u\in H^2:M=m,\int_{B_{L/4}(0)}|u|^2\ge0.9m,\ u\circ R_{\pi/2}=u\}$. Classify masses satisfying $\inf_{\mathcal A_{loc}}E_L<\inf_{\mathcal A_{str}}E_L$, or equality.

## Resolution criterion
