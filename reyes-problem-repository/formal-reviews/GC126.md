**Precise source target, reproduced without changing mathematical quantifiers:**

Let $u_n\in H^2(\mathbb R^2)$ obey $\|u_n\|_2=1$, $\sup_n\|u_n\|_{H^2}<\infty$, and $\operatorname{supp}\widehat u_n\subset\{\xi:||\xi|-1|\le1/n\}$.
