## Canonical mathematical statement

**Model freeze.** For $m>0$, define $I(m)=\inf_{\|u\|_2^2=m}E_W(u)$, $B=\{m>0:\forall\mu\in(0,m),\ I(m)<I(\mu)+I(m-\mu)\}$ and $\Delta_m(\mu)=I(\mu)+I(m-\mu)-I(m)$. Use the exact W energy and $H^2(\mathbb R^2)$ admissible class.

**Precise mathematical target / proven narrower result.** Determine the full set $B$ and its boundary. Separately, for $m\in B$, decide whether $b(m)=\inf_{0<\mu<m}\Delta_m(\mu)/\min(\mu,m-\mu)>0$. Do not identify a sufficient mass window from existing theorems with a proved *sharp* transition; the set B may have multiple components or no single threshold.

**Original scope text (preserved for comparison):**

For $m>0$ set $I(m)=\inf_{M(u)=m}E_W(u)$. Define $B=\{m>0:\forall\mu\in(0,m),\ I(m)<I(\mu)+I(m-\mu)\}$ and $\Delta_m(\mu)=I(\mu)+I(m-\mu)-I(m)$. Determine the set B and its boundary, and whether $\inf_{0<\mu<m}\Delta_m(\mu)/\min(\mu,m-\mu)>0$ for each m in B.

## Resolution criterion

Classify the binding set with exact quantified lower and upper estimates or explicit splitting equalities; for uniform strength prove b(m)>0 or a sequence of splits with ratio tending to zero.

**Proof standard:** Give all quantifiers, functional spaces, parameter ranges, symmetries, boundary conditions, and the appropriate affirmative proof or explicit admissible negative witness. Lean 4 verification is valuable when a new exact theorem or checker is claimed, but is not implied by this editorial page.

## Notes / remaining modeling decisions

Strong binding and strict subadditivity differ. A limit μ→0 can defeat a uniform strength estimate even if all splits are strictly positive.

**Existing audit (P1*):** Define the mass threshold independently of the binding conclusion to avoid circularity.
