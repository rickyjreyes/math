**Precise source target, reproduced without changing mathematical quantifiers:**

Let real $u\in C^2_tH^4_x(\mathbb T^2)$ solve $u_{tt}+(\Delta+1)^2u=0$. Derive and prove conservation of $H=\tfrac12\int(|u_t|^2+|(\Delta+1)u|^2)dx$ and spatial momenta $P_j=\int u_t\partial_j u\,dx$. Determine whether H controls $\|u\|_{H^2}$ uniformly after additionally fixing its projection onto the finite shell $\{k\in\mathbb Z^2:|k|=1\}$.

**Requested decision.** Determine whether the exact quantified claim above holds for the specified admissible class. If it holds, identify the strongest necessary and sufficient conditions available in the frozen model; if not, construct a counterexample or prove an obstruction with explicitly stated parameters, topology and norm.
