Let $\Lambda\subset\mathbb R^2$ be generated by wavevectors $k_1=(1,0)$, $k_2=(-1/2,\sqrt3/2)$, $k_3=-k_1-k_2$, and let $\mathbb T_\Lambda$ be the dual reciprocal torus. Define stripe trials $u=a\cos(k_1\cdot x)$ and hexagon trials $u=a\sum_{j=1}^3\cos(k_j\cdot x)$. Determine the exact minimum energy within each trial family and which is lower.
