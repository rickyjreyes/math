**Precise source target, reproduced without changing mathematical quantifiers:**

For each N≥4 let $L_N=2\pi N$ and $\mathbb T_{L_N}^2$ be the square torus. Independently sample circular complex Gaussian coefficients $g_k$ indexed by integer $k\in\mathbb Z^2$ with $0<|k|\le3N$ and variance $(1+|k|^2/N^2)^{-3}$. Set $A_N(0,x)=N^{-1}\sum_{0<|k|\le3N}g_ke^{ik\cdot x/N}$, and solve $A_t=\Delta A+A-|A|^2A$. Let $D_N(t)$ be the number of spatial zeros with nonzero real Jacobian divided by $L_N^2$, with D_N=0 if the zeros are not isolated or not finite. Decide whether there exist $\alpha>0,c\in(0,\infty)$ such that $\lim_{t\to\infty}\lim_{N\to\infty}t^\alpha\mathbb E D_N(t)=c$.
