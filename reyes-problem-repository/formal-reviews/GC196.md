**Precise source target, reproduced without changing mathematical quantifiers:**

Let $S^3$ have its standard unit round metric, and $S^2$ its unit round metric. Let $\omega\in\Omega^2(S^2)$ be the smooth positively oriented area form **normalized by** $\int_{S^2}\omega=1$. For every smooth $n:S^3\to S^2$, define
$$
F_n=n^*\omega,\qquad
E(n)=\int_{S^3}\bigl(|dn|^2+|F_n|^2\bigr)\,dV.
$$
Since $H^2(S^3)=0$, choose a one-form $A_n$ with $dA_n=F_n$. Define the Hopf integer $Q(n)=\int_{S^3}A_n\wedge F_n\in\mathbb Z$, independent of the choice of primitive. For every integer Q let
$$
I_Q=\inf\{E(n):n\in C^\infty(S^3,S^2),\ Q(n)=Q\}.
$$
Every Q is represented by some smooth map because $\pi_3(S^2)\cong\mathbb Z$.

## Resolution criterion

**Verified obstruction to the earlier target.** Take the unique **coexact** Hodge primitive of F, with $dA_n=F_n$ and $\delta A_n=0$. On the compact sphere, the positive spectral gap of the Hodge Laplacian on coexact one-forms gives
$$
\|A_n\|_{L^2}\le C_{S^3}\|F_n\|_{L^2}.
$$
Cauchy–Schwarz then yields
$$
|Q(n)|=\bigl|\int A_n\wedge F_n\bigr|
\le C_{S^3}\|F_n\|_2^2\le C_{S^3}E(n).
$$
Consequently $I_Q\ge c_{S^3}|Q|$ for all Q. **No bound** $I_Q\le C(1+|Q|)^{3/4}$ with a Q-independent constant can hold as $|Q|\to\infty$ for this compact-domain model.
