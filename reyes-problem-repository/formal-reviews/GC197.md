Let $n_0,n_1\in C^\infty(S^3,S^2)$ have equal Hopf invariant. Fix $y_0\in S^2$ regular for both, with links $K_i=n_i^{-1}(y_0)\subset S^3$. Determine whether there is a smooth homotopy $n_s$ from n0 to n1 such that **y0 is a regular value of every n_s**. Classify the obstruction in terms of ambient isotopy of K0 and K1.

## Resolution criterion

**Resolution and safeguards.** Without the regular-value requirement, equal Hopf degree already implies homotopy and the question is classical. Under the restriction, preimage links form a smooth isotopy, so distinct isotopy types give an immediate obstruction.
