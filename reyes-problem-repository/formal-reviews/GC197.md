# GC197: Knot type versus Hopf degree

**Status:** Refined draft; regular-value isotopy obstruction.
**Machine registry:** candidate (unchanged). **Audit type:** individual mathematical scoping review, not independent peer review or a Lean-checked new result.
**Sources:** [Existing scoped mathematical target](../scoped/GC197.md) · [Original GC page](../problems/08-topology-multicomponent/GC197.md) · [Six-step standard](../PROBLEM_STANDARD.md) · [Review queue](REVIEW_QUEUE.md)

## Canonical mathematical statement

**Model freeze.** Fix smooth maps $n_0,n_1:S^3\to S^2$ of equal Hopf invariant and a specified common regular value $y_0\in S^2$. Their links $K_i=n_i^{-1}(y_0)$ carry the regular-value normal framing. Allowed homotopies are $C^\infty$ maps $n_s$ for $s\in[0,1]$ such that $y_0$ stays regular for every $s$.

**Precise mathematical target / proven narrower result.** Determine necessary and sufficient conditions, in terms of the links and their induced framings, for the existence of such a regular-value-preserving homotopy $n_s$. Under an allowed homotopy the preimage in $S^3\times[0,1]$ projects submersively to the parameter interval and yields a framed link isotopy. Thus nonisotopic framed links obstruct the restricted homotopy, although equal Hopf invariants guarantee unrestricted homotopy.

**Original scope text (preserved for comparison):**

Let $n_0,n_1\in C^\infty(S^3,S^2)$ have equal Hopf invariant. Fix $y_0\in S^2$ regular for both, with links $K_i=n_i^{-1}(y_0)\subset S^3$. Determine whether there is a smooth homotopy $n_s$ from n0 to n1 such that **y0 is a regular value of every n_s**. Classify the obstruction in terms of ambient isotopy of K0 and K1.

## Resolution criterion

Prove a framed-isotopy classification including any required extension/homotopy data, or give explicit same-Hopf maps with framed-link obstruction. Do not claim isotopy sufficiency without proving an extension theorem.

**Proof standard:** Give all quantifiers, functional spaces, parameter ranges, symmetries, boundary conditions, and the appropriate affirmative proof or explicit admissible negative witness. Lean 4 verification is valuable when a new exact theorem or checker is claimed, but is not implied by this editorial page.

## Closest known results

- [Hatcher, Algebraic Topology](https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/AT/ATchapters.html): Equality of Hopf invariants classifies unrestricted $S^3\to S^2$ homotopy, not regular-value-preserving homotopy.
- [Pontryagin–Thom framed bordism construction](https://web.math.princeton.edu/~petero/konyv8.pdf): Framed bordism is weaker than the regular-value-preserving isotopy demanded here.

**Novelty boundary:** The references support the stated classical or related results, not a globally complete novelty search. Any new claim needs focused source-to-statement comparison, and any proposed analytic derivation above needs independent checking.

## Falsifiable first milestones

1. Prove a smooth family with a common regular value induces a framed link isotopy.
2. Construct explicit equal-Hopf maps whose regular-value links have different knot type.
3. Investigate whether framed isotopy is sufficient by constructing or obstructing a homotopy of maps with that preimage family.
4. Compare the model and theorem quantifiers with the closest cited source; document any genuinely missing result before novelty or closure is claimed.

## Notes / remaining modeling decisions

Do not confuse ordinary homotopy, framed cobordism and framed ambient isotopy. The latter is enforced by the unusually strong regular-value-at-every-slice condition.

**Existing audit (P0*):** Under S³→S² maps the Hopf invariant classifies homotopy; same-charge maps are homotopic. Require additional restrictions for a nontrivial obstruction.
