**Precise source target, reproduced without changing mathematical quantifiers:**

On the unit ball $B^3$, fix $\delta\in(0,1)$, $E_\delta(u)=\int_{B^3}(|\nabla u|^2+\delta^{-2}(1-|u|^2)^2)dx$. For any smooth $u_0,u_1:B^3\to\mathbb C$ with common boundary trace u=1, and whose regular zero sets have distinct link isotopy classes, define $\mathcal B_\delta(u_0,u_1)=\inf_{\gamma\in C([0,1],H^1_1(B^3)),\gamma(0)=u_0,\gamma(1)=u_1}\max_s E_\delta(\gamma(s))-\max(E_\delta(u_0),E_\delta(u_1))$. Determine whether any such endpoints have $\mathcal B_\delta>0$.
