**Precise source target, reproduced without changing mathematical quantifiers:**

Fix elementary cellular automaton Rule 110 with synchronous updates on bi-infinite $\{0,1\}^\mathbb Z$ and finite-support encodings above a fixed periodic background. Consider uniform computable encoders E that map a binary-encoded Turing machine M, input x, and tape bound S to a Rule-110 initial state of length polynomial in $|M|+|x|+S$, and decoders that reproduce M's bounded-tape computation after at most $p(T,S,|M|)$ CA steps whenever M halts in T steps. Determine the smallest possible polynomial degree of p among valid uniform simulations.
