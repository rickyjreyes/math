## Canonical mathematical statement

**Model freeze.** Use the original unitary convention $\widehat f(\xi)=(2\pi)^{-1}\int_{\mathbb R^2}e^{-ix\cdot\xi}f(x)\,dx$ for Schwartz $f$, extended to $H^2(\mathbb R^2;\mathbb C)$; take the Laplacian as an $L^2$ distribution/derivative and assert Fourier equality almost everywhere.

**Precise mathematical target / proven narrower result.** Formalize the exact theorem $\|\widehat f\|_2=\|f\|_2$ and $\widehat{\Delta f}(\xi)=-|\xi|^2\widehat f(\xi)$ a.e. for every $f\in H^2(\mathbb R^2;\mathbb C)$ in Lean 4, checking Schwartz-density extension and conventions. This is a verified-formalization deliverable, not an open analytic claim.

**Original scope text (preserved for comparison):**

On $\mathbb R^2$, normalize $\widehat f(\xi)=(2\pi)^{-1}\int e^{-ix\cdot\xi}f(x)dx$ for Schwartz f. Define $\|f\|_{H^2}^2=\int(1+|\xi|^2)^2|\widehat f|^2d\xi$. Formalize the exact assertion $\forall f\in H^2(\mathbb R^2;\mathbb C),\ \|\widehat f\|_{L^2}=\|f\|_{L^2}\land \widehat{\Delta f}(\xi)=-|\xi|^2\widehat f(\xi)\text{ a.e.}$, including Schwartz-density extension.
