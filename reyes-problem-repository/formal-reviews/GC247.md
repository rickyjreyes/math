## Canonical mathematical statement

**Model freeze.** Let $H_0,H$ be self-adjoint on complex Hilbert spaces and assume wave operators $W_\pm:\mathcal H_{ac}(H_0)\to\mathcal H_{ac}(H)$ exist and are unitary onto the latter subspace. Define $S=W_+^*W_-$ on $\mathcal H_{ac}(H_0)$.

**Precise mathematical target / proven narrower result.** Prove $S^*S=SS^*=I_{\mathcal H_{ac}(H_0)}$. This is a standard algebraic consequence: $S^*S=W_-^*W_+W_+^*W_-=W_-^*W_-=I$, and similarly $SS^*=I$. The substantive PDE problem of existence and completeness of $W_\pm$ is **not** part of the quantified source hypothesis.

**Original scope text (preserved for comparison):**

Let H0,H be self-adjoint operators on a complex Hilbert space. Assume incoming/outgoing wave operators W± exist and are **unitary from** the absolutely continuous subspace $\mathcal H_{ac}(H_0)$ **onto** $\mathcal H_{ac}(H)$. Define scattering operator $S=W_+^*W_-$ on $\mathcal H_{ac}(H_0)$. Prove $S^*S=SS^*=I$.

## Resolution criterion

For a Lean deliverable, define the absolutely continuous subspaces and bounded unitary wave operators with compatible types, then prove the two identities with kernel checking, no sorry and exact correspondence. Do not market conditional unitarity as a novel scattering theorem.

**Proof standard:** Give all quantifiers, functional spaces, parameter ranges, symmetries, boundary conditions, and the appropriate affirmative proof or explicit admissible negative witness. Lean 4 verification is valuable when a new exact theorem or checker is claimed, but is not implied by this editorial page.
