## Canonical mathematical statement

**Model freeze.** Take $H\in C^1([0,1]\times\mathbb T^2;S^1)$, coordinate loops $\gamma_1,\gamma_2$, and $n_j(s)=(2\pi i)^{-1}\int_0^{2\pi}\overline{H(s,\gamma_j(t))}\,\partial_tH(s,\gamma_j(t))\,dt\in\mathbb Z$.

**Precise mathematical target / proven narrower result.** Prove that for every $s\in[0,1]$ and $j\in\{1,2\}$, $n_j(s)=n_j(0)$. This is ordinary homotopy invariance of the winding number for an $S^1$-valued homotopy. A PDE-specific new theorem would separately have to show that its evolution remains nonvanishing with this regularity.

**Original scope text (preserved for comparison):**

Let $H\in C^1([0,1]\times\mathbb T^2;S^1)$. For each s, define winding numbers $n_j(s)=(2\pi i)^{-1}\int_0^{2\pi}\overline{H(s,\gamma_j(t))}\,\frac d{dt}H(s,\gamma_j(t))\,dt$, where $\gamma_j$ are the coordinate generating loops. Prove $n_j(s)\in\mathbb Z$ and $n_j(s)$ is constant in s for j=1,2.
