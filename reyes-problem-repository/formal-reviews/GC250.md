## Canonical mathematical statement

**Model freeze.** Given $P\in\mathbb Q[X_1,\dots,X_n]$, $q\in\mathbb Q^n$, and exact rational evaluation $P(q)<0$, embed coefficients and q into $\mathbb R$. A checker accepts a certificate only after kernel-reduced rational evaluation proves the inequality.

**Precise mathematical target / proven narrower result.** Prove soundness: $P(q)<0$ implies $\neg(\forall x\in\mathbb R^n,\ P_\mathbb R(x)\ge0)$, because $P_\mathbb R(\iota q)=\iota(P(q))<0$. The implication is elementary; novelty, if any, is in designing and testing the trusted certificate parser, elaboration boundary and Lean tactic.

**Original scope text (preserved for comparison):**

Define a certificate pair $(P,q)$ with $P\in\mathbb Q[X_1,…,X_n]$, $q\in\mathbb Q^n$, and exact rational evaluation $\mathrm{eval}(P,q)<0$. Prove a trusted checker accepting such a pair establishes $\neg(\forall x\in\mathbb R^n,P(x)\ge0)$, using the natural rational-to-real embedding.

## Resolution criterion

Give a sound Lean checker for the frozen certificate syntax and prove its accepted outputs correspond to an exact theorem refutation; do not claim general automatic counterexample checking of arbitrary logical propositions.

**Proof standard:** Give all quantifiers, functional spaces, parameter ranges, symmetries, boundary conditions, and the appropriate affirmative proof or explicit admissible negative witness. Lean 4 verification is valuable when a new exact theorem or checker is claimed, but is not implied by this editorial page.
