**Precise source target, reproduced without changing mathematical quantifiers:**

Let $u_n\rightharpoonup u$ in L²($\mathbb T$). For each fixed $g\in L²$ define interferometric measurement $O_g(v)=|\langle v,g\rangle|^2$, and define intensity $I(v)=\int|v|^4$, where finite. Prove $O_g(u_n)\to O_g(u)$, but determine whether $I(u_n)\to I(u)$ follows for every uniformly H¹-bounded sequence, and exhibit a counterexample if the uniform regularity condition is dropped.

If it holds, identify the strongest necessary and sufficient conditions available in the frozen model; if not, construct a counterexample or prove an obstruction with explicitly stated parameters, topology and norm. ## Resolution criterion

**Decisive test and caveat.** On the compact 1D torus, a uniform H¹ bound gives strong L⁴ convergence along weak-L²-convergent sequences by Rellich; this important compactness distinction must be reflected in the answer.

A complete answer must give a theorem with all domains, parameters, quantifiers, function spaces, boundary/initial data and the relevant topology, plus a proof or an admissible counterexample. Identify known theorems first; avoid treating a classical consequence as novel.
