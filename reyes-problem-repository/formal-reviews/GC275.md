**Precise source target, reproduced without changing mathematical quantifiers:**

On a square photonic lattice with positive baseline coupling J0, let two programmable couplings be $J_x=J_0\cos\phi_x$, $J_y=J_0\cos\phi_y$, $(\phi_x,\phi_y)\in(0,\pi/2)^2$. The Bloch dispersion is $\omega(k)=\omega_0+2J_x(1-\cos k_xa)+2J_y(1-\cos k_ya)$. Derive the continuum quadratic coefficients $D_x=J_xa^2,D_y=J_ya^2$, prove local/global invertibility of phase↔(Dx,Dy) on the stated domain, and bound conditioning near its endpoints.
