**Precise source target, reproduced without changing mathematical quantifiers:**

Consider a D-stage optical path carrying bit 1 as power P₀>0 and bit 0 as zero power. Each passive stage transmits a fixed fraction 0<η<1 and introduces no gain: P_{j+1}=ηP_j. A final detector declares 1 iff P_D≥τ with 0<τ<P₀. Determine the largest integer D for which all ideal input bits are decoded correctly, and prove that no fixed P₀,η,τ permits arbitrary-depth reliable composition without energy regeneration.
