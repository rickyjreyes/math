**Precise source target, reproduced without changing mathematical quantifiers:**

For n≥1, construct a classical n-bit ripple-carry adder entirely from idealized optical XOR, AND and OR modules followed by threshold detection and fresh output re-encoding at each logical stage. Bits are encoded as phases 0/π. Each module's phase errors satisfy |e|≤ε and output detection noise |η|≤δ, and assume each primitive's ideal Boolean truth table is correct whenever ε,δ satisfy its declared margin. Determine a uniform sufficient phase/noise margin to guarantee the entire n-bit sum and carry for all 2^{2n} inputs; under independent per-gate errors ≤p, give an end-to-end failure bound in n.
