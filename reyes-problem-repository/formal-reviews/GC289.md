**Precise source target, reproduced without changing mathematical quantifiers:**

Let a phase error e(t) satisfy |de/dt|≤v almost everywhere between ideal instantaneous reset operations at times nΔ, where e(nΔ)=0, v>0 and Δ>0. Correctness of a phase-coded detector requires |e(t)|<ε for all t, with ε∈(0,π/4). Determine the necessary and sufficient maximum Δ under arbitrary adversarial drift, hence the minimum feedback sample rate 1/Δ.
