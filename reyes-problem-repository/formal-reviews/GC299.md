**Precise source target, reproduced without changing mathematical quantifiers:**

A classical stored bit is equiprobable and encoded by vacuum |0⟩ or one photon |1⟩ of energy hν. Restrict reset protocols to a zero-temperature amplitude-damping channel which loses the stored photon to an unobserved environment with probability q∈[0,1], and otherwise leaves the one-photon state, with no active drive. Output should be vacuum with error probability at most p∈[0,1/2). Determine the minimum q and mean photons emitted per reset. Separately compare the entropy decrease with the Landauer work bound k_BT[ln2−h₂(p)ln2] for a **thermal** bath at T>0 and explain why the two bath models cannot be conflated.
