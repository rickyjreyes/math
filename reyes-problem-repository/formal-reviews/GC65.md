## Canonical mathematical statement

**Model freeze.** Fix the real Swift–Hohenberg flow $u_t=2u-(1+\Delta)^2u-u^3$ on $\mathbb T^2=(\mathbb R/2\pi\mathbb Z)^2$, initial $u_0\in H^2(\mathbb T^2;\mathbb R)$. For each integer $N\ge1$, $P_N$ projects to $|k|\le N$, and $Q_N=I-P_N$.

**Precise mathematical target / proven narrower result.** Determine whether there exist $N$ and a globally Lipschitz graph $\Phi:P_NH^2\to Q_NH^2$ such that $\mathcal M=\{p+\Phi(p)\}$ is forward invariant and attracts every bounded $B\subset H^2$ exponentially: $\operatorname{dist}_{H^2}(S(t)B,\mathcal M)\le C_Be^{-\lambda t}$ for $t\ge0$, with $\lambda>0$ uniform in B. This is an inertial-manifold problem, stronger than a finite-dimensional attractor.

**Original scope text (preserved for comparison):**

Let $u_t=\mu u-(1+\Delta)^2u-u^3$ on the square torus $\mathbb T^2=(\mathbb R/2\pi\mathbb Z)^2$, with $\mu=2$, real initial data $H^2$. For each integer $N\ge1$, let $P_N$ be the $L^2$-orthogonal projection onto Fourier modes $|k|\le N$, and $Q_N=I-P_N$. Determine whether **there exist** $N$ and a globally Lipschitz $\Phi:P_NH^2\to Q_NH^2$ such that $\mathcal M=\{p+\Phi(p)\}$ is forward invariant and, for every bounded subset $B\subset H^2$, there are $C_B,\lambda>0$ with $\operatorname{dist}_{H^2}(S(t)B,\mathcal M)\le C_B e^{-\lambda t}$ for all $t\ge0$.

## Resolution criterion

Prove explicit N, spectral gap/cone invariance and graph tracking estimates in H², or prove no such graph exists for the frozen flow and Fourier cutoffs.

**Proof standard:** Give all quantifiers, functional spaces, parameter ranges, symmetries, boundary conditions, and the appropriate affirmative proof or explicit admissible negative witness. Lean 4 verification is valuable when a new exact theorem or checker is claimed, but is not implied by this editorial page.
