## Canonical mathematical statement

**Model freeze.** Take $i u_t=(\Delta+1)^2u-|u|^2u+|u|^4u$ on $\mathbb R^2$, $u_0\in H^2(\mathbb R^2;\mathbb C)$. Use $u=e^{i\lambda t}\phi$ with $\phi\in H^4(\mathbb R^2;\mathbb R)\setminus\{0\}$, $\lambda>0$, solving $(\Delta+1)^2\phi+\lambda\phi-\phi^3+\phi^5=0$. Define $L_+=(\Delta+1)^2+\lambda-3\phi^2+5\phi^4$, $L_-=(\Delta+1)^2+\lambda-\phi^2+\phi^4$, $\mathcal A=J\operatorname{diag}(L_+,L_-)$.

**Precise mathematical target / proven narrower result.** First prove nonzero standing-wave existence and an isolated $\pm i\nu$ eigenpair with $0<\nu<\lambda<1/4$. The upper bound $\lambda<1/4$ is necessary for real localized profiles: $\phi^4-\phi^6\le\phi^2/4$ and the stationary energy identity exclude $\lambda\ge1/4$. Given a verified profile, internal mode, spectral projection and modulation slice, determine whether the mode amplitude tends to zero for a specified small $H^2$-open perturbation family; any Fermi golden rule must be proved nonzero, not assumed.

**Original scope text (preserved for comparison):**

For each nonzero real $\phi\in H^4(\mathbb R^2)$ satisfying $F_\omega(\phi)=0$, define $\mathcal A_\phi$ as in W. Call an internal mode an $L^2$ eigenvector of $\mathcal A_\phi$ at $i\nu$, $0<\nu<1$, isolated from the essential spectrum. Determine whether **there exist** such $(\omega,\phi,\nu)$ and an $H^2$-open perturbation class of solutions whose Riesz-mode projection amplitude tends to zero as $t\to+\infty$.

## Resolution criterion

Prove profile and eigenmode existence, relevant spectral isolation, modulation estimates and nonlinear radiation transfer, or construct a profile/perturbation proving failure. If the profile has unstable modes, distinguish stable-manifold damping from damping on an open neighborhood.

**Proof standard:** Give all quantifiers, functional spaces, parameter ranges, symmetries, boundary conditions, and the appropriate affirmative proof or explicit admissible negative witness. Lean 4 verification is valuable when a new exact theorem or checker is claimed, but is not implied by this editorial page.

## Falsifiable first milestones

1. Prove or exclude a nonzero $H^4$ standing-wave branch for a fixed $0<\lambda<1/4$.
2. Certify a simple isolated eigenpair in the true gap $0<\nu<\lambda$ and account for symmetry zero modes.
3. Derive a concrete nonzero nonlinear radiation coefficient and state a conditional decay theorem under exact hypotheses.
4. Compare the model and theorem quantifiers with the closest cited source; document any genuinely missing result before novelty or closure is claimed.

## Notes / remaining modeling decisions

The historical target used $0<\nu<1$, which does not locate the actual essential spectral gap. Higher-harmonic thresholds and the degeneracy of the dispersion minimum circle need separate analysis.
