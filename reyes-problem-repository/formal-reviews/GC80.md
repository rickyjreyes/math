**Precise source target, reproduced without changing mathematical quantifiers:**

Consider $i u_t=((\Delta+1)^2+\varepsilon e^{-|x|^2})u-|u|^2u+|u|^4u$ on $H^2(\mathbb R^2)$. A breather is a non-standing solution with $e^{i\omega T}u(T)=u(0)$ for some $T>0,\omega\in\mathbb R$, and $u(t)\in H^4$ uniformly for $t\in[0,T]$. Determine whether there exists an unperturbed breather with a nearby $H^2$-continuous branch of breathers for all sufficiently small $\varepsilon$.
