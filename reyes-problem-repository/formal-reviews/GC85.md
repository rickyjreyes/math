**Precise source target, reproduced without changing mathematical quantifiers:**

Let $(M_1,g_1)$ be the unit round $S^2$, and $(M_2,g_2)$ the flat square torus of area $4\pi$. For $j=1,2$ define $E_j(u)=\int_{M_j}(|(\Delta_{g_j}+1)u|^2-\tfrac12|u|^4+\tfrac13|u|^6)\,dV_{g_j}$, $u\in H^2(M_j;\mathbb C)$. For every mass $m>0$ define $I_j(m)=\inf\{E_j(u):\|u\|_2^2=m\}$. Determine whether $I_1(m)=I_2(m)$, and for which $m$ each minimizing orbit is spatially constant.
