**Precise source target, reproduced without changing mathematical quantifiers:**

For $f\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R)$ with $f\ge0$ and $\|f\|_\infty=1$, take $\Omega_{\delta,f}=\{(x,y):0<y<1+\delta f(x)\}$ for $0<\delta<1/4$. Determine for which $(\delta,f)$ there is an eigenvalue strictly below $\inf\sigma_{\rm ess}(L_{\delta,f})$.
