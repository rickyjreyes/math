## Canonical mathematical statement

**Model freeze.** Unit disk $B\subset\mathbb R^2$, $n\in\mathbb Z\setminus\{0\}$, $0<\delta<1$, $u\in H^2(B;\mathbb C)$ with trace $e^{in\theta}$. Freeze $K_1(u)=\operatorname{div}(\operatorname{Im}(\bar u\nabla u)/(1+|u|^2))$ and $E_{\delta,n}(u)=\int_B(|\nabla u|^2+\delta^{-2}(1-|u|^2)^2+|K_1(u)|^2)\,dx$. Interior zeros are allowed.

**Precise mathematical target / proven narrower result.** For fixed $n\ne0$, determine the sharp coefficient and limiting vortex geometry of $I_n(\delta)=\inf E_{\delta,n}$ as $\delta\downarrow0$. The weaker logarithmic-order target is already approachable with standard Ginzburg–Landau lower bounds and a smooth equivariant radial trial: $u=f(r)e^{in\theta}$ has purely angular regularized current and $K_1(u)=0$ distributionally. A new claim should address coefficient, $O(1)$ corrections or minimizers, not logarithmic order alone.

**Original scope text (preserved for comparison):**

Let $B$ be the unit disk and $n\in\mathbb Z\setminus\{0\}$. For $0<\delta<1$, minimize $E_{\delta,n}(u)=\int_B(|\nabla u|^2+\delta^{-2}(1-|u|^2)^2+|K_1(u)|^2)dx$ over $u\in H^2(B;\mathbb C)$ with trace $u(e^{i\theta})=e^{in\theta}$, where K uses distributional derivatives on B. Determine the sharp order of $\inf E_{\delta,n}$ as $\delta\downarrow0$.

## Falsifiable first milestones

1. Prove $K_1(f(r)e^{in\theta})=0$ for a smooth admissible radial vortex.
2. Show $c_n|\log\delta|\le I_n(\delta)\le C_n|\log\delta|$ by classical GL bounds and one $H^2$ trial.
3. Determine whether separated unit vortices lower the leading coefficient while controlling $\|K_1(u)\|_2^2$.
4. Compare the model and theorem quantifiers with the closest cited source; document any genuinely missing result before novelty or closure is claimed.
