## 2026-11-24 analytical progress (pending review)

See the [working manuscript](../../../papers/gc03-polyharmonic-mixed-power-flow/manuscript.md), [research ledger](../../research-ledgers/GC03-2026-11-24.md), and issue #100. The strict energy-subcritical global theorem is argued for all real powers; p=1 critical is existing prior art. Supercritical finite-energy obstruction and polynomial norm inflation are distinct from actual finite-time blow-up. Higher-order critical strong global theory and other supercritical cases remain open here. Candidate general coercivity Lean code has not been kernel checked. Original statement and candidate status unchanged.

Follow-up (PR #101, manuscript sections 9–11): all finite-energy data admit global mass-conserving weak evolution with an energy inequality. For every real supercritical pair, smooth data tending to zero in H^p force norm inflation for every such energy-admissible solution, ruling out a compatible continuous strong H^p flow. Higher-order critical strong global dynamics and individual-data supercritical strong classification remain unresolved. The new analytical proofs are not Lean-certified; status unchanged.


Critical follow-up (PR #101, manuscript §§12–14): all critical real powers admit strong local flow and small shifted-energy global strong flow. Any genuine finite endpoint requires a positive energy defect at its L² trace; a precise high-amplitude tail continuation criterion and finite-restart weak continuation are proved analytically. A transfer from pure defocusing uniform scattering bounds covers fourth-order critical arbitrary data in d=8..12 for r>=1+2/d, and radial data in d=5..12 under that exponent bound. General large-data critical classification remains incomplete. Candidate Lean arithmetic and energy-count lemmas are not a formalization of these PDE results. Status unchanged.


All-power follow-up (PR #101, manuscript §§16–18): a fractional time-Sobolev Duhamel estimate for the low-amplitude Lipschitz component removes the lower-power spatial integrability restriction, and Pausader's high-dimensional stability proposition removes the upper-power Lipschitz-derivative restriction. The analytical fourth-order critical transfer now covers all real lower powers in d>=8 (arbitrary data) and d>=5 (radial data). Zhang–Zheng (2010) is exact prior art for d=8. Remaining critical large-data cases: nonradial d=5..7 and higher dispersion orders p>=3; individual supercritical strong classification also remains open. New Lean scalar threshold lemmas do not formalize the analytic dynamics. Status unchanged.


General-order critical follow-up (PR #101, manuscript §§19–22): fractional stability extends the conditional critical transfer to all integer orders and all real powers, assuming a uniform pure critical Z bound. The uniform mixed slab property with Z control is equivalent to that pure bound; a hypothetical finite endpoint necessarily has a concentrating nonzero Sobolev profile, and full critical-moment concentration of an entire nonzero trajectory is impossible in finite time by localized mass flux. Background-plus-bubble scenarios and the missing pure uniform estimates are not resolved. No new unconditional parameter classification or kernel certification is claimed. Status unchanged.


Terminal-background follow-up (PR #101, manuscript §§23–26): a genuine critical endpoint has a backward strong background and residual with mass o(T-t), hence all extracted residual concentrations are faster than the natural dispersive scale. A nonzero global homogeneous pure weak profile is extracted, and the endpoint energy defect is bounded below by the pure uniform critical scattering threshold. Bounded-background exclusion for s<=2 is conditional. Faster partial concentration and the missing pure critical bounds remain unresolved. No new unconditional parameter slice or kernel certification is claimed; status unchanged.


Interaction-rigidity follow-up (PR #101, manuscript §§27–29): an all-order pure critical two-field commutator excludes nonzero global strong trajectories precompact in H^p modulo spatial translations, without radiality or center-speed restrictions. Low-frequency mass tightness is a sufficient extra hypothesis to upgrade homogeneous compactness; it is not established for missing minimal elements. Weak–strong uniqueness for 1<r<s<=2 with time-integrable reference amplitude bounds also holds in the supercritical finite-energy space. No new unconditional global parameter region or successful Lean verification is asserted. Status unchanged.
