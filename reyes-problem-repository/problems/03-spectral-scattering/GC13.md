## GC13: Interband nonlinear energy transfer

**Problem.** For the cubic–quintic Schrödinger equation on the torus T², partition Fourier modes into two disjoint finite shells A,B. Determine whether a universal bound sup_{t∈[0,T]}∑_{n∈B}|û_n(t)|² ≤ F(T,∥u₀∥H²,∑_{n∈B}|û_n(0)|²) exists with F(T,M,0)=0; prove or refute.

## Exact-shell analytical negative result (November 4, 2026)

For the F04 cubic–quintic Schrödinger equation on T² with **nonzero quintic coefficient γ**, take Fourier shells `A={n∈Z²:|n|²=1}`, `B={n∈Z²:|n|²=25}`, and smooth initial data `u₀(x₁,x₂)=cos x₁`. The initial B-shell energy is zero, while `(d/dt) û_(5,0)(0)=−iγ/32 ≠ 0` by the exact quintic Fourier coefficient, so a classical local solution immediately has positive B-shell energy. Therefore **no universal two-shell bound with F(T,M,0)=0** holds for those shells, `γ≠0`, and any `T>0`.

The existing [F04 Lean local-existence and leakage results](../../../lean/MathProofs/FourierLeakageWellPosed.lean) support the construction, while the precise GC13 2D shell-energy implication is an **analytical corollary**, not a separately encoded Lean theorem. The result does **not** classify other shell choices or estimates permitting leakage terms, and the original candidate status remains unchanged.
