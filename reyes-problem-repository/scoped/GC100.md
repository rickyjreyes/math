# GC100: Long-range defect threshold spectral diagram

**Version:** concrete mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30). **Status:** mathematically proposed, not prior-art-certified or proved. Shared definitions: [COMMON_MODELS](COMMON_MODELS.md). Historical candidate: [GC100](../problems/02-confinement-curvature/GC100.md).

## Exact question

For \(\alpha>0\) set \(H_\alpha=(\Delta+1)^2-(1+|x|^2)^{-\alpha/2}\) on \(H^4(\mathbb R^2)\). Let \(N_-(\alpha)=\dim\mathbf1_{(-\infty,0)}(H_\alpha)L^2\in\mathbb N\cup\{\infty\}\). Classify \(\alpha\mapsto N_-(\alpha)\). Independently determine whether at \(0\) there exists a distributional solution \(0\ne\psi\notin L^2\) with \(\langle x\rangle^{-s}\psi\in L^2\) for every \(s>1\).

## Proof or counterexample acceptance

Fixes the defect shape, decay power and a threshold-resonance convention; proofs may yield a phase diagram or show that no proposed simple threshold exists.
