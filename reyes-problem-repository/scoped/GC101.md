# GC101: Multiplicity of radial threshold resonances

**Version:** concrete mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30). **Status:** mathematically proposed, not prior-art-certified or proved. Shared definitions: [COMMON_MODELS](COMMON_MODELS.md). Historical candidate: [GC101](../problems/03-spectral-scattering/GC101.md).

## Exact question

For each real radial \(V\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R^2)\) with \(\operatorname{supp}V\subset B_1\) and \(\|V\|_\infty\le10\), define \(\mathcal R(V)=\{\psi\in\mathcal D'(\mathbb R^2):H_V\psi=0,\ \langle x\rangle^{-s}\psi\in L^2\ \forall s>1\}\big/\ker_{L^2}H_V\). Determine the possible dimensions of \(\mathcal R(V)\) and sharp upper bounds in this V class.

## Proof or counterexample acceptance

One must prove weighted solution spaces and the quotient well-defined; an example with a larger dimension refutes an asserted universal bound.
