# GC102: Positive embedded eigenvalues for decaying potentials

**Version:** concrete mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30). **Status:** mathematically proposed, not prior-art-certified or proved. Shared definitions: [COMMON_MODELS](COMMON_MODELS.md). Historical candidate: [GC102](../problems/03-spectral-scattering/GC102.md).

## Exact question

For \(\alpha>0\), let \(\mathcal V_\alpha=\{V\in C^\infty(\mathbb R^2;\mathbb R):\exists C_V<\infty,\ |V(x)|\le C_V(1+|x|)^{-\alpha}\}\). Define \(A=\{\alpha>0:\exists V\in\mathcal V_\alpha,\lambda>0,\psi\in H^4\setminus\{0\},\ H_V\psi=\lambda\psi\}\). Determine A and whether \(A\) has a sharp endpoint.

## Proof or counterexample acceptance

Both constructive embedded eigenfunctions and absence theorems for a decay range count; positivity refers to the essential band [0,infinity), not discrete negative bound states.
