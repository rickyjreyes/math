# GC103: Weighted resolvent at a minimum shell

**Version:** concrete mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30). **Status:** mathematically proposed, not prior-art-certified or proved. Shared definitions: [COMMON_MODELS](COMMON_MODELS.md). Historical candidate: [GC103](../problems/03-spectral-scattering/GC103.md).

## Exact question

Let \(H_0=(\Delta+1)^2\) on \(L^2(\mathbb R^2)\). For \(0<\kappa<1\), set \(R_\kappa=(H_0+\kappa)^{-1}\), \(w(x)=\langle x\rangle^{-3}\), and \(N(\kappa)=\|wR_\kappa w\|_{L^2\to L^2}\). Determine the exact growth exponent \(p=\lim_{\kappa\downarrow0}\log N(\kappa)/\log(1/\kappa)\), if it exists, and sharp two-sided bounds for small kappa.

## Proof or counterexample acceptance

The operator, side of the threshold, weights and norm are fixed; competing exponents can be resolved by explicit shell test functions and weighted upper estimates.
