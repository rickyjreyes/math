# GC106: Spectral type under two-frequency quasiperiodic modulation

**Version:** concrete mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30). **Status:** mathematically proposed, not prior-art-certified or proved. Shared definitions: [COMMON_MODELS](COMMON_MODELS.md). Historical candidate: [GC106](../problems/03-spectral-scattering/GC106.md).

## Exact question

On \(L^2(\mathbb R)\), let \(H_\varepsilon=(\partial_x^2+1)^2+\varepsilon(\cos x+\cos(\sqrt2 x))\) with domain \(H^4\) and \(0<|\varepsilon|\le1/10\). Determine whether \(H_\varepsilon\) has any \(L^2\) eigenfunctions and classify the pure point, absolutely continuous and singular continuous parts of its spectrum in the energy window [0,4].

## Proof or counterexample acceptance

The frequency vector, coupling range, energy window and self-adjoint realization are fixed; a spectral-type theorem or a concrete localized state answers an explicit part.
