# GC109: Cross-shell leakage at small times

**Version:** concrete mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30). **Status:** mathematically proposed, not prior-art-certified or proved. Shared definitions: [COMMON_MODELS](COMMON_MODELS.md). Historical candidate: [GC109](../problems/03-spectral-scattering/GC109.md).

## Exact question

For W on \(\mathbb T^2\), let \(A=\{k\in\mathbb Z^2:|k|\le2\}\) and \(B=\{k\in\mathbb Z^2:|k|\ge3\}\), with L² Fourier projectors \(P_A,P_B\). For initial \(u_0\in\operatorname{ran}P_A\) with \(\|u_0\|_{H^2}\le1\), determine the best uniform exponent \(p>0\) such that \(\|P_Bu(t)\|_{L^2}\le Ct^p\) for all \(0\le t\le t_0\) and all such data.

## Proof or counterexample acceptance

Compute the first nonzero Taylor coefficient for a witness; verify polynomial nonlinearities and a uniform local time bound. Constant initial data are not sufficient to establish sharpness.
