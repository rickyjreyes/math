# GC110: Inverse phase shifts of a two-parameter defect

**Version:** concrete mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30). **Status:** mathematically proposed, not prior-art-certified or proved. Shared definitions: [COMMON_MODELS](COMMON_MODELS.md). Historical candidate: [GC110](../problems/03-spectral-scattering/GC110.md).

## Exact question

For \((a,b)\in[-1/10,1/10]^2\), set \(H_{a,b}=(\Delta+1)^2+a e^{-|x|^2}+b e^{-2|x|^2}\). Let \(\delta_{\ell}(E;a,b)\) be the radial partial-wave scattering phase shift for angular momentum \(\ell\in\{0,1\}\), at energy \(E=1\), normalized continuously from \((a,b)=(0,0)\). Determine whether the forward map \((a,b)\mapsto(\delta_0(1;a,b),\delta_1(1;a,b))\) is injective on some open neighborhood of the origin.

## Proof or counterexample acceptance

Define the scattering phases from the exact fourth-order radial channel and verify their continuous branch; use Jacobian rank or two distinct parameter pairs as decisive outcomes.
