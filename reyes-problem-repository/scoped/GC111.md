# GC111: Spectral measure of a stationary-wave linearization

**Version:** concrete mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30). **Status:** mathematically proposed, not prior-art-certified or proved. Shared definitions: [COMMON_MODELS](COMMON_MODELS.md). Historical candidate: [GC111](../problems/03-spectral-scattering/GC111.md).

## Exact question

For each real \(\phi\in H^4\) satisfying \(F_\omega(\phi)=0\), let \(L_+(\phi)=(\Delta+1)^2-3\phi^2+5\phi^4-\omega\), a self-adjoint operator on \(H^4\). For \(f\in C_c^\infty\), denote its scalar spectral measure by \(\mu_{\phi,f}(I)=\langle f,\mathbf1_I(L_+(\phi))f\rangle\). Classify whether \(\mu_{\phi,f}\) has singular continuous component on the essential spectrum and determine its point-mass contribution.

## Proof or counterexample acceptance

A spectral measure belongs to the explicitly identified self-adjoint linearization, not to a nonlinear equilibrium itself. If no stationary phi exists, the universal component is vacuous and must be disclosed.
