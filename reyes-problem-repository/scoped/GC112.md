# GC112: Transient growth with a stable spectrum

**Version:** concrete mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30). **Status:** mathematically proposed, not prior-art-certified or proved. Shared definitions: [COMMON_MODELS](COMMON_MODELS.md). Historical candidate: [GC112](../problems/03-spectral-scattering/GC112.md).

## Exact question

On \(L^2(\mathbb R^2;\mathbb C^2)\), set \(A_M=\begin{pmatrix}-(\Delta+1)^2-1&M\\0&-(\Delta+1)^2-1\end{pmatrix}\) with domain \(H^4\oplus H^4\), \(M>0\). Compute \(G(M)=\sup_{t\ge0}\|e^{tA_M}\|_{L^2\to L^2}\) and characterize precisely when \(G(M)>1\), despite \(\sup\operatorname{Re}\sigma(A_M)=-1\).

## Proof or counterexample acceptance

The finite-dimensional Jordan coupling is an explicit nonnormal PDE example. The calculation may be elementary, but it is mathematically meaningful and testable.
