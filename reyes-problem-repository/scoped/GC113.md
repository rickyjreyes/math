# GC113: Sharp weighted forced resolvent derivative gain

**Version:** concrete mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30). **Status:** mathematically proposed, not prior-art-certified or proved. Shared definitions: [COMMON_MODELS](COMMON_MODELS.md). Historical candidate: [GC113](../problems/03-spectral-scattering/GC113.md).

## Exact question

For \(H_0=(\Delta+1)^2\), \(0<\kappa\le1\), let \(R_\kappa=(H_0+\kappa)^{-1}\). For \(s=3\) and \(r\in[0,4]\), define \(T_{r,\kappa}=\langle x\rangle^{-s}(1-\Delta)^{r/2}R_\kappa\langle x\rangle^{-s}\) on \(L^2\). Determine the smallest \(p_r\ge0\) such that \(\sup_{0<\kappa\le1}\kappa^{p_r}\|T_{r,\kappa}\|_{2\to2}<\infty\).

## Proof or counterexample acceptance

Prove both boundedness at p_r and failure below it, with functional calculus conventions fixed. This is a threshold rather than arbitrary-frequency limiting-absorption claim.
