# GC114: Nonlinear creation of a Fourier harmonic

**Version:** concrete mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30). **Status:** mathematically proposed, not prior-art-certified or proved. Shared definitions: [COMMON_MODELS](COMMON_MODELS.md). Historical candidate: [GC114](../problems/03-spectral-scattering/GC114.md).

## Exact question

On \(\mathbb T^2\) solve W with \(u_A(0,x)=A(e^{ix_1}+e^{ix_2})\), real \(0<A<1\). Define the complex Fourier coefficient \(c_A(t)=\widehat u_A(t,2e_1-e_2)\). Determine the first nonzero coefficient in the small-amplitude expansion of \(c_A(t)\) for fixed \(t\in(0,1)\), and decide whether \(\lim_{A\downarrow0}A^{-3}c_A(t)\) is identically zero in t.

## Proof or counterexample acceptance

Calculate cubic forcing by Duhamel, including phase mismatch; an exact A³ term with controlled A⁵ remainder proves harmonic generation for this data, not a general threshold theorem.
