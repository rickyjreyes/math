## Candidate mathematical question

Determine whether a small nonlinear interaction preserves a solitary mode whose linear frequency lies inside a continuous band.

**Quantifiers and domain discipline:** Specify the spectral parameter `z∈ℂ\ℝ`, energy window, perturbation V, resolvent weight and norm. For nonlinear resonance statements additionally fix an actual mode and a separate nonlinear evolution.

**Observable to define explicitly:** spectrum, resolvent bound, spectral gap, scattering amplitude or coupling coefficient. Provide the exact norm, parameter ranges, and dependence of any claimed constant, bound, rate or threshold on all inputs.

Establish structural persistence or a generic radiation obstruction.

**Valid negative outcomes:** A counterexample potential, a threshold state, or failure of a claimed uniform resolvent or spectral limit.
