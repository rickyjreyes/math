# GC121: Nondegeneracy of constrained ground states

**Scoped mathematical target v1, 2026-10-30.** Reference: [W model](COMMON_MODELS.md); original candidate: [GC121](../problems/04-variational-solitons/GC121.md). Not yet certified novel or unresolved.

## Exact statement / classification set

For \(m>0\), let \(\mathcal M_m\) be all minimizers of \(I_0(m)=\inf_{M(u)=m}E_W(u)\) in \(H^2(\mathbb R^2)\). For every real, positive \(\phi\in\mathcal M_m\cap H^4\) with multiplier \(\omega\), determine whether \(\ker_{L^2}L_+=\operatorname{span}\{\partial_1\phi,\partial_2\phi\}\) and \(\ker_{L^2}L_-=\operatorname{span}\{\phi\}\).

## Decisive resolution criterion

Classify masses where such a ground state exists and test the exact two kernel equalities, including additional radial or parameter degeneracies. The claim is conditional on nonempty minimizer sets.

## Review gate

Check admissibility, operator domain, possible vacuity, the simplest counterexamples, and matching prior art. A named theorem is accepted only under exactly the displayed assumptions; do not promote registry status automatically.
