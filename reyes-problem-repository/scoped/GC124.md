# GC124: Localized versus stripe energy on a periodic box

**Scoped mathematical target v1, 2026-10-30.** Reference: [W model](COMMON_MODELS.md); original candidate: [GC124](../problems/04-variational-solitons/GC124.md). Not yet certified novel or unresolved.

## Exact statement / classification set

On \(\mathbb T_L^2=(\mathbb R/L\mathbb Z)^2\) with \(L=8\pi\) and mass \(m>0\), define \(E_L(u)=\int(|(\Delta+1)u|^2-\tfrac12|u|^4+\tfrac13|u|^6)\). Let \(\mathcal A_{str} =\{u(x_1,x_2)=f(x_1)\in H^2:M=m\}\), and \(\mathcal A_{loc}=\{u\in H^2:M=m,\int_{B_{L/4}(0)}|u|^2\ge0.9m,\ u\circ R_{\pi/2}=u\}\). Classify masses satisfying \(\inf_{\mathcal A_{loc}}E_L<\inf_{\mathcal A_{str}}E_L\), or equality.

## Decisive resolution criterion

Both competitors have finite mass on the same domain. The fourfold symmetry replaces ambiguous full rotational invariance of the square torus; do not compare a nonzero periodic whole-plane state to a finite-L² soliton.
