# GC125: Two-component mass partition

**Scoped mathematical target v1, 2026-10-30.** Reference: [W model](COMMON_MODELS.md); original candidate: [GC125](../problems/04-variational-solitons/GC125.md). Not yet certified novel or unresolved.

## Exact statement / classification set

For \((u,v)\in H^2(\mathbb R^2;\mathbb C)^2\), define \(E(u,v)=\sum_{w=u,v}(\|(\Delta+1)w\|_2^2-\tfrac12\|w\|_4^4+\tfrac13\|w\|_6^6)+\int|u|^2|v|^2\). Under \(\|u\|_2^2+\|v\|_2^2=1\), define \(J(a)=\inf\{E(u,v):\|u\|_2^2=a,\|v\|_2^2=1-a\}\). Determine \(\operatorname{argmin}_{a\in[0,1]}J(a)\) and uniqueness modulo component exchange.

## Decisive resolution criterion

The kinetic, focusing, defocusing and coupling terms are fixed. If endpoint or mirror mass allocations tie, state nonuniqueness rather than presuming a unique partition.

## Review gate

Check admissibility, operator domain, possible vacuity, the simplest counterexamples, and matching prior art. A named theorem is accepted only under exactly the displayed assumptions; do not promote registry status automatically.
