# GC127: Barycenter-pinned strong compactness

**Scoped mathematical target v1, 2026-10-30.** Reference: [W model](COMMON_MODELS.md); original candidate: [GC127](../problems/04-variational-solitons/GC127.md). Not yet certified novel or unresolved.

## Exact statement / classification set

For fixed \(m,B>0\), let \(\mathcal A_{m,B}=\{u\in H^2(\mathbb R^2):M(u)=m,\int |x|^2|u|^2\le B,\int x|u|^2=0\}\). For any sequence \(u_n\in\mathcal A_{m,B}\) with \(E_W(u_n)\to\inf_{u\in\mathcal A_{m,B}}E_W(u)\), determine whether, after choosing constant phases, some subsequence converges strongly in \(H^2\).

## Decisive resolution criterion

The second-moment bound makes the barycenter defined and prevents translation escape. Establish coercivity and energy convergence or give a failure of H² compactness.

## Review gate

Check admissibility, operator domain, possible vacuity, the simplest counterexamples, and matching prior art. A named theorem is accepted only under exactly the displayed assumptions; do not promote registry status automatically.
