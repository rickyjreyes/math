# GC129: Slope and Hamiltonian instability at a fold

**Scoped mathematical target v1, 2026-10-30.** Reference: [W model](COMMON_MODELS.md); original candidate: [GC129](../problems/04-variational-solitons/GC129.md). Not yet certified novel or unresolved.

## Exact statement / classification set

Let \(s\mapsto(\omega_s,\phi_s)\in\mathbb R\times H^4(\mathbb R^2;\mathbb R)\) be a \(C^1\) curve of nonzero W standing waves, \(F_{\omega_s}(\phi_s)=0\). At values where \(\omega'_s\ne0\), define \(Q_s=(d/ds)M(\phi_s)/\omega'_s\) and unstable eigenvalue count \(n_+(\mathcal A_{\phi_s})\). Determine whether a sign change in \(Q_s\) forces a change in \(n_+\) under the additional conditions \(n^-(L_+)=1\) and \(\ker L_-=\operatorname{span}\phi_s\).

## Decisive resolution criterion

A full index proof must account for zero eigenvalues and Krein signature. If no admissible curve satisfies the hypotheses, report that rather than claiming a universal slope law.

## Review gate

Check admissibility, operator domain, possible vacuity, the simplest counterexamples, and matching prior art. A named theorem is accepted only under exactly the displayed assumptions; do not promote registry status automatically.
