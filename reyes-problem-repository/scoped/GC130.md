# GC130: Fixed-angular-momentum constrained minimization

**Scoped mathematical target v1, 2026-10-30.** Reference: [W model](COMMON_MODELS.md); original candidate: [GC130](../problems/04-variational-solitons/GC130.md). Not yet certified novel or unresolved.

## Exact statement / classification set

Let \(\mathcal A=\{u\in H^2(\mathbb R^2):\int|x|^2|u|^2<\infty,\ M(u)=1,\ L_z(u)=1\}\) with \(L_z(u)=\operatorname{Im}\int\bar u(x_1\partial_2-x_2\partial_1)u\). Determine whether \(\inf_{u\in\mathcal A}E_W(u)\) is attained and whether any minimizer generates a rotating relative equilibrium of W.

## Decisive resolution criterion

The moment restriction makes angular momentum well-defined. Construct an admissible state first, then test splitting/escape to infinity; energy minimization and orbital stability are separate outcomes.
