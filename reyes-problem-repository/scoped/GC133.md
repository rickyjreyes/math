# GC133: Small-dispersion minimizing sequence limit

**Scoped mathematical target v1, 2026-10-30.** Reference: [W model](COMMON_MODELS.md); original candidate: [GC133](../problems/04-variational-solitons/GC133.md). Not yet certified novel or unresolved.

## Exact statement / classification set

For \(0<\varepsilon\le1\) define \(E_\varepsilon(u)=\varepsilon\|\Delta u\|_2^2+\|u\|_2^2-\frac12\|u\|_4^4+\frac13\|u\|_6^6\), \(u\in H^2(\mathbb R^2)\), and impose \(M(u)=1\). Determine whether minimizers \(u_\varepsilon\) exist for each epsilon and, after optimally translating and rescaling, have a nonzero limit in \(L^2_{loc}\) as \(\varepsilon\to0\).

## Decisive resolution criterion

Record the scaling and topology as part of any convergence theorem; show concentration or construct sequences refuting a claimed universal profile.

## Review gate

Check admissibility, operator domain, possible vacuity, the simplest counterexamples, and matching prior art. A named theorem is accepted only under exactly the displayed assumptions; do not promote registry status automatically.
