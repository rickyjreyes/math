# GC135: First variation of a two-soliton binding gap

**Scoped mathematical target v1, 2026-10-30.** Reference: [W model](COMMON_MODELS.md); original candidate: [GC135](../problems/04-variational-solitons/GC135.md). Not yet certified novel or unresolved.

## Exact statement / classification set

For \(V(x)=e^{-|x|^2}\), \(E_\varepsilon(u)=E_W(u)+\varepsilon\int V|u|^2\), \(|\varepsilon|<1\). For a fixed real exponentially localized W standing wave \(\phi\) and \(R>1\), define \(B_\varepsilon(R)=E_\varepsilon(\phi(\cdot-Re_1)+\phi(\cdot+Re_1))-2E_\varepsilon(\phi)\). Compute \(\partial_\varepsilon B_\varepsilon(R)|_{\varepsilon=0}\) and its sharp large-R asymptotic.

## Decisive resolution criterion

The derivative is an explicit potential integral. Include cross terms and changes caused by placing the two profiles away from the fixed defect; do not silently recenter V.
