# GC137: Energy quantization for constrained Palais–Smale sequences

**Scoped mathematical target v1, 2026-10-30.** Reference: [W model](COMMON_MODELS.md); original candidate: [GC137](../problems/04-variational-solitons/GC137.md). Not yet certified novel or unresolved.

## Exact statement / classification set

For fixed \(m>0,c\in\mathbb R\), let \(u_n\in S_m\) satisfy \(E_W(u_n)\to c\) and \(\|d(E_W|_{S_m})(u_n)\|_{(T_{u_n}S_m)^*}\to0\). Determine whether every such bounded-H² sequence admits, up to subsequences and translations, a finite profile decomposition \(u_n=\sum_{j=1}^J\phi^j(\cdot-x_n^j)+r_n\) with energy splitting and \(\|r_n\|_{H^2}\to0\), for a J bounded by c and a positive energy quantum.

## Decisive resolution criterion

Prove any positive quantum and handle zero-energy dispersive remainder; the unconstrained functional may have no uniform positive bubble cost.

## Review gate

Check admissibility, operator domain, possible vacuity, the simplest counterexamples, and matching prior art. A named theorem is accepted only under exactly the displayed assumptions; do not promote registry status automatically.
