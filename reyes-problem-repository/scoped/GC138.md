# GC138: Stability of strict nonglobal minima

**Scoped mathematical target v1, 2026-10-30.** Reference: [W model](COMMON_MODELS.md); original candidate: [GC138](../problems/04-variational-solitons/GC138.md). Not yet certified novel or unresolved.

## Exact statement / classification set

Suppose \(\phi\in S_m\cap H^4\) is a stationary W profile, not a global energy minimizer, and for some \(c,\rho>0\), every \(u\in S_m\) with \(\operatorname{dist}_{H^2}(u,G\phi)<\rho\) satisfies \(E_W(u)-E_W(\phi)\ge c\operatorname{dist}_{H^2}(u,G\phi)^2\). Determine whether any global H² flow preserving M and E_W is orbitally stable around Gφ.

## Decisive resolution criterion

This conditional implication is a Lyapunov argument and may be classical. The substantive work is finding a nonglobal phi meeting the strict coercivity assumption.
