# GC139: Degenerate polynomial higher-time-derivative mechanics

**Mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30)**. Original: [GC139](../problems/05-higher-derivative-fields/GC139.md). Not literature-certified open; no Lean resolution asserted.

## Exact problem

Let \(\mathcal L_4\) be the space of real polynomials \(L(q,\dot q,\ddot q)\) of total degree at most four with rational coefficients. Classify the subset for which \(\partial^2L/\partial\ddot q^2\equiv0\), modulo total time derivatives \(dF(q,\dot q)/dt\) with \(F\) polynomial of degree at most four. For each equivalence class determine the number of freely specifiable initial values in the Euler–Lagrange equation.

## Resolution test

The finite polynomial ansatz and equivalence relation are fixed. Check separately that a degenerate Hessian does not by itself prove a healthy bounded Hamiltonian; compute actual constraint counts.
