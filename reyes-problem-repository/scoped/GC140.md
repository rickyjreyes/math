## Candidate mathematical question

Determine whether a primary degeneracy constraint in a higher-derivative scalar theory is preserved under a nonlinear interaction.

**Quantifiers and domain discipline:** Specify finite-dimensional canonical versus linearized PDE versus nonlinear flow claims. Fix allowed constraints, phase space and what “stable” means: energy bounded below, strong hyperbolicity, or growth norm.

**Observable to define explicitly:** Hamiltonian lower bound, constraint rank, dispersion roots or semigroup growth. Provide the exact norm, parameter ranges, and dependence of any claimed constant, bound, rate or threshold on all inputs.

## First decisive result

**Valid negative outcomes:** A negative canonical direction, incompatible constraint propagation, or an admissible degenerate countermodel in the quantified class.

State at least one precisely checkable theorem or counterexample on the benchmark. If the desired reference state, operator or measurement does not exist, that nonvacuity obstruction must be resolved **before** treating any conditional conclusion as substantive.
