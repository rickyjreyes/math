# GC140: Constraint preservation under a nonlinear coupling

**Mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30)**. Original: [GC140](../problems/05-higher-derivative-fields/GC140.md). Not literature-certified open; no Lean resolution asserted.

## Exact problem

For real mechanical coordinates \(q_1,q_2\), consider \(L_\varepsilon=\tfrac12(\ddot q_1+\dot q_2)^2+\tfrac12\dot q_1^2-\tfrac12(q_1^2+q_2^2)-\varepsilon q_1^2\dot q_2\), \(|\varepsilon|\le1\). Its Hessian in \((\ddot q_1,\ddot q_2)\) has rank one. Compute the complete Dirac–Bergmann primary/secondary constraint chain, reduced Hamiltonian and physical degrees of freedom as a function of epsilon.

## Resolution test

No unspecified interaction remains. A correct answer must exhibit all canonical variables and constraints, test their consistency and determine whether linear degeneracy protects against an unbounded energy.
