# GC141: Unique nonzero dispersion minimum

**Mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30)**. Original: [GC141](../problems/05-higher-derivative-fields/GC141.md). Not literature-certified open; no Lean resolution asserted.

## Exact problem

For real \(a,b,c\), define \(\omega^2(k)=a|k|^4+b|k|^2+c\), \(k\in\mathbb R^d\), \(d\ge1\). Classify exactly those triples for which \(\omega^2(k)>0\) for all k and its global minimum occurs at one positive radius \(r_*>0\), with no other minimizing radius. Give \(r_*\) and the sharp lower bound on \(\omega^2\).

## Resolution test

This algebraic classification is fully explicit and may be elementary. Do not call a unique radius a unique wavevector: rotations produce a sphere of minima.
