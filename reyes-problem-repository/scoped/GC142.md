# GC142: Auxiliary-field identity for spatial fourth-order energy

**Mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30)**. Original: [GC142](../problems/05-higher-derivative-fields/GC142.md). Not literature-certified open; no Lean resolution asserted.

## Exact problem

On \(\mathbb T^2\) define \(A=\Delta+1\). For \(u\in H^2(\mathbb T^2;\mathbb C)\), \(v\in L^2\), let \(\mathcal H(u,v)=2\operatorname{Re}\langle v,Au\rangle-\|v\|_2^2\). Prove or refute the identity \(\|Au\|_2^2=\sup_{v\in L^2}\mathcal H(u,v)\), unique maximizer \(v=Au\), and characterize whether the corresponding first-order-in-time constrained auxiliary-field action has the same solution set as W on the torus.

## Resolution test

The exact quadratic identity is standard completion of squares; dynamical equivalence additionally requires variation of the auxiliary variable and correct time-boundary conditions. No ghost-freedom claim follows merely from an algebraic rewrite.

## Review boundary

Check operator/action conventions, domains and quantifiers, possible classical solutions or immediate counterexamples, and closest literature. Preserve the original candidate as a historical sketch.
