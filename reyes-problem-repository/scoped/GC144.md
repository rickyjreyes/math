# GC144: Quartic temporal hyperbolicity region

**Mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30)**. Original: [GC144](../problems/05-higher-derivative-fields/GC144.md). Not literature-certified open; no Lean resolution asserted.

## Exact problem

For \(a\in[-4,4]\), \(\xi\in\mathbb R^d\setminus\{0\}\), define \(P_a(\tau,\xi)=\tau^4+a|\xi|^2\tau^2+|\xi|^4\). Determine precisely for which a the polynomial has four distinct real roots in \(\tau\) for every nonzero \(\xi\); determine how root multiplicities change at the boundary and whether a uniformly diagonalizable first-order reduction exists.

## Resolution test

Explicitly distinguish real characteristic roots from strong hyperbolicity and from positive Hamiltonian energy. A root plot alone is not a uniform symmetrizer proof.

## Review boundary

Check operator/action conventions, domains and quantifiers, possible classical solutions or immediate counterexamples, and closest literature. Preserve the original candidate as a historical sketch.
