# GC145: Controlled cutoff for a truncated dispersion law

**Mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30)**. Original: [GC145](../problems/05-higher-derivative-fields/GC145.md). Not literature-certified open; no Lean resolution asserted.

## Exact problem

Let \(u_\varepsilon\) and \(v\) solve \(u_{tt}+(1-\Delta+\varepsilon\Delta^2)u=0\) and \(v_{tt}+(1-\Delta)v=0\) on \(\mathbb R^2\), with identical real data \((f,g)\in H^2\times H^1\), Fourier-supported in \(|\xi|\le\Lambda(\varepsilon)\) and normalized \(\|(f,g)\|\le1\). For \(T(\varepsilon)=\varepsilon^{-1/4}\), characterize the largest power \(\rho\ge0\) such that \(\sup_{0\le t\le T}\|u_\varepsilon(t)-v(t)\|_{L^2}\to0\) uniformly if \(\Lambda=\varepsilon^{-\rho}\).

## Resolution test

The parent, truncation, frequency window, time horizon and error norm are fixed. Prove matching upper and counterexample bounds by exact Fourier phase comparisons.

Check operator/action conventions, domains and quantifiers, possible classical solutions or immediate counterexamples, and closest literature. Preserve the original candidate as a historical sketch.
