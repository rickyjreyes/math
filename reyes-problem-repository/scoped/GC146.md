# GC146: Positive Hamiltonian for coupled derivative fields

**Mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30)**. Original: [GC146](../problems/05-higher-derivative-fields/GC146.md). Not literature-certified open; no Lean resolution asserted.

## Exact problem

On the one-dimensional torus, let \(q=(q_1,q_2):\mathbb T\to\mathbb R^2\) and take \(L=\tfrac12\int(|q_t|^2-|q_x|^2-|q|^2+2a(q_{1,t}q_{2,x}-q_{2,t}q_{1,x})+2b q_1q_2)dx\), \(a,b\in\mathbb R\). Derive canonical momenta and the exact Hamiltonian. Classify \((a,b)\) for which it is bounded below by \(c\|(q_t,q_x,q)\|_{L^2}^2\) for some \(c>0\).

## Resolution test

Complete the momentum square, account for the spatial boundary terms, and prove necessity and sufficiency. The stability region is an exact coefficient-set problem.

## Review boundary

Check operator/action conventions, domains and quantifiers, possible classical solutions or immediate counterexamples, and closest literature. Preserve the original candidate as a historical sketch.
