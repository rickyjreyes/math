# GC147: Derivative-coupled fourth-order Schrödinger regularity

**Mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30)**. Original: [GC147](../problems/05-higher-derivative-fields/GC147.md). Not literature-certified open; no Lean resolution asserted.

## Exact problem

On \(\mathbb R^2\), study \(iu_t=(\Delta+1)^2u+\partial_{x_1}(u^2)\), \(u(0)=u_0\in H^s\), where \(s\in[0,5]\) and the quadratic product is interpreted distributionally whenever defined. Classify s for which the solution map is locally well-posed in \(C([-T,T];H^s)\) with T depending continuously on \(\|u_0\|_{H^s}\), and exhibit norm-inflation examples below a sharp threshold if they exist.

## Resolution test
