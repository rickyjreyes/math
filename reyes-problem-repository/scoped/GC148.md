# GC148: Gauge-invariant regularized phase curvature

**Mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30)**. Original: [GC148](../problems/05-higher-derivative-fields/GC148.md). Not literature-certified open; no Lean resolution asserted.

## Exact problem

For \(u:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb C\), real \(A:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R^2\), set \(D_Au=(\nabla-iA)u\), \(J_A(u)=\operatorname{Im}(\bar uD_Au)\), and \(K_A(u)=\operatorname{div}(J_A(u)/(1+|u|^2))\). Under \(u\mapsto e^{i\chi}u\), \(A\mapsto A+\nabla\chi\), prove gauge invariance of K_A and classify all real polynomial densities of total field degree at most four formed from \(|u|^2, |D_Au|^2, |K_A|^2, |\operatorname{curl}A|^2\) with at most four spatial derivatives.

## Resolution test

The field content, gauge group and derivative cutoff are explicit. The classification is modulo integration by parts, with a fixed smooth compact-support test class to avoid boundary ambiguities.
