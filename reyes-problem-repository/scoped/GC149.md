# GC149: Heavy-mode elimination and quartic dispersion

**Mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30)**. Original: [GC149](../problems/05-higher-derivative-fields/GC149.md). Not literature-certified open; no Lean resolution asserted.

## Exact problem

For \(M>2\), \(g\in[-1/2,1/2]\), define a Hermitian two-band symbol \(H(k)=\begin{pmatrix}1+|k|^2&g|k|^2\\g|k|^2&M^2+|k|^2\end{pmatrix}\). Let \(\lambda_-(k)\) be its smaller eigenvalue. Compute the Taylor expansion through \(|k|^4\) near \(k=0\), and prove a uniform remainder bound for \(|k|\le M^{1/2}/4\).

## Resolution test

Give the exact light-band quartic coefficient and its sign, including the g=0 case. This is a controlled spectral reduction, not a claim about arbitrary nonlinear parent theories.
