# GC150: Canonical self-adjoint fourth-order boundaries

**Mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30)**. Original: [GC150](../problems/05-higher-derivative-fields/GC150.md). Not literature-certified open; no Lean resolution asserted.

## Exact problem

On the unit disk \(B\subset\mathbb R^2\), let \(A=-\Delta+1\). Compare the nonnegative fourth-order realizations (i) \(L_{cl}=A^2\) generated by \(q_{cl}(u)=\|Au\|_2^2\) on \(H_0^2(B)\), and (ii) \(L_{Nav}=(-\Delta_D+1)^2\) by spectral functional calculus of the Dirichlet Laplacian. Determine their self-adjoint domains, trace boundary conditions, compact resolvent, and whether their eigenvalue sequences interlace.

## Resolution test

Self-adjointness is automatic for the closed-form/functional-calculus constructions once domains are checked; do not infer all self-adjoint fourth-order boundary conditions from these two examples.
