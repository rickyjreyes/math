# GC154: Noether charges for a fourth-order spatial field

**Mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30)**. Original: [GC154](../problems/05-higher-derivative-fields/GC154.md). Not literature-certified open; no Lean resolution asserted.

## Exact problem

Let real \(u\in C^2_tH^4_x(\mathbb T^2)\) solve \(u_{tt}+(\Delta+1)^2u=0\). Derive and prove conservation of \(H=\tfrac12\int(|u_t|^2+|(\Delta+1)u|^2)dx\) and spatial momenta \(P_j=\int u_t\partial_j u\,dx\). Determine whether H controls \(\|u\|_{H^2}\) uniformly after additionally fixing its projection onto the finite shell \(\{k\in\mathbb Z^2:|k|=1\}\).

## Resolution test

The shell contains zero-frequency spatial modes, so energy alone need not coerce H². Quantify any extra conserved quantity used in the estimate.
