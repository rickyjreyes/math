# GC155: Continuation criterion for a quasilinear fourth-order flow

**Mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30)**. Original: [GC155](../problems/05-higher-derivative-fields/GC155.md). Not literature-certified open; no Lean resolution asserted.

## Exact problem

On \(\mathbb R^2\), consider \(iu_t=(\Delta+1)^2u+\partial_{x_1}(|u|^2\partial_{x_1}u)\). For integer \(s\ge6\), let \([0,T_*)\) be the maximal forward interval of an \(H^s\) strong solution. Decide whether \(T_*<\infty\) implies \(\int_0^{T_*}(1+\|u(t)\|_{W^{2,\infty}}^2)dt=\infty\).

## Resolution test

First prove a local strong solution theory in Hs or state it as an explicit antecedent; establish a closed energy estimate or a concrete finite-time counterexample.
