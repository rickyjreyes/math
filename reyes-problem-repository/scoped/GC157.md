# GC157: Two-mode nonlinear resonance normal form

**Mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30)**. Original: [GC157](../problems/05-higher-derivative-fields/GC157.md). Not literature-certified open; no Lean resolution asserted.

## Exact problem

For complex \((a,b)\in\mathbb C^2\), let \(H_\varepsilon(a,b)=|a|^2+(2+\varepsilon\delta)|b|^2+\varepsilon\operatorname{Re}(a^2\bar b)\), \(\delta\in[-1,1]\), \(0<\varepsilon\le1/10\). Define Hamiltonian dynamics \(i\dot a=\partial_{\bar a}H\), \(i\dot b=\partial_{\bar b}H\). For \(\tau=\varepsilon t\) and rotating amplitudes \(A(\tau)=e^{it}a(t)\), \(B(\tau)=e^{2it}b(t)\), derive exact autonomous modulation equations and classify trajectories with nontrivial exchange \(|A|^2\leftrightarrow|B|^2\).

## Resolution test

The resonance, coupling, scaling and finite-dimensional dynamics are exact. State invariant total action and energy, and do not claim the normal form approximates a PDE without a separate error theorem.

## Review boundary

Check operator/action conventions, domains and quantifiers, possible classical solutions or immediate counterexamples, and closest literature. Preserve the original candidate as a historical sketch.
