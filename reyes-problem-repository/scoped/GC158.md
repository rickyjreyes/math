# GC158: Band-limited equivalence under local field redefinition

**Mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30)**. Original: [GC158](../problems/05-higher-derivative-fields/GC158.md). Not literature-certified open; no Lean resolution asserted.

## Exact problem

On \(\mathbb T^2\), let \(P_\Lambda\) project to Fourier \(|k|\le\Lambda\). For \(\varepsilon>0\) with \(\varepsilon\Lambda^2<1/2\), define \(T_\varepsilon=I+\varepsilon\Delta\) on \(P_\Lambda L^2\) and \(H=(\Delta+1)^2\). Prove \(T_\varepsilon\) invertible with sharp condition number, and compare \(H\) with \(T_\varepsilon^{-1}HT_\varepsilon\) and its order-\(\varepsilon\) truncation.

## Resolution test

Because T and H commute in this frozen linear example, the transformed operator is identical: record that exact invariance instead of inventing a nonzero EFT correction. Noncommuting nonlinear redefinitions are a distinct extension.
