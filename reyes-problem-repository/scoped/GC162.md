# GC162: Finite minimax dispersion design

**Concrete target v1, 2026-10-30.** The exact mathematical model, data and decision criterion are below. Original: [GC162](../problems/06-inverse-observation-control/GC162.md). Research/novelty status not yet certified.

## Frozen question

For \(k\in\{0,1,2,3\}\), define \(f_A(k)=1+k^2+k^4\) and \(f_B(k)=1+1.1k^2+0.9k^4\). Select two distinct measurement frequencies \(S\) and assume per-sample deterministic errors in [-δ,δ]. Determine \(D_2=\max_{|S|=2}\min_{k\in S}|f_A(k)-f_B(k)|\) and the largest δ for which both models can always be distinguished by at least one selected measurement.

## Acceptance and falsification

The finite experiment and worst-case noise are fixed. A valid solution enumerates all six designs and proves the separation threshold, even if the optimum is elementary.

## Review

Audit all measurements, source-model correspondences, identifiability symmetries, noise/precision, quantifiers, and previous theorems. Do not claim verification from a theorem about another forward model.
