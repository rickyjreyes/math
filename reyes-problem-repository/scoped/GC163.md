# GC163: Observability from half a boundary edge

**Concrete target v1, 2026-10-30.** The exact mathematical model, data and decision criterion are below. Original: [GC163](../problems/06-inverse-observation-control/GC163.md). Research/novelty status not yet certified.

## Frozen question

On \(\Omega=(0,\pi)^2\) let \(A=-\Delta_D+1\). Solve \(u_{tt}+A^2u=0\) with \((u(0),u_t(0))\in D(A)\times H_0^1\) and observe \(y=\partial_{x_1}u(t,0,x_2)\) on \(\Gamma=\{0\}\times(0,\pi/2)\). Determine whether there exist T,C>0 such that \(\|u(0)\|_{H_0^1}^2+\|u_t(0)\|_{L^2}^2\le C\int_0^T\int_\Gamma|y|^2\) holds for every such solution.

## Acceptance and falsification

The wave generator, measured trace, initial norm and arc are fixed. Prove admissibility of the observation map or construct high-frequency near-invisible data.

## Review

Audit all measurements, source-model correspondences, identifiability symmetries, noise/precision, quantifiers, and previous theorems. Do not claim verification from a theorem about another forward model.
