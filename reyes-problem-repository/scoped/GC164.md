# GC164: Identifiability of cubic versus quintic coefficients

**Concrete target v1, 2026-10-30.** The exact mathematical model, data and decision criterion are below. Original: [GC164](../problems/06-inverse-observation-control/GC164.md). Research/novelty status not yet certified.

## Frozen question

On \(\mathbb T^2\), for \((b,g)\in[1/2,3/2]^2\), solve \(iu_t=(\Delta+1)^2u-b|u|^2u+g|u|^4u\) with \(u(0,x)=a(1+\tfrac12\cos x_1)\), \(0<a<1\). Define observations \(Y_{b,g}(a)=\big(\widehat u(1,0),\widehat u(1,e_1)\big)\). Determine whether the coefficient map \((b,g)\mapsto(Y(a_1),Y(a_2))\in\mathbb C^4\) is locally injective at (1,1) for two explicitly chosen \(0<a_1<a_2<1\).

## Acceptance and falsification

Use distinct small-amplitude powers (cubic order a³ and quintic order a⁵) to seek a full-rank real sensitivity; calculate and control truncation errors.

## Review

Audit all measurements, source-model correspondences, identifiability symmetries, noise/precision, quantifiers, and previous theorems. Do not claim verification from a theorem about another forward model.
