# GC166: Conditioning near rank-deficient spectral parameters

**Concrete target v1, 2026-10-30.** The exact mathematical model, data and decision criterion are below. Original: [GC166](../problems/06-inverse-observation-control/GC166.md). Research/novelty status not yet certified.

## Frozen question

For \(F=\texttt{V18Forward.observed}\), let \(D\) be its C¹ locus and \(Z=\{\theta\in D:\operatorname{rank}DF(\theta)<11\}\). On compact smooth clipping cells \(K\subset D\), determine whether constants \(C_K>0,p_K>0\) exist with \(\sigma_{\min}(DF(\theta))\ge C_K\operatorname{dist}(\theta,Z)^{p_K}\) for \(\theta\in K\setminus Z\), and compute the optimal exponent whenever \(K\cap Z\ne\emptyset\).

## Acceptance and falsification

Avoid undefined distances by treating Z empty separately; use exact F09 model rather than a surrogate. Semialgebraic or analytic properties across exponential clipping must be verified.
