# GC168: Recovery of a two-coefficient potential from boundary spectral data

**Concrete target v1, 2026-10-30.** The exact mathematical model, data and decision criterion are below. Original: [GC168](../problems/06-inverse-observation-control/GC168.md). Research/novelty status not yet certified.

## Frozen question

On \((0,\pi)\) define \(A_{a,b}=-d^2/dx^2+a\cos x+b\cos2x\) with Dirichlet boundary, \((a,b)\in[-1/10,1/10]^2\), and \(H_{a,b}=A_{a,b}^2\). Let \(\lambda_j\) be its first three ordered eigenvalues and \(\psi_j\) normalized Dirichlet eigenfunctions with \(\psi_j'(0)>0\). Determine whether \((a,b)\mapsto(\lambda_1,\lambda_2,\psi_1'(0),\psi_2'(0))\) is locally injective at (0,0).

## Acceptance and falsification

Fix the sign convention for eigenfunctions and avoid confusing spectral data of A with those of A². Prove rank or exhibit indistinguishable potentials.

## Review

Audit all measurements, source-model correspondences, identifiability symmetries, noise/precision, quantifiers, and previous theorems. Do not claim verification from a theorem about another forward model.
