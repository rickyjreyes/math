# GC169: Identifiability of the curvature regularizer

**Concrete target v1, 2026-10-30.** The exact mathematical model, data and decision criterion are below. Original: [GC169](../problems/06-inverse-observation-control/GC169.md). Research/novelty status not yet certified.

## Frozen question

For \(0<\varepsilon\le1\), define \(K_\varepsilon\) as in K and let \(u_1(x)=e^{-|x|^2}(1+x_1)e^{ix_1}\), \(u_2(x)=e^{-|x|^2}(1+x_2)e^{i(x_1+x_2)}\). For \(B=B_1(0)\), define \(F(\varepsilon)=(\|K_\varepsilon(u_1)\|_{L^2(B)}^2,\|K_\varepsilon(u_2)\|_{L^2(B)}^2)\). Determine whether F is injective on (0,1], and give quantitative lower separation on any compact subinterval.

## Acceptance and falsification

All probes and observables are explicit. If the map aliases two epsilon values, exhibit them; if monotone or bi-Lipschitz, prove an actual derivative bound.

## Review

Audit all measurements, source-model correspondences, identifiability symmetries, noise/precision, quantifiers, and previous theorems. Do not claim verification from a theorem about another forward model.
