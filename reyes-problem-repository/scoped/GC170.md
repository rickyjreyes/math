# GC170: Controlled soliton-center displacement

**Concrete target v1, 2026-10-30.** The exact mathematical model, data and decision criterion are below. Original: [GC170](../problems/06-inverse-observation-control/GC170.md). Research/novelty status not yet certified.

## Frozen question

For a real W standing wave \(\phi\in H^4\cap H^2(|x|^2dx)\), consider \(iu_t=(\Delta+1)^2u-|u|^2u+|u|^4u+f(t,x)\) with forcing \(f(t,x)=a(t)\partial_{x_1}\phi(x)\), \(a\in L^2(0,1;\mathbb R)\). Define center \(X(u)=M(u)^{-1}\int x_1|u|^2dx\), whenever finite. Determine whether for every sufficiently small d there is control with \(\|a\|_{L^2}\le C|d|\) and \(|X(u(1))-X(\phi)-d|\le Cd^2\).

## Acceptance and falsification

A solution must exhibit the chosen profile and well-defined moment evolution or state a conditional theorem; the first-order controllability coefficient must be shown nonzero.

## Review

Audit all measurements, source-model correspondences, identifiability symmetries, noise/precision, quantifiers, and previous theorems. Do not claim verification from a theorem about another forward model.
