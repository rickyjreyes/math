# GC171: Minimum-energy stabilization of a stripe eigenmode

**Concrete target v1, 2026-10-30.** The exact mathematical model, data and decision criterion are below. Original: [GC171](../problems/06-inverse-observation-control/GC171.md). Research/novelty status not yet certified.

## Frozen question

Let \(\phi(x_1)\in H^4(\mathbb T^2;\mathbb R)\) be a nonconstant stationary stripe for \(u_t=2u-(1+\Delta)^2u-u^3\). Linearize to \(v_t=Lv+b f(t)\), \(L=2-(1+\Delta)^2-3\phi^2\), with fixed actuator \(b(x)=\cos x_1\). For any isolated real unstable eigenpair \(Lv=\lambda v\), \(\lambda>0\), determine whether \(\langle b,v\rangle\ne0\) and the minimum \(\int_0^T|f(t)|^2dt\) to drive its modal amplitude to zero at T=1.

## Acceptance and falsification

Verify an actual stripe and unstable mode first; the scalar Gramian formula is valid only for a nonzero actuator projection, which must be checked rather than assumed.

## Review

Audit all measurements, source-model correspondences, identifiability symmetries, noise/precision, quantifiers, and previous theorems. Do not claim verification from a theorem about another forward model.
