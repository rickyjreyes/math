# GC172: Modal observer with nonlinear Fourier residual

**Concrete target v1, 2026-10-30.** The exact mathematical model, data and decision criterion are below. Original: [GC172](../problems/06-inverse-observation-control/GC172.md). Research/novelty status not yet certified.

## Frozen question

For the Swift–Hohenberg equation \(u_t=2u-(1+\Delta)^2u-u^3\) on \(\mathbb T^2\), define modes \(K=\{(0,0),(1,0),(0,1)\}\) and amplitudes \(a=P_Ku\). Observe \(y(t)=(u(t,0),u(t,(\pi,0)),u(t,(0,\pi)))\) and define an observer \(\dot{\hat a}=L_K\hat a+\widehat{(-u^3)}|_K+G(y-C\hat a)\), with fixed gain G and C the evaluation matrix on modes. Determine G and a residual bound that yield an exponential estimate for \(a-\hat a\) with an explicit convolution term for omitted modes.

## Acceptance and falsification

The unmeasured nonlinear term in the displayed observer is not implementable without extra observations. Replace it by a closed estimator using hat a and prove an actual error inequality, or record the impossibility.

## Review

Audit all measurements, source-model correspondences, identifiability symmetries, noise/precision, quantifiers, and previous theorems. Do not claim verification from a theorem about another forward model.
