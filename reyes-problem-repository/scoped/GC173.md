# GC173: Distinguishing model mismatch from Gaussian noise

**Concrete target v1, 2026-10-30.** The exact mathematical model, data and decision criterion are below. Original: [GC173](../problems/06-inverse-observation-control/GC173.md). Research/novelty status not yet certified.

## Frozen question

Let \(u_0=e^{-|x|^2}\), and let \(u_b\) solve W with cubic coefficient b instead of 1, \(b\in\{1,1+\delta\}\), \(\delta\in(0,1/10]\). Observe \(Y_j=\operatorname{Re}u_b(1,x_j)+\eta_j\) for fixed \(x_1=0,x_2=(1,0)\), with independent \(\eta_j\sim N(0,\sigma^2)\). Determine the optimal equal-prior hypothesis test and exact misclassification probability as a function of \(\delta,\sigma\) and the two deterministic forward predictions.

## Acceptance and falsification

Gaussian equal-covariance testing is classical; the new PDE task is to prove a nonzero separation between these specified predictions. If predictions coincide, no classifier can beat chance.

## Review

Audit all measurements, source-model correspondences, identifiability symmetries, noise/precision, quantifiers, and previous theorems. Do not claim verification from a theorem about another forward model.
