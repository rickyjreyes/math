# GC174: Adaptive measurements at an algebraic fold

**Concrete target v1, 2026-10-30.** The exact mathematical model, data and decision criterion are below. Original: [GC174](../problems/06-inverse-observation-control/GC174.md). Research/novelty status not yet certified.

## Frozen question

For \(a\in[-1,1]\) define the cubic response curve \(y_a(x)=x^3-a x\), \(x\in[-1,1]\). A fold means some x has \(y_a'(x)=0\). Measurements are \(Y_j=y_a(x_j)+\eta_j\), \(\eta_j\overset{iid}{\sim}N(0,\sigma^2)\), with \(x_j\) adaptively chosen from [-1,1]. Determine the minimax number of samples required to distinguish \(a\le0\) from \(a\ge a_0>0\) with error at most 0.05, and compare adaptive with fixed x choices.

## Acceptance and falsification

The gap a0, error target and admissible design are explicit. The no-gap distinction a≤0 vs a>0 has no finite uniform sample bound; retain that warning.

## Review

Audit all measurements, source-model correspondences, identifiability symmetries, noise/precision, quantifiers, and previous theorems. Do not claim verification from a theorem about another forward model.
