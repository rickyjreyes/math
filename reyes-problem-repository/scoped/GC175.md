# GC175: Inverse two-Gaussian scattering from three phase shifts

**Concrete target v1, 2026-10-30.** The exact mathematical model, data and decision criterion are below. Original: [GC175](../problems/06-inverse-observation-control/GC175.md). Research/novelty status not yet certified.

## Frozen question

For \((a,b)\in[-1/10,1/10]^2\), \(H_{a,b}=(\Delta+1)^2+a e^{-|x|^2}+b e^{-2|x|^2}\), let \(\delta_0(E;a,b)\) be the radially normalized s-wave scattering phase at E=1,2,3, with phase branch continuing from zero coupling. Determine whether the map \((a,b)\mapsto(\delta_0(1),\delta_0(2),\delta_0(3))\) is injective on the specified square, and provide a stable inverse modulus or collision pair.

## Acceptance and falsification

The defect family, finite frequencies and observable are fixed. This is a global 2-parameter inverse question distinct from GC110 local two-channel rank.
