## Frozen question

Let \(F=\texttt{V18Forward.observed}:\mathbb R^{31}\to\mathbb R^{11}\), \(\theta_0=\texttt{V18Forward.frozen 5.1725 5}\), \(K=\overline{B_{10^{-3}}(\theta_0)}\), and \(\delta\ge0\). For data y define \(S(y,\delta)=\{\theta\in K:\|F(\theta)-y\|_\infty\le\delta\}\). Construct sound rational outer enclosures of S, and determine whether \(\operatorname{diam}S(F(\theta_0),0)>0\).
