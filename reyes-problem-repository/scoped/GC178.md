# GC178: Hexagons versus stripes in a cubic energy model

**Candidate mathematical specification v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC178](../problems/07-pattern-formation/GC178.md). Precise mathematical questions are not certified open or solved.

## Explicit model and quantified target

Let \(\Lambda\subset\mathbb R^2\) be generated by wavevectors \(k_1=(1,0)\), \(k_2=(-1/2,\sqrt3/2)\), \(k_3=-k_1-k_2\), and let \(\mathbb T_\Lambda\) be the dual reciprocal torus. For real \(u\in H^2\), take \(E(u)=\int[\tfrac12|(\Delta+1)u|^2-0.05u^2-\tfrac{0.2}{3}u^3+\tfrac14u^4]dx\). Define stripe trials \(u=a\cos(k_1\cdot x)\) and hexagon trials \(u=a\sum_{j=1}^3\cos(k_j\cdot x)\). Determine the exact minimum energy within each trial family and which is lower.

## Proof-or-counterexample test

This comparison is a finite-dimensional weakly nonlinear benchmark. Showing one trial lower does not prove it minimizes among all PDE solutions; that stronger question needs a separate coercivity argument.
