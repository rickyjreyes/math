# GC179: Phase pinning under a periodic substrate

**Candidate mathematical specification v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC179](../problems/07-pattern-formation/GC179.md). Precise mathematical questions are not certified open or solved.

## Explicit model and quantified target

On \(\mathbb T^2\), let \(E_\varepsilon(u)=\int[\tfrac12|(\Delta+1)u|^2-\tfrac\mu2u^2+\tfrac14u^4-\varepsilon u\cos x_1]dx\), \(\mu=2\). For any nonconstant stationary stripe \(\phi(x_1)\) of the unforced flow, define \(P_\varepsilon(\vartheta)=E_\varepsilon(\phi(\cdot-\vartheta))\), \(\vartheta\in\mathbb T\). Classify minimizers of P and whether the full PDE has nearby pinned equilibria near each nondegenerate minimizer for sufficiently small epsilon.

## Proof-or-counterexample test

The first-order pinning potential is an explicit Fourier correlation. Verify nonzero relevant Fourier coefficient; pinning may vanish when forcing is orthogonal to the stripe.
