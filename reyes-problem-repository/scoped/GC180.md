# GC180: Motion of a phase dislocation in complex Ginzburg–Landau flow

**Candidate mathematical specification v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC180](../problems/07-pattern-formation/GC180.md). Precise mathematical questions are not certified open or solved.

## Explicit model and quantified target

On \(\mathbb R^2\), study \(A_t=\Delta A+A-|A|^2A-\varepsilon V(x)A\), with \(V(x)=e^{-|x|^2}\), \(0<\varepsilon<1/10\). Consider degree-one vortex initial states \(A(0,x)=f(|x-a_0|)e^{i\arg(x-a_0)}\), where f is the unique radial stationary-vortex profile if it exists, tending to 1 at infinity. Define vortex center as the isolated zero of A. Determine the leading center-velocity law on time intervals \([0,c/\varepsilon]\) and an error bound.

## Proof-or-counterexample test

The nonzero background requires a uniformly-local or renormalized energy space rather than whole-space L². Prove existence/uniqueness of the tracked simple zero and address vortex translation modes.
