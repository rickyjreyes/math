# GC181: Wavenumber drift under a weak periodic coefficient

**Candidate mathematical specification v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC181](../problems/07-pattern-formation/GC181.md). Precise mathematical questions are not certified open or solved.

## Explicit model and quantified target

For \(L=8\pi N\), N≥1, on \(\mathbb T_L^1\) consider \(u_t=2u-(1+\partial_x^2)^2u-u^3-\varepsilon\cos(2x)u\). For each linearly stable stationary \(2\pi/k\)-periodic branch with admissible integer Fourier k=2πn/L, define its continuation as epsilon varies near zero. Determine whether its dominant Fourier wavenumber changes continuously, jumps, or remains fixed for each finite L; then classify limits as \(N\to\infty\).

## Proof-or-counterexample test

On a fixed box Fourier labels are discrete, so a continuous 'selected wavenumber' requires a spectral-centroid or large-box definition. Branch continuation and thermodynamic limits are separate.

Confirm nonempty model class, trace and limiting conventions, dimensional consistency, closest existing theorems and the exact counterexample alternative. Preserve historical draft and defer novelty certification.
