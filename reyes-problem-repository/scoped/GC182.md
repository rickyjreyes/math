# GC182: revised mathematically explicit candidate

**Definition revision v1.1 (2026-10-30).** Earlier scoping draft corrected for internal domain or topology inconsistency. Original candidate: [GC182](../problems/07-pattern-formation/GC182.md).

## Exact proposed target

For each N≥4 let \(L_N=2\pi N\) and \(\mathbb T_{L_N}^2\) be the square torus. Independently sample circular complex Gaussian coefficients \(g_k\) indexed by integer \(k\in\mathbb Z^2\) with \(0<|k|\le3N\) and variance \((1+|k|^2/N^2)^{-3}\). Set \(A_N(0,x)=N^{-1}\sum_{0<|k|\le3N}g_ke^{ik\cdot x/N}\), and solve \(A_t=\Delta A+A-|A|^2A\). Let \(D_N(t)\) be the number of spatial zeros with nonzero real Jacobian divided by \(L_N^2\), with D_N=0 if the zeros are not isolated or not finite. Decide whether there exist \(\alpha>0,c\in(0,\infty)\) such that \(\lim_{t\to\infty}\lim_{N\to\infty}t^\alpha\mathbb E D_N(t)=c\).

## Acceptance and outstanding verification

The lattice frequencies are now k/N, correctly matched to the torus, with a domain-size-dependent ensemble and a specified amplitude normalization. Check that the random-field continuum limit and the zero-count expectations exist before making a power-law claim.
