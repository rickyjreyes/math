# GC183: Finite-box versus whole-space spectral selection

**Candidate mathematical specification v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC183](../problems/07-pattern-formation/GC183.md). Precise mathematical questions are not certified open or solved.

## Explicit model and quantified target

Consider Swift–Hohenberg flow \(u_t=2u-(1+\Delta)^2u-u^3\) on tori \(\mathbb T_L^2\), \(L=2\pi N\). For nonzero u define \(k_{rms}(u)^2=\sum_k|k|^2|\widehat u(k)|^2/\sum_k|\widehat u(k)|^2\). With initial data \(u_{L,0}=0.01(\cos x_1+\cos x_2)\), determine whether the two iterated limits of \(k_{rms}(u_L(t))\) as \(t\to\infty\) and \(L\to\infty\) exist and agree.

## Proof-or-counterexample test

The parameter, input, observable and order of limits are fixed; any whole-plane comparison requires consistently identifying the periodic initial data with bounded, not L², whole-plane data.
