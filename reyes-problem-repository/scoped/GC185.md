# GC185: Front between opposite stationary stripe phases

**Candidate mathematical specification v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC185](../problems/07-pattern-formation/GC185.md). Precise mathematical questions are not certified open or solved.

## Explicit model and quantified target

On the cylinder \(\mathbb R_{x_1}\times\mathbb T_{x_2}\), let \(u_t=2u-(1+\Delta)^2u-u^3\), and suppose a nonconstant periodic \(\phi(x_2)\) solves \(2\phi-(1+\partial_{x_2}^2)^2\phi-\phi^3=0\). Determine whether there are \(c\in\mathbb R\) and bounded \(U(z,x_2)\) with \(-c\partial_z U=2U-(1+\partial_z^2+\partial_{x_2}^2)^2U-U^3\), and \(U(z,\cdot)\to\pm\phi\) in \(H^2(\mathbb T)\) as \(z\to\pm\infty\).

## Proof-or-counterexample test

Existence of the periodic endpoint and its nondegenerate spectrum must be checked; a gradient-energy identity may force c=0 for equal-energy endpoints.
