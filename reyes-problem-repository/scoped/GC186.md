# GC186: Phase-diffusion coefficient of a stripe branch

**Candidate mathematical specification v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC186](../problems/07-pattern-formation/GC186.md). Precise mathematical questions are not certified open or solved.

## Explicit model and quantified target

For \(0<\mu\le1/10\), consider \(u_t=\mu u-(1+\Delta)^2u-u^3\) on \(\mathbb R^2\), and any real period-2π stationary stripe \(\phi_\mu(x_1)\) with \(\phi_\mu\to0\) as μ→0. Let \(\lambda_\mu(\eta)\) be the Bloch eigenvalue of the linearization passing through zero at \(\eta=0\) due to translation. Determine the coefficient \(D(\mu)=-\tfrac12\lambda_\mu''(0)\), its leading asymptotic and sign for small μ.

## Proof-or-counterexample test

The eigenbranch must be simple after factoring the neutral phase and the Bloch direction specified (longitudinal η e1). Different transverse sidebands require a distinct coefficient.
