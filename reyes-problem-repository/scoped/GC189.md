**Definition revision v1.1 (2026-10-30).** Earlier scoping draft corrected for internal domain or topology inconsistency. Original candidate: [GC189](../problems/07-pattern-formation/GC189.md).

## Exact proposed target

On \(\Omega=\mathbb R\times(\mathbb R/2\pi\mathbb Z)\), take the smooth coefficient \(k(x_1)=\tfrac32+\tfrac12\tanh x_1\), and define \(L_k u=\Delta^2u+2k(x_1)^2\Delta u+k(x_1)^4u\) on smooth bounded states, with no claim this is \((\Delta+k^2)^2\) when k varies. Let \(E(u)=\int_\Omega[\tfrac12|\Delta u+k(x_1)^2u|^2-\tfrac\mu2u^2+\tfrac14u^4]dx\), \(\mu=2\). Fix real periodic minimizing bulk profiles \(p_\pm(x_2)\) for the constant asymptotic coefficients k=1 and k=2, if they exist, and fields u approaching p_± in H² on unit-width strips as x1→±∞. Define excess energy by subtracting the two bulk energy densities on rectangles [-R,0]×T and [0,R]×T before sending R→∞. Determine whether a stationary critical point of the **exact variational energy** has finite excess and minimizes it.

## Acceptance and outstanding verification

The smooth coefficient removes the artificial derivative jump. The fourth-order Euler–Lagrange operator must be derived from the displayed squared energy, including k' terms; it is not the naive L_k expression. Verify existence and energy normalization of both bulk profiles before asserting a grain boundary.
