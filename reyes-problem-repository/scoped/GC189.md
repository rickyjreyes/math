# GC189: Renormalized interface between different preferred stripe periods

**Scoped formulation v1.2, 2026-10-30.** [Original GC189](../problems/07-pattern-formation/GC189.md). This remains a candidate pending a full analytic review, not a verified existence theorem.

## Equation and function spaces

Let \(\Omega=\mathbb R\times\mathbb T\), \(\mathbb T=\mathbb R/(2\pi\mathbb Z)\), \(k(x_1)=\frac32+\frac12\tanh(x_1)\), and \(\mu=2\). For real \(u\in H^4_{loc}(\Omega)\), define \(A_k u=(\Delta+k(x_1)^2)u\), and energy density
\[
e_k(u)=\frac12|A_ku|^2-\frac\mu2|u|^2+\frac14|u|^4.
\]
For each \(j\in\{-,+\}\), put \(k_-=1,k_+=2\) and consider the exact bulk energy per longitudinal unit length
\[
B_j(p)=\int_{\mathbb T}\left[\frac12|p''+k_j^2p|^2-\frac\mu2p^2+\frac14p^4\right]dx_2
\]
on real \(H^2(\mathbb T)\). Set \(b_j=\inf B_j\) and let \(\mathcal M_j=\{p:B_j(p)=b_j\}\). Do **not** presume the minimizers are nonconstant.

Select any \(p_j\in\mathcal M_j\). A candidate heteroclinic is a bounded \(u\in H^4_{loc}(\Omega)\) with \(u(x_1,\cdot)\to p_\pm\) in \(H^2(\mathbb T)\) as \(x_1\to\pm\infty\) and a finite renormalized energy
\[
\mathcal E(u)=\lim_{R\to\infty}\left\{\int_{-R}^R\int_{\mathbb T} e_k(u)\,dx_2dx_1-R(b_-+b_+)\right\}\in\mathbb R.
\]
The **limit is part of admissibility**: ordinary energy on the infinite cylinder generally diverges. Determine whether an admissible u exists and whether \(\inf\mathcal E\) is attained in this class. For a minimizer derive, in distributions, the genuine Euler–Lagrange equation
\[
(\Delta+k^2)(\Delta+k^2)u-\mu u+u^3=0.
\]

## Falsification and analytical gates

Prove existence/nontriviality of bulk minimizing profiles, finite-excess competitors and the limit; then establish compactness/attainment, or show a precise obstruction. The composition above includes \(\nabla k\) and \(\Delta k\) contributions, so it is **not** the naive \(\Delta^2+2k^2\Delta+k^4\). Boundary phase registration and possible interface translations require explicit treatment. No existence theorem is claimed here.
