# GC191: Sharp algebraic relaxation at a degenerate equilibrium

**Candidate mathematical specification v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC191](../problems/07-pattern-formation/GC191.md). Precise mathematical questions are not certified open or solved.

## Explicit model and quantified target

On \(\mathbb T^2\) solve the **critical-parameter** Swift–Hohenberg flow \(u_t=-(1+\Delta)^2u-u^3\) with real \(u_0\in H^2\) sufficiently small. The linearization at zero is \(-(1+\Delta)^2\), with neutral Fourier modes \(|k|=1\) and strictly negative spectrum on their complement. Determine whether all such small solutions satisfy \(\|u(t)\|_{L^2}\le C(u_0)(1+t)^{-1/2}\), and whether there is nonzero \(u_0\) with \(\liminf_{t\to\infty}t^{1/2}\|u(t)\|_{L^2}>0\).

## Proof-or-counterexample test

The critical parameter is \(\mu=0\), not \(\mu=1\) (which has unstable \(|k|=1\) modes). Prove a nonlinear center-manifold or normal form with **cubic** leading damping and controlled interactions and remainders.

Confirm nonempty model class, trace and limiting conventions, dimensional consistency, closest existing theorems and the exact counterexample alternative. Preserve historical draft and defer novelty certification.
