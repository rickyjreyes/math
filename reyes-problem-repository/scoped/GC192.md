## Candidate mathematical question

For a smooth complex amplitude description of stripes, derive conservation laws for signed phase-dislocation charge while defects remain isolated.

**Quantifiers and domain discipline:** Choose a stationary stripe/hexagon/front, perturbation topology, box or whole-plane domain and observable (spectral sign, wavenumber, defect count or speed). State uniformity in r,L.

**Observable to define explicitly:** Bloch eigenvalue, wavelength selection, front speed or defect rate. Provide the exact norm, parameter ranges, and dependence of any claimed constant, bound, rate or threshold on all inputs.

## First decisive result

Prove charge balance through nucleation and annihilation events.

**Valid negative outcomes:** An unstable sideband, competing pattern or lack of convergence as L→∞.

State at least one precisely checkable theorem or counterexample on the benchmark. If the desired reference state, operator or measurement does not exist, that nonvacuity obstruction must be resolved **before** treating any conditional conclusion as substantive.
