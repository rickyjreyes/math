# GC192: Conservation of isolated phase-dislocation indices

**Candidate mathematical specification v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC192](../problems/07-pattern-formation/GC192.md). Precise mathematical questions are not certified open or solved.

## Explicit model and quantified target

Let \(A\in C^1([0,T]\times\mathbb T^2;\mathbb C)\) have finitely many simple spatial zeros for each t except finitely many isolated critical times. Define \(q_p(t)=\operatorname{sgn}\det D_x(\operatorname{Re}A,\operatorname{Im}A)(t,p)\in\{\pm1\}\) at each simple zero. Prove the total signed index \(Q(t)=\sum_p q_p(t)\) is constant across regular times and determine allowed charge balance at zero creation/annihilation events.

## Proof-or-counterexample test

The torus has no boundary flux, and signed degree conservation is classical under the stated continuity. A source/sink must coincide with singular zeros or failure of regularity.
