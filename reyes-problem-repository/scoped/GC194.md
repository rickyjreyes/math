# GC194: Coupled Swift–Hohenberg phase locking

**Candidate mathematical specification v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC194](../problems/07-pattern-formation/GC194.md). Precise mathematical questions are not certified open or solved.

## Explicit model and quantified target

On \(\mathbb T^2\), let \(u_t=2u-(1+\Delta)^2u-u^3+\kappa(v-u)\), \(v_t=2v-(1+\Delta)^2v-v^3+\kappa(u-v)\), \(\kappa\ge0\). For each real stationary stripe φ of the single equation, \((u,v)=(\phi,\phi)\) is synchronized. Determine the sharp κ region for linear asymptotic stability **transverse to the diagonal** in H², in terms of the spectral bound of \(2-(1+\Delta)^2-3\phi^2\).

## Proof-or-counterexample test

Separate synchronization stability from stability along the stripe branch. The transverse generator is exactly Lφ−2κ; neutral phase/translation modes are not unstable transverse ones once κ>0.
