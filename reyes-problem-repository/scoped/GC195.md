# GC195: Renormalized grain-boundary stripe orientation

**Candidate mathematical specification v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC195](../problems/07-pattern-formation/GC195.md). Precise mathematical questions are not certified open or solved.

## Explicit model and quantified target

For \(u_t=2u-(1+\Delta)^2u-u^3\) on \(\mathbb R^2\), let \(p_1(x)=\phi(x_1)\) and \(p_2(x)=\phi(x_2)\) for a 2π-periodic real stationary stripe φ. Consider fields u whose difference from p1 as x1→−∞ and p2 as x1→+∞ tends to zero in \(H^2\) on bounded-width transverse strips. Define grain-boundary excess energy by subtracting the corresponding bulk energy from rectangles [-R,R]×[-L,L], taking first R→∞ then dividing by 2L and L→∞. Determine if a stationary u has finite excess and attains the infimum among such interfaces.

## Proof-or-counterexample test

The double limit and asymptotic patterns are fixed, but phase registration and transverse strip-topology require rigorous choices; prove that renormalized energy exists before asserting its minimizer.
