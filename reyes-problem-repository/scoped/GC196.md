# GC196: Hopf-sector lower bounds for the Faddeev–Skyrme energy

**Precise candidate target v1, 2026-10-30.** Historical prompt: [GC196](../problems/08-topology-multicomponent/GC196.md). No claim of novel open status or Lean certification.

## Model and exact mathematical question

On the unit round \(S^3\), for smooth \(n:S^3\to S^2\), set \(E(n)=\int_{S^3}(|dn|^2+|n^*\omega_{S^2}|^2)dV\), where \(\omega_{S^2}\) is the unit-area-normalized area form. Define \(Q(n)\in\mathbb Z\) as the Hopf invariant and \(I_Q=\inf\{E(n):Q(n)=Q\}\). Determine the sharp asymptotic growth and best constants in bounds of the form \(c|Q|^{3/4}\le I_Q\le C(1+|Q|)^{3/4}\) for \(|Q|\to\infty\).

## Resolution and safeguards

Fix the normalization of omega and the domain metric; compare the classical Hopf-energy bounds before claiming a new inequality. A matching construction or disproof of a proposed exponent is decisive.

## Review checklist

Verify target homotopy groups, regularity/topology, energy finiteness, domain and boundary assumptions, ordinary counterexamples and prior work. A topological invariant is not automatically a dynamical stability theorem.
