# GC196: Correct large-Hopf-charge growth for compact Faddeev–Skyrme energy

**Mathematical correction v2 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC196](../problems/08-topology-multicomponent/GC196.md). This version removes an impossible imported \(|Q|^{3/4}\) **upper bound** for the fixed compact \(S^3\) problem. This is not a claimed new theorem or complete novelty clearance.

## Frozen model

Let \(S^3\) have its standard unit round metric, and \(S^2\) its unit round metric. Let \(\omega\in\Omega^2(S^2)\) be the smooth positively oriented area form **normalized by** \(\int_{S^2}\omega=1\). For every smooth \(n:S^3\to S^2\), define
\[
F_n=n^*\omega,\qquad
E(n)=\int_{S^3}\bigl(|dn|^2+|F_n|^2\bigr)\,dV.
\]
Since \(H^2(S^3)=0\), choose a one-form \(A_n\) with \(dA_n=F_n\). Define the Hopf integer \(Q(n)=\int_{S^3}A_n\wedge F_n\in\mathbb Z\), independent of the choice of primitive. For every integer Q let
\[
I_Q=\inf\{E(n):n\in C^\infty(S^3,S^2),\ Q(n)=Q\}.
\]
Every Q is represented by some smooth map because \(\pi_3(S^2)\cong\mathbb Z\).

## Verified obstruction to the earlier target

Take the unique **coexact** Hodge primitive of F, with \(dA_n=F_n\) and \(\delta A_n=0\). On the compact sphere, the positive spectral gap of the Hodge Laplacian on coexact one-forms gives
\[
\|A_n\|_{L^2}\le C_{S^3}\|F_n\|_{L^2}.
\]
Cauchy–Schwarz then yields
\[
|Q(n)|=\bigl|\int A_n\wedge F_n\bigr|
\le C_{S^3}\|F_n\|_2^2\le C_{S^3}E(n).
\]
Consequently \(I_Q\ge c_{S^3}|Q|\) for all Q. **No bound** \(I_Q\le C(1+|Q|)^{3/4}\) with a Q-independent constant can hold as \(|Q|\to\infty\) for this compact-domain model.

## Corrected mathematical target

Determine the sharp growth of \(I_Q\) as \(|Q|\to\infty\), specifically whether there exists a constant C with \(I_Q\le C(1+|Q|)\) for all Q, or whether \(I_Q/|Q|\) is unbounded. Independently determine attainment and symmetry types for each Q. Any upper construction must use smooth maps on the **fixed unit compact** \(S^3\); Euclidean \(\mathbb R^3\) scaling arguments cannot be imported unchanged.
