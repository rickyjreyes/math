# GC197: Knot-type obstruction under a regular-value homotopy

**Precise candidate target v1, 2026-10-30.** Historical prompt: [GC197](../problems/08-topology-multicomponent/GC197.md). No claim of novel open status or Lean certification.

## Model and exact mathematical question

Let \(n_0,n_1\in C^\infty(S^3,S^2)\) have equal Hopf invariant. Fix \(y_0\in S^2\) regular for both, with links \(K_i=n_i^{-1}(y_0)\subset S^3\). Determine whether there is a smooth homotopy \(n_s\) from n0 to n1 such that **y0 is a regular value of every n_s**. Classify the obstruction in terms of ambient isotopy of K0 and K1.

## Resolution and safeguards

Without the regular-value requirement, equal Hopf degree already implies homotopy and the question is classical. Under the restriction, preimage links form a smooth isotopy, so distinct isotopy types give an immediate obstruction.

## Review checklist

Verify target homotopy groups, regularity/topology, energy finiteness, domain and boundary assumptions, ordinary counterexamples and prior work. A topological invariant is not automatically a dynamical stability theorem.
