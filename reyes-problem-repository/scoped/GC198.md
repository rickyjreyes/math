# GC198: Orbital stability of a linked Hopf-energy minimizer

**Precise candidate target v1, 2026-10-30.** Historical prompt: [GC198](../problems/08-topology-multicomponent/GC198.md). No claim of novel open status or Lean certification.

## Model and exact mathematical question

On \(\mathbb R^3\) let \(n:\mathbb R^3\to S^2\) tend to the north pole at infinity with finite Faddeev energy \(E(n)=\int(|\nabla n|^2+\sum_{i<j}|\partial_i n\times\partial_j n|^2)dx\), and let Q(n)=2. Define \(I_2=\inf_{Q(n)=2}E(n)\). Determine whether I2 is attained and whether any smooth minimizer is orbitally stable modulo spatial translations and target rotations under the Landau–Lifshitz flow \(n_t=n\times\delta E/\delta n\) in a declared strong energy topology.

## Resolution and safeguards

Existence and well-posedness of the Hamiltonian flow are separate requirements. Linked preimages alone do not certify dynamical orbital stability.

## Review checklist

Verify target homotopy groups, regularity/topology, energy finiteness, domain and boundary assumptions, ordinary counterexamples and prior work. A topological invariant is not automatically a dynamical stability theorem.
