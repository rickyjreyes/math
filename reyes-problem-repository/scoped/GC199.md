# GC199: Nodal-line reconnection in a smooth parabolic complex field

**Precise candidate target v1, 2026-10-30.** Historical prompt: [GC199](../problems/08-topology-multicomponent/GC199.md). No claim of novel open status or Lean certification.

## Model and exact mathematical question

Let \(u_t=\Delta u+u-|u|^2u\) on \(\mathbb R^3\) with \(u_0-1\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R^3;\mathbb C)\). For t>0 let \(Z_t=\{x:u(t,x)=0\}\). Determine whether there exist data and a finite \(t_*\) such that for t on opposite sides of t*, the compact one-dimensional regular parts of \(Z_t\) have different link type, while \(u\in C^\infty\) near \((t_*,Z_{t_*})\).

## Resolution and safeguards

Reconnection requires a failure of spatial transversality at the event, not a blow-up of the field. Demonstrate an exact evolving zero set or prove a topological obstruction for this PDE.

## Review checklist

Verify target homotopy groups, regularity/topology, energy finiteness, domain and boundary assumptions, ordinary counterexamples and prior work. A topological invariant is not automatically a dynamical stability theorem.
