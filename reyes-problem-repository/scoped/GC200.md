# GC200: Exact phase-gradient minimum in a winding class

**Precise candidate target v1, 2026-10-30.** Historical prompt: [GC200](../problems/08-topology-multicomponent/GC200.md). No claim of novel open status or Lean certification.

## Model and exact mathematical question

For \(L_x,L_y>0\), \(T=\mathbb R/L_x\mathbb Z\times\mathbb R/L_y\mathbb Z\), and \(u\in H^1(T;S^1)\) with winding pair \((m,n)\in\mathbb Z^2\), minimize \(E(u)=\tfrac12\int_T|\nabla u|^2dx\). Prove or refute \(\min E=2\pi^2(m^2L_y/L_x+n^2L_x/L_y)\) and uniqueness of minimizers modulo constant phase for representatives \(u=e^{2\pi i(mx/L_x+ny/L_y)}\).

## Resolution and safeguards

This is an elementary harmonic-representative result, not a globally open conjecture. Any additional PDE orbital stability question must specify a flow separately.

## Review checklist

Verify target homotopy groups, regularity/topology, energy finiteness, domain and boundary assumptions, ordinary counterexamples and prior work. A topological invariant is not automatically a dynamical stability theorem.
