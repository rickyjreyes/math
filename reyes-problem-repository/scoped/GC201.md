# GC201: Hopf minimizers with a nonvacuous circle-equivariance restriction

**Mathematical correction v2 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC201](../problems/08-topology-multicomponent/GC201.md). Uses GC196's [corrected compact-domain energy](GC196.md).

## Why the original SO(3) formulation was impossible

Identify \(S^3\) with unit quaternions \(q\in\mathbb H\), and \(S^2\) with unit imaginary quaternions. The action of \(SO(3)\) on \(S^3\) by conjugation **fixes** \(q=1\) (and \(q=-1\)), while the standard rotation action of the full \(SO(3)\) on \(S^2\) has **no globally fixed point**. Any equivariant map n would require \(n(1)=g\cdot n(1)\) for all g, a contradiction. Hence the previously proposed \(SO(3)\)-equivariant admissible set was empty for **every** Hopf class; its 'symmetry-breaking gap' was automatically infinite and scientifically uninformative.

## Corrected admissible group and model

Let \(H=\{g_\theta=\cos\theta+i\sin\theta:\theta\in\mathbb R\}\cong S^1\subset S^3\). Define the conjugation action of H on \(S^3\) by \(q\mapsto gqg^{-1}\) and on \(S^2\) (imaginary unit quaternions) by \(v\mapsto gvg^{-1}\).

For every integer Q define
\[
\mathcal A_Q^H=\{n\in C^\infty(S^3,S^2):
Q(n)=Q,\quad n(gqg^{-1})=gn(q)g^{-1}\quad
\forall g\in H,\ q\in S^3\},
\]
and \(I_Q^H=\inf\{E(n):n\in\mathcal A_Q^H\}\), with \(\inf\varnothing=+\infty\). Let \(I_Q\) be the unrestricted infimum of GC196.

The admissible class is **nonempty at Q=1**: the Hopf map \(h(q)=qiq^{-1}\) has Hopf invariant 1 (with a compatible orientation) and obeys
\(h(gqg^{-1})=g h(q)g^{-1}\), because each g in H commutes with i.

## Corrected research target

For each \(|Q|\ge2\), determine whether \(\mathcal A_Q^H\) is nonempty, whether either infimum is attained, and whether \(I_Q<I_Q^H\), \(I_Q=I_Q^H\), or \(I_Q^H=+\infty\). Identify the first Q for which an established strict **finite** gap occurs, if any. Interpret strict inequality as symmetry selection only after both restricted and unrestricted configurations exist at that Q.

## Review boundary

Do not confuse the H circle action above with the impossible full SO(3) action. Distinguish classical Hopf-map minimality at Q=1 under specific coefficients from the proposed higher-charge constrained comparison. No Lean formalization or novelty claim is supplied by this scope file.
