# GC201: Equivariant versus unrestricted Hopf minimizers

**Precise candidate target v1, 2026-10-30.** Historical prompt: [GC201](../problems/08-topology-multicomponent/GC201.md). No claim of novel open status or Lean certification.

## Model and exact mathematical question

Let E and I_Q be as in GC196. Identify \(S^3\) with unit quaternions and let SO(3) act by conjugation on S³ and by rotations on S². Define \(I_Q^{eq}=\inf\{E(n):Q(n)=Q,\ n(gxg^{-1})=g\cdot n(x)\ \forall g\in SO(3)\}\) with inf empty=∞. Determine for which Q≥1 \(I_Q<I_Q^{eq}\), and whether the least such Q exists.

## Resolution and safeguards

The symmetry group and its action are exact. Check that equivariant maps exist in the candidate Hopf class before attributing a strict gap to spontaneous symmetry breaking.

## Review checklist

Verify target homotopy groups, regularity/topology, energy finiteness, domain and boundary assumptions, ordinary counterexamples and prior work. A topological invariant is not automatically a dynamical stability theorem.
