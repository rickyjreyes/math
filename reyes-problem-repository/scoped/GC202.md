# GC202: Polarization precession in a coupled optical spinor

**Precise candidate target v1, 2026-10-30.** Historical prompt: [GC202](../problems/08-topology-multicomponent/GC202.md). No claim of novel open status or Lean certification.

## Model and exact mathematical question

On \(\mathbb T^2\), let \(\Psi=(u,v)\in\mathbb C^2\) obey \(i\Psi_t=(\Delta+1)^2\Psi-|\Psi|^2\Psi+|\Psi|^4\Psi+\kappa\sigma_x\Psi\), \(\kappa\in\mathbb R\). For constant-in-space nonzero initial \(\Psi_0\), define normalized Stokes vector \(S_j=\Psi^\dagger\sigma_j\Psi/|\Psi|^2\). Derive the exact ODE for \(S(t)\) and determine its period and invariants.

## Resolution and safeguards

This is a precisely solvable uniform-mode polarization benchmark. A modulated localized bound state would require a separate spectral/modulation derivation.

## Review checklist

Verify target homotopy groups, regularity/topology, energy finiteness, domain and boundary assumptions, ordinary counterexamples and prior work. A topological invariant is not automatically a dynamical stability theorem.
