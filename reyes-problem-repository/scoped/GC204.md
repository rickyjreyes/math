# GC204: Minimal reconnection barrier for two prescribed line defects

**Precise candidate target v1, 2026-10-30.** Historical prompt: [GC204](../problems/08-topology-multicomponent/GC204.md). No claim of novel open status or Lean certification.

## Model and exact mathematical question

On the unit ball \(B^3\), fix \(\delta\in(0,1)\), \(E_\delta(u)=\int_{B^3}(|\nabla u|^2+\delta^{-2}(1-|u|^2)^2)dx\). For any smooth \(u_0,u_1:B^3\to\mathbb C\) with common boundary trace u=1, and whose regular zero sets have distinct link isotopy classes, define \(\mathcal B_\delta(u_0,u_1)=\inf_{\gamma\in C([0,1],H^1_1(B^3)),\gamma(0)=u_0,\gamma(1)=u_1}\max_s E_\delta(\gamma(s))-\max(E_\delta(u_0),E_\delta(u_1))\). Determine whether any such endpoints have \(\mathcal B_\delta>0\).

## Resolution and safeguards

The energy and path topology are fixed. Construct valid endpoints before asserting a universal barrier; zero crossing is allowed because the complex amplitude is not constrained to S¹.

## Review checklist

Verify target homotopy groups, regularity/topology, energy finiteness, domain and boundary assumptions, ordinary counterexamples and prior work. A topological invariant is not automatically a dynamical stability theorem.
