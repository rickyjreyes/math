# GC205: SSH bulk–boundary zero mode and chiral defects

**Precise candidate target v1, 2026-10-30.** Historical prompt: [GC205](../problems/08-topology-multicomponent/GC205.md). No claim of novel open status or Lean certification.

## Model and exact mathematical question

For \(0<t_1,t_2\le2\), define the chiral Bloch matrix \(H(k)=\begin{psmallmatrix}0&t_1+t_2e^{-ik}\\t_1+t_2e^{ik}&0\end{psmallmatrix}\), k∈[0,2π), and its half-line nearest-neighbor realization \(\mathcal H_+\) on \(\ell^2(\mathbb N;\mathbb C^2)\) with open left boundary. Prove the number and exponential localization of zero-energy edge modes for t1<t2 versus t1>t2, and classify their persistence under bounded finite-support perturbations preserving chiral symmetry and the bulk gap.

## Resolution and safeguards

This is the classical SSH representative of bulk–boundary correspondence and may be known; the exact boundary and allowed defect class must be used, with no claim of a new universal topological theorem.

## Review checklist

Verify target homotopy groups, regularity/topology, energy finiteness, domain and boundary assumptions, ordinary counterexamples and prior work. A topological invariant is not automatically a dynamical stability theorem.
