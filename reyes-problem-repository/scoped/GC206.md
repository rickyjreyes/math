# GC206: Concentration-compactness for the Hopf charge-one energy

**Precise candidate target v1, 2026-10-30.** Historical prompt: [GC206](../problems/08-topology-multicomponent/GC206.md). No claim of novel open status or Lean certification.

## Model and exact mathematical question

On \(\mathbb R^3\) for finite-energy \(n:\mathbb R^3\to S^2\) with n→north pole at infinity, let \(I_1\) be the infimum of Faddeev energy in Hopf class Q=1. Decide whether every minimizing sequence \(n_j\) has translations \(x_j\) such that \(n_j(\cdot+x_j)\) converges strongly in \(\dot H^1\) and with the Skyrme energy component, to a charge-one minimizer.

## Resolution and safeguards

The exact topology and two energy components are specified. Exhibit energy splitting or loss of topological charge to refute compactness; a numerical knot image does not settle it.

## Review checklist

Verify target homotopy groups, regularity/topology, energy finiteness, domain and boundary assumptions, ordinary counterexamples and prior work. A topological invariant is not automatically a dynamical stability theorem.
