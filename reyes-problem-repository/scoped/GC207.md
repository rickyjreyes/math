# GC207: Internal modes of a frozen Hopf soliton

**Precise candidate target v1, 2026-10-30.** Historical prompt: [GC207](../problems/08-topology-multicomponent/GC207.md). No claim of novel open status or Lean certification.

## Model and exact mathematical question

Assume a smooth finite-energy \(Q=1\) Faddeev minimizer \(n_*:\mathbb R^3\to S^2\) exists and is nondegenerate modulo translations and target rotations. Define the constrained Hessian \(L_*\) as the second variation of the Faddeev energy on compactly supported tangent sections \(v(x)\perp n_*(x)\), then close its quadratic form in the energy norm. Determine whether L* has any discrete positive eigenvalue below its essential spectrum and whether the linear Landau–Lifshitz generator \(J_*L_*\) has purely imaginary isolated modes.

## Resolution and safeguards

The target is conditional and its nonemptiness must be established; an isolated internal eigenvalue is distinct from a radiative resonance and from nonlinear damping.

## Review checklist

Verify target homotopy groups, regularity/topology, energy finiteness, domain and boundary assumptions, ordinary counterexamples and prior work. A topological invariant is not automatically a dynamical stability theorem.
