# GC208: Josephson confinement of fractional vortices

**Precise candidate target v1, 2026-10-30.** Historical prompt: [GC208](../problems/08-topology-multicomponent/GC208.md). No claim of novel open status or Lean certification.

## Model and exact mathematical question

On \(\Omega_R=B_R(0)\setminus(B_{1/4}(Re_1/2)\cup B_{1/4}(-Re_1/2))\) for R>2, consider \(\theta_1,\theta_2\in H^1_{loc}(\Omega_R;\mathbb R/2\pi\mathbb Z)\) with windings (1,0) and (0,1) respectively around the two holes and zero total relative winding on the outer boundary. Define \(E_\eta=\int_{\Omega_R}[\tfrac12(|\nabla\theta_1|^2+|\nabla\theta_2|^2)+\eta(1-\cos(\theta_1-\theta_2))]dx\), η>0, subtract the single-vortex logarithmic reference energy, and determine whether minimal interaction is asymptotically \(\tau_\eta R+o(R)\).

## Resolution and safeguards

The moving-hole geometry as written puts hole centers at ±R/2 inside B_R, but logarithmic renormalization must be defined explicitly when deriving any finite tension. A nonzero Josephson string is not automatic if boundary winding constraints are inconsistent.

## Review checklist

Verify target homotopy groups, regularity/topology, energy finiteness, domain and boundary assumptions, ordinary counterexamples and prior work. A topological invariant is not automatically a dynamical stability theorem.
