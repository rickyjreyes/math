# GC208: revised mathematically explicit candidate

**Definition revision v1.1 (2026-10-30).** Earlier scoping draft corrected for internal domain or topology inconsistency. Original candidate: [GC208](../problems/08-topology-multicomponent/GC208.md).

## Exact proposed target

Fix \(R>4\), let \(\Omega_R=B_{2R}(0)\setminus(\overline{B_{1/4}(-Re_1/2)}\cup\overline{B_{1/4}(Re_1/2)})\), with two disjoint holes. For maps \(\theta_j\in H^1(\Omega_R;S^1)\), prescribe windings \((1,0)\) for θ1 and \((0,1)\) for θ2 around the two inner boundaries; outer winding for each θj is consequently 1. With Eη=∫Ω_R[1/2(|∇θ1|²+|∇θ2|²)+η(1−cos(θ1−θ2))]dx, η>0, define the interaction cost \(I_\eta(R)=\inf E_\eta-C_\eta(R)\), where \(C_\eta(R)\) is the sum of the two independently minimized single-vortex energies on the same outer disk with the corresponding single hole, using compatible boundary windings. Determine whether \(I_\eta(R)/R\to\tau_\eta\in(0,\infty)\) as R→∞.

## Acceptance and outstanding verification

The outer winding must equal the sum of inner winding numbers for each component; requiring zero outer winding would contradict the boundary data. The comparison energy Cη(R) must include explicit single-hole domains and any renormalized gauge choice. If ambiguity remains, this is a scoping proposal, not a finished sharp-tension theorem.

**Status:** scoped candidate pending independent analytical and prior-art review, not an established open theorem or Lean proof.
