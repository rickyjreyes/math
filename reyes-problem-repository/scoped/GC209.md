# GC209: Absence of isolated π₃ charges for nonvanishing complex scalar fields

**Precise candidate target v1, 2026-10-30.** Historical prompt: [GC209](../problems/08-topology-multicomponent/GC209.md). No claim of novel open status or Lean certification.

## Model and exact mathematical question

For smooth maps \(u:S^3\to S^1\subset\mathbb C\), classify homotopy classes and prove whether every such field admits a continuous deformation to the constant map without crossing zero intensity. More generally compare \(\pi_3(S^1)\) with vortex winding in \(\pi_1(S^1)\) on punctured 3-space.

## Resolution and safeguards

The nonvanishing target-space assumption is explicit. This is a known homotopy calculation; additional 'emergent conserved particle charge' would need a different target or dynamical constraint.

## Review checklist

Verify target homotopy groups, regularity/topology, energy finiteness, domain and boundary assumptions, ordinary counterexamples and prior work. A topological invariant is not automatically a dynamical stability theorem.
