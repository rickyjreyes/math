# GC210: Discrete winding preservation under interpolation

**Precise candidate target v1, 2026-10-30.** Historical prompt: [GC210](../problems/08-topology-multicomponent/GC210.md). No claim of novel open status or Lean certification.

## Model and exact mathematical question

On \(\mathbb T^2\), let \(u\in C^2(\mathbb T^2;S^1)\) and uniform periodic triangulations of mesh h→0. Assign nodal values u(v) and interpolate along each edge by the shortest geodesic on S¹, provided neighboring phase differences are strictly less than π. Define discrete winding pair by sums of these principal edge increments around fundamental cycles. Prove that for all sufficiently small h the discrete pair equals the continuum winding pair, and give an explicit h-bound in terms of \(\|\nabla u\|_\infty\).

## Resolution and safeguards

A winding invariant is well-defined on the chosen mesh; for Hopf degree a distinct three-dimensional interpolation/cell complex would be required.

## Review checklist

Verify target homotopy groups, regularity/topology, energy finiteness, domain and boundary assumptions, ordinary counterexamples and prior work. A topological invariant is not automatically a dynamical stability theorem.
