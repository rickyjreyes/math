# GC211: Creating opposite local Hopf charges under smooth forcing

**Precise candidate target v1, 2026-10-30.** Historical prompt: [GC211](../problems/08-topology-multicomponent/GC211.md). No claim of novel open status or Lean certification.

## Model and exact mathematical question

Let \(n_t=n\times\delta E/\delta n+F(t,x)\) on \([0,1]\times\mathbb R^3\), where E is the Faddeev energy, \(F\perp n\), smooth and supported in \([0,1]\times B_3\). Take n(0) constant. Let \(B_1(a_\pm)\) be disjoint balls in B3 and require n(1) constant on their boundaries with their restricted compactifications carrying Hopf charges +1 and -1, respectively. Determine whether such a smooth finite-cost control exists and the infimum \(\int_0^1\int|F|^2dxdt\).

## Resolution and safeguards

Total global Hopf charge remains zero for a smooth homotopy; the local charges must be computed using constant boundary values. Derive a legitimate controlled flow, not arbitrary maps disguised as dynamics.

## Review checklist

Verify target homotopy groups, regularity/topology, energy finiteness, domain and boundary assumptions, ordinary counterexamples and prior work. A topological invariant is not automatically a dynamical stability theorem.
