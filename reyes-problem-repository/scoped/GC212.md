# GC212: Pathwise invariance of Hopf charge under stochastic continuity

**Precise candidate target v1, 2026-10-30.** Historical prompt: [GC212](../problems/08-topology-multicomponent/GC212.md). No claim of novel open status or Lean certification.

## Model and exact mathematical question

Let \((\Omega,\mathcal F,\mathbb P)\) be any probability space and \(n_\omega\in C([0,T],C(S^3,S^2))\) for almost every ω. Determine whether \(Q(n_\omega(t))=Q(n_\omega(0))\) for all t almost surely. For random paths defined only on \([0,\tau(\omega))\), determine the necessary kind of loss of continuity before the integer can change.

## Resolution and safeguards

This is a pathwise homotopy-invariance theorem, so the continuous nonvanishing case is settled by topology rather than a new stochastic discovery. A stochastic PDE version must justify the sample-path continuity assumption.

## Review checklist

Verify target homotopy groups, regularity/topology, energy finiteness, domain and boundary assumptions, ordinary counterexamples and prior work. A topological invariant is not automatically a dynamical stability theorem.
