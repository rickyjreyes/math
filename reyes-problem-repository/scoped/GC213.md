# GC213: Two-component magnetic vortex lattice variational problem

**Precise candidate target v1, 2026-10-30.** Historical prompt: [GC213](../problems/08-topology-multicomponent/GC213.md). No claim of novel open status or Lean certification.

## Model and exact mathematical question

Let L→\(\mathbb T^2\) be a Hermitian line bundle with first Chern number 2 and a fixed unitary connection A of constant curvature. For sections \(u,v\in H^1(L)\), define \(E_\gamma(u,v)=\int_{\mathbb T^2}(|D_Au|^2+|D_Av|^2+\tfrac12(1-|u|^2-|v|^2)^2+\gamma|u|^2|v|^2)dx\), \(\gamma\in[0,2]\). Classify the zero sets and symmetry classes of global minimizers as gamma varies.

## Resolution and safeguards

The magnetic flux fixes vortex degree without an impossible globally periodic vector potential. Existence follows from coercivity/direct methods only after the fixed-bundle formulation is checked.

## Review checklist

Verify target homotopy groups, regularity/topology, energy finiteness, domain and boundary assumptions, ordinary counterexamples and prior work. A topological invariant is not automatically a dynamical stability theorem.
