# GC214: Topology of the quotient minimizer space

**Precise candidate target v1, 2026-10-30.** Historical prompt: [GC214](../problems/08-topology-multicomponent/GC214.md). No claim of novel open status or Lean certification.

## Model and exact mathematical question

For fixed \(m>0\) with nonempty constrained minimizer set \(\mathcal M_m=\{u\in H^2(\mathbb R^2;\mathbb C):M(u)=m,E_W(u)=I(m)\}\), define the quotient \(\mathcal Q_m=\mathcal M_m/(\mathbb R^2\times S^1)\) with quotient H² topology. Determine its connected components and fundamental group, and classify masses for which \(\mathcal Q_m\) is a singleton.

## Resolution and safeguards

Existence and Hausdorff/compact quotient properties need proof; if no minimizers, the quotient is empty and no homotopy invariant should be invented.

## Review checklist

Verify target homotopy groups, regularity/topology, energy finiteness, domain and boundary assumptions, ordinary counterexamples and prior work. A topological invariant is not automatically a dynamical stability theorem.
