# GC220: P-completeness of bounded-time Rule-110 prediction

**Explicit decision problem v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC220](../problems/09-computability-complexity/GC220.md). No unverified complexity hardness or novelty is asserted.

## Model, encoding and quantified question

For elementary CA Rule 110 with rule table 01101110 on finite binary words extended by a fixed periodic background, input a finite initial perturbation w, unary time 1^T and site i in unary, and ask whether the cell at i is 1 after T synchronous updates. Determine whether this language is P-complete under logspace many-one reductions or belongs to a strictly smaller standard class.

## Acceptance / disproof condition

The rule, background, unary-time representation and decision predicate are fixed. A P-completeness proof needs a uniform logspace reduction; membership in P follows by direct simulation.

## Scope check

Any complexity-class result must use the exact input representation, resource and promise model stated above. Verify established complexity and computability results before marking open.
