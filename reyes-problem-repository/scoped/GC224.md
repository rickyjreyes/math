# GC224: Stable localized equilibrium decision for a lattice energy

**Explicit decision problem v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC224](../problems/09-computability-complexity/GC224.md). No unverified complexity hardness or novelty is asserted.

## Model, encoding and quantified question

Input a finite graph \(G=(V,E)\), positive rational λ and rational K. Let \(E_G(x)=\sum_{i\in V}(x_i^2-1)^2+\lambda\sum_{(i,j)\in E}(x_i-x_j)^2\), \(x\in\mathbb R^{|V|}\). Decide whether there exists x with \(E_G(x)\le K\), \(\nabla E_G(x)=0\), Hessian \(D^2E_G(x)\succ0\), and \(\max_i x_i-\min_i x_i\ge1\) (a nonconstant localized-pattern proxy). Classify the decision complexity under binary rational encoding.

## Acceptance / disproof condition

The problem is first-order definable over real closed fields; membership in the existential theory of reals is clear. Hardness needs a reduction for this exact energy and nontriviality test.
