# GC233: Promise gap of a rational graph Laplacian

**Explicit decision problem v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC233](../problems/09-computability-complexity/GC233.md). No unverified complexity hardness or novelty is asserted.

## Model, encoding and quantified question

Input a finite connected simple graph G with n≥5 vertices encoded by its adjacency matrix. Let \(L_G\) be its integer graph Laplacian and \(\delta=40/n^2\). Under the promise \(\lambda_2(L_G)\le20/n^2\) or \(\lambda_2(L_G)\ge80/n^2\), decide which case holds. Classify bit complexity and produce sound rational spectral certificates for both cases.

## Acceptance / disproof condition

Both promised branches occur for n≥5 (paths versus complete graphs). Verify eigenvalue inequalities with rational certificates, not untrusted floating-point values. This is a tractable linear-algebra decision problem unless a distinctly harder variant is demonstrated.
