# GC236: Frechet derivative of a cubic–quintic energy

**Lean-oriented exact proposition v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC236](../problems/10-lean-certified-analysis/GC236.md). Known analytic results and any existing Lean proofs must be distinguished from missing formalization.

## Exact mathematical theorem or verification target

On complex \(H^2(\mathbb R^2)\) viewed as a **real** Banach space, define \(E(u)=\|(\Delta+1)u\|_2^2-\tfrac12\|u\|_4^4+\tfrac13\|u\|_6^6\). Prove E is C¹ and \(DE(u)[h]=2\operatorname{Re}\int\overline{((\Delta+1)^2u-|u|^2u+|u|^4u)}h\,dx\) whenever u∈H⁴; for general u∈H² use the corresponding H^{-2}–H² dual pairing.

## Acceptance / caveat

Formalize the bounded multilinear remainders and the real-linear derivative; an L² Hessian formula at arbitrary H² input is a domain error.
