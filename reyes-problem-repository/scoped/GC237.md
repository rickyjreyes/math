# GC237: Conditional direct-method minimizer theorem

**Lean-oriented exact proposition v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC237](../problems/10-lean-certified-analysis/GC237.md). Known analytic results and any existing Lean proofs must be distinguished from missing formalization.

## Exact mathematical theorem or verification target

Let \(X=H^2(\mathbb R^2;\mathbb C)\), \(S_m=\{u:\|u\|_2^2=m\}\), and E=EW. Prove the following implication: if \(I(m)>-\infty\) and some minimizing sequence admits translations a_n such that \(u_n(\cdot+a_n)\rightharpoonup u\) in H², converges strongly in L²,L⁴,L⁶, and \(\|u\|_2^2=m\), then u∈S_m and E(u)=I(m).

## Acceptance / caveat

The compactness hypotheses are explicit and not mistaken for a proved whole-space concentration-compactness theorem. Verify lower semicontinuity of the kinetic term and strong convergence of negative nonlinear terms.
