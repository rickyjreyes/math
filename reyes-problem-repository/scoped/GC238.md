# GC238: Phase-translation quotient distance

**Lean-oriented exact proposition v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC238](../problems/10-lean-certified-analysis/GC238.md). Known analytic results and any existing Lean proofs must be distinguished from missing formalization.

## Exact mathematical theorem or verification target

For \(u,v\in H^2(\mathbb R^2;\mathbb C)\), define \(d_G(u,v)=\inf_{a\in\mathbb R^2,\theta\in\mathbb R}\|u-e^{i\theta}v(\cdot-a)\|_{H^2}\). Prove nonnegativity, symmetry, triangle inequality, invariance under G, and that \(d_G(u,v)=0\) implies u and v belong to the same G orbit; hence the induced metric on H²/G is well-defined.

## Acceptance / caveat

Check orbit closedness including u=0 and escaping translations, rather than assuming a noncompact group automatically yields a metric quotient.
