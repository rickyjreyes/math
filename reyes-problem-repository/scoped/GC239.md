# GC239: Fractional Sobolev smoothing of a fourth-order semigroup

**Lean-oriented exact proposition v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC239](../problems/10-lean-certified-analysis/GC239.md). Known analytic results and any existing Lean proofs must be distinguished from missing formalization.

## Exact mathematical theorem or verification target

On \(\mathbb T^2\), let \(S(t)=e^{-t(1+\Delta)^2}\) and define H^s via Fourier weights \((1+|k|^2)^s\). For real s and r∈[0,4], prove \(\|S(t)f\|_{H^{s+r}}\le C_r t^{-r/4}\|f\|_{H^s}\) for 0<t≤1. Determine optimal exponent for r>0 and why exponential long-time H² decay fails on the \(|k|=1\) kernel.

## Acceptance / caveat

Use the exact multiplier \(e^{-t(1-|k|^2)^2}\) and finite shell neutral modes, avoiding an invalid global positive spectral gap.
