# GC240: Strong convergence of torus Fourier projectors

**Lean-oriented exact proposition v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC240](../problems/10-lean-certified-analysis/GC240.md). Known analytic results and any existing Lean proofs must be distinguished from missing formalization.

## Exact mathematical theorem or verification target

For N∈N define \(P_Nf=\sum_{|k|\le N}\widehat f(k)e^{ik\cdot x}\) on \(L^2(\mathbb T^2)\), normalized Fourier coefficients. Prove \(P_N\) is an orthogonal projector, \(\|P_Nf\|_2\le\|f\|_2\), \(\|P_Nf-f\|_{H^s}\to0\) for each s∈R and f∈H^s, and \(\|f\|_2^2=(2\pi)^2\sum_k|\widehat f(k)|^2\).

## Acceptance / caveat

Exact torus side length and Fourier normalization matter; state all finite sums and limits with Mathlib-supported summability rather than rewriting Parseval as a finite-dimensional surrogate.

## Formal proof contract

A full resolution must identify Lean 4 declaration(s), exact theorem statement, Mathlib imports, successful exact-head CI, no sorry/admit or added unproved axioms, and independent theorem-to-mathematics correspondence. A file in this folder alone is **not** a Lean proof.
