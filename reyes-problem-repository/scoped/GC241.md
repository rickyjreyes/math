# GC241: Isolated spectrum under bounded self-adjoint perturbation

**Lean-oriented exact proposition v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC241](../problems/10-lean-certified-analysis/GC241.md). Known analytic results and any existing Lean proofs must be distinguished from missing formalization.

## Exact mathematical theorem or verification target

Let H be a complex Hilbert space, A a bounded self-adjoint operator, λ₀∈σ(A) isolated by gap g>0, and P its finite-rank spectral projection on (λ₀−g/2,λ₀+g/2). For bounded self-adjoint B with \(\|B\|<g/4\), prove the Riesz projection of A+B on the same contour exists, has rank rank(P), and spectral points in that cluster lie within \(\|B\|\) of σ(A).

## Acceptance / caveat

The interval's contour must enclose only one spectral cluster and avoid others at the chosen radius. Formalization needs spectral calculus, a perturbative Neumann bound and a dimension-invariance argument.
