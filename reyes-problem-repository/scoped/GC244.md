# GC244: Mass-tail estimate from a second moment

**Lean-oriented exact proposition v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC244](../problems/10-lean-certified-analysis/GC244.md). Known analytic results and any existing Lean proofs must be distinguished from missing formalization.

## Exact mathematical theorem or verification target

For \(d\ge1\), \(u\in L^2(\mathbb R^d)\) with \(\int|x|^2|u(x)|^2dx<\infty\), and R>0, prove \(\int_{|x|>R}|u|^2dx\le R^{-2}\int|x|^2|u|^2dx\). Show the power R^{-2} cannot be uniformly improved over all such u without extra assumptions.

## Acceptance / caveat

This is Chebyshev/Markov in a Sobolev-compatible language, not a novel concentration theorem. Any H²-controlled confinement estimate without a moment bound is a different problem.
