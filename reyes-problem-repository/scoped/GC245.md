# GC245: Exact rank and nullspace of a degenerate auxiliary-field Hessian

**Lean-oriented exact proposition v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC245](../problems/10-lean-certified-analysis/GC245.md). Known analytic results and any existing Lean proofs must be distinguished from missing formalization.

## Exact mathematical theorem or verification target

For real a≠0, let \(M(a)=\begin{pmatrix}a&a\\a&a\end{pmatrix}\) on \(\mathbb R^2\). Formalize \(\operatorname{rank}M(a)=1\), \(\ker M(a)=\operatorname{span}\{(1,-1)\}\), and \(\operatorname{nullity}M(a)=1\), with all cases of the coefficient a=0 stated separately.

## Acceptance / caveat

These are exact finite-dimensional Lean lemmas, useful as a constraint-rank benchmark. A Hessian rank alone does not certify the complete Dirac constraint chain or ghost freedom.
