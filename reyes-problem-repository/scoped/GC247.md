# GC247: Abstract scattering unitarity from complete wave operators

**Lean-oriented exact proposition v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC247](../problems/10-lean-certified-analysis/GC247.md). Known analytic results and any existing Lean proofs must be distinguished from missing formalization.

## Exact mathematical theorem or verification target

Let H0,H be self-adjoint operators on a complex Hilbert space. Assume incoming/outgoing wave operators W± exist and are **unitary from** the absolutely continuous subspace \(\mathcal H_{ac}(H_0)\) **onto** \(\mathcal H_{ac}(H)\). Define scattering operator \(S=W_+^*W_-\) on \(\mathcal H_{ac}(H_0)\). Prove \(S^*S=SS^*=I\).

## Acceptance / caveat

The theorem is classically immediate once completeness and isometry are assumptions. The Lean contribution is encoding the correct subspaces and operator domains, not proving completeness for an unspecified PDE.
