# GC248: Homotopy invariance of torus winding

**Lean-oriented exact proposition v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC248](../problems/10-lean-certified-analysis/GC248.md). Known analytic results and any existing Lean proofs must be distinguished from missing formalization.

## Exact mathematical theorem or verification target

Let \(H\in C^1([0,1]\times\mathbb T^2;S^1)\). For each s, define winding numbers \(n_j(s)=(2\pi i)^{-1}\int_0^{2\pi}\overline{H(s,\gamma_j(t))}\,\frac d{dt}H(s,\gamma_j(t))\,dt\), where \(\gamma_j\) are the coordinate generating loops. Prove \(n_j(s)\in\mathbb Z\) and \(n_j(s)\) is constant in s for j=1,2.

## Acceptance / caveat

The full nonvanishing homotopy is assumed. Formalize the exact integral and degree; no PDE guarantee that a solution remains nonzero follows from this theorem.
