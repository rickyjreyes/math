# GC249: Sound interval residual certificate for a polynomial ODE

**Lean-oriented exact proposition v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC249](../problems/10-lean-certified-analysis/GC249.md). Known analytic results and any existing Lean proofs must be distinguished from missing formalization.

## Exact mathematical theorem or verification target

On [0,T] let \(x'=x^2\), \(x(0)=x_0\in\mathbb Q\), and suppose an interval checker verifies a rational piecewise-C¹ approximation y, \(|y(0)-x_0|\le\delta\), \(|y'(t)-y(t)^2|\le\varepsilon\), and \(|x(t)|,|y(t)|\le R\) for all t in [0,T]. Prove \(|x(t)-y(t)|\le\delta e^{2Rt}+\varepsilon(e^{2Rt}-1)/(2R)\), with R=0 interpreted by continuity.

## Acceptance / caveat

The checker must also validate enclosure of the true solution in [-R,R], not just assert it. Prove soundness of the certificate parser and analytic inequality separately.

## Formal proof contract

A full resolution must identify Lean 4 declaration(s), exact theorem statement, Mathlib imports, successful exact-head CI, no sorry/admit or added unproved axioms, and independent theorem-to-mathematics correspondence. A file in this folder alone is **not** a Lean proof.
