# GC253: Residual-to-error transfer for a Lipschitz wave truncation

**Lean-oriented exact proposition v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC253](../problems/10-lean-certified-analysis/GC253.md). Known analytic results and any existing Lean proofs must be distinguished from missing formalization.

## Exact mathematical theorem or verification target

Let \(f:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^n\) be globally L-Lipschitz, x a solution of x'=f(x), and y∈C¹([0,T];Rⁿ) with \(\|x(0)-y(0)\|\le\delta\) and \(\sup_{[0,T]}\|y'-f(y)\|\le\varepsilon\). Formalize \(\|x(t)-y(t)\|\le\delta e^{Lt}+\varepsilon\int_0^t e^{L(t-s)}ds\) for all t≤T. Instantiate for a finite-dimensional Fourier-Galerkin truncation of W with a separately proved bound on its nonlinear Lipschitz constant inside a radius-R ball.

## Acceptance / caveat

Do not infer a global Lipschitz bound for a polynomial nonlinearity on all space. The finite-dimensional bounded-ball instantiation must include an invariant or certified radius R.
