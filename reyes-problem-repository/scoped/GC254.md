# GC254: Lattice derivation of fourth-order continuum dispersion

**Model-complete research candidate v1 (2026-10-30)**. Original: [GC254](../problems/11-physical-emergence-multiscale/GC254.md). Mathematical well-definedness and prior-art novelty are separate review gates.

## Exact microscopic/continuum target

For h∈(0,1), on \(\ell_h^2(h\mathbb Z)\) define \(\Delta_hu_n=(u_{n+1}-2u_n+u_{n-1})/h^2\), \(L_h=(\Delta_h+1)^2\), and \(iu_{h,t}=L_hu_h-|u_h|^2u_h+|u_h|^4u_h\). Let u solve the corresponding one-dimensional continuum W flow with smooth Schwartz initial data, and initialize u_h(0,n)=u(0,nh). Determine the optimal power p for \(\sup_{t\in[0,1]}\|u_h(t)-u(t,nh)\|_{\ell_h^2}\le C h^p\), and calculate the quartic-minimum lattice dispersion symbol.

## Decisive test and caveat

The sample/interpolation correspondence and fixed time horizon are explicit. Prove uniform smoothness of both flows; a Taylor expansion of Δ_h alone is not a nonlinear convergence theorem.

## Review conditions

Verify dimensional units, Fourier and interpolation conventions, existence of relevant solutions, quantifier/limit order and matched error estimate. A formal limiting expansion by itself is not a convergent approximation theorem.
