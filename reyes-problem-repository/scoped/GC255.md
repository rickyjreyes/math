# GC255: Discrete-to-continuum constrained minimizer convergence

**Model-complete research candidate v1 (2026-10-30)**. Original: [GC255](../problems/11-physical-emergence-multiscale/GC255.md). Mathematical well-definedness and prior-art novelty are separate review gates.

## Exact microscopic/continuum target

On \(h\mathbb Z\), for \(u\in\ell_h^2\) with \(h\sum|u_n|^2=m\), define \(E_h(u)=h\sum_n(|(\Delta_h+1)u_n|^2-\tfrac12|u_n|^4+\tfrac13|u_n|^6)\). Let \(I_h(m)=\inf E_h\), and \(I(m)\) be the analogous continuum H²(R) infimum. Determine whether \(I_h(m)\to I(m)\) and whether discrete minimizers, after translation and band-limited interpolation, converge strongly in H² to continuum minimizers wherever attainment holds.

## Decisive test and caveat

The discrete energy, fixed mass, interpolation and limiting topology are exact. Address loss of compactness and the fact a piecewise-constant interpolant is not H².

## Review conditions

Verify dimensional units, Fourier and interpolation conventions, existence of relevant solutions, quantifier/limit order and matched error estimate. A formal limiting expansion by itself is not a convergent approximation theorem.
