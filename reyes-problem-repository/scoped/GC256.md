# GC256: Envelope approximation near the quartic minimum shell

**Model-complete research candidate v1 (2026-10-30)**. Original: [GC256](../problems/11-physical-emergence-multiscale/GC256.md). Mathematical well-definedness and prior-art novelty are separate review gates.

## Exact microscopic/continuum target

For one-dimensional W, let \(A\in C([0,T];H^6(\mathbb R))\) solve \(iA_T=-4A_{XX}-|A|^2A\). Set \(u_{app}^\varepsilon(t,x)=\varepsilon A(\varepsilon^2t,\varepsilon x)e^{ix}\), 0<ε<1, and let u^ε be the exact W solution with \(u^\varepsilon(0)=u_{app}^\varepsilon(0)\). Determine the largest p>0 for which \(\sup_{0\le t\le T/\varepsilon^2}\|u^\varepsilon(t)-u_{app}^\varepsilon(t)\|_{L^2}\le C\varepsilon^p\).

## Decisive test and caveat

Verify the carrier dispersion and signed envelope coefficient by expansion. The quintic term enters at higher order but may accumulate; prove the residual and nonlinear stability bounds.

## Review conditions

Verify dimensional units, Fourier and interpolation conventions, existence of relevant solutions, quantifier/limit order and matched error estimate. A formal limiting expansion by itself is not a convergent approximation theorem.
