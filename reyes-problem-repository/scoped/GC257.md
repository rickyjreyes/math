# GC257: Signs of cubic and quintic onsite envelope coefficients

**Model-complete research candidate v1 (2026-10-30)**. Original: [GC257](../problems/11-physical-emergence-multiscale/GC257.md). Mathematical well-definedness and prior-art novelty are separate review gates.

## Exact microscopic/continuum target

For complex lattice amplitudes \(z_n\), consider \(iz_{n,t}=-(\Delta_h+1)^2z_n-a|z_n|^2z_n+b|z_n|^4z_n\), with rational parameters a,b∈[-2,2]. Under carrier ansatz \(z_n=\varepsilon A(\varepsilon nh,\varepsilon^2t)e^{inh}\), compute the order-ε³ and ε⁵ envelope nonlinearities and classify exactly which (a,b) give focusing cubic and defocusing quintic signs under the W Hamiltonian convention.

## Decisive test and caveat

Explicitly distinguish the sign convention in i z_t from that in energy. This is a coefficient-derivation benchmark, not a claim the full quintic envelope is justified on the cubic time scale.

## Review conditions

Verify dimensional units, Fourier and interpolation conventions, existence of relevant solutions, quantifier/limit order and matched error estimate. A formal limiting expansion by itself is not a convergent approximation theorem.
