# GC259: No amplitude-dependent curvature from a purely linear lattice

**Model-complete research candidate v1 (2026-10-30)**. Original: [GC259](../problems/11-physical-emergence-multiscale/GC259.md). Mathematical well-definedness and prior-art novelty are separate review gates.

## Exact microscopic/continuum target

Let \(L_h:\ell_h^2\to\ell_h^2\) be any real linear finite-range lattice operator whose coefficients depend on position and h but **not** on the field. Suppose its interpolated continuum action converges strongly on a dense smooth class to an operator L. Decide whether L can equal \(u\mapsto K_1(u)u\), with \(K_1(u)=\operatorname{div}(\operatorname{Im}(\bar u\nabla u)/(1+|u|^2))\), for every smooth compactly supported complex u.

## Decisive test and caveat

Limits of linear operators are linear on common domains, while u↦K1(u)u is generally nonlinear. An exact additivity counterexample is a decisive obstruction; nonlinear microscopic couplings are a separate program.

## Review conditions

Verify dimensional units, Fourier and interpolation conventions, existence of relevant solutions, quantifier/limit order and matched error estimate. A formal limiting expansion by itself is not a convergent approximation theorem.
