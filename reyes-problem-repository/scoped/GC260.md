# GC260: Homogenized preferred frequency of a fourth-order operator

**Model-complete research candidate v1 (2026-10-30)**. Original: [GC260](../problems/11-physical-emergence-multiscale/GC260.md). Mathematical well-definedness and prior-art novelty are separate review gates.

## Exact microscopic/continuum target

For \(a:\mathbb R^2\to[1/2,2]\) smooth, positive and \(\mathbb Z^2\)-periodic, set \(A_\varepsilon=-\nabla\cdot(a(x/\varepsilon)\nabla)\), with positive self-adjoint realization on L²(R²), and \(H_\varepsilon=(A_\varepsilon-1)^2\) via functional calculus. Determine whether \((H_\varepsilon+i)^{-1}\) converges strongly to \(((-\nabla\cdot A_{hom}\nabla-1)^2+i)^{-1}\), and characterize the minimizing wavevector ellipse \(k^TA_{hom}k=1\).

## Decisive test and caveat

The homogenized coefficient matrix is the classical corrector tensor for the chosen scalar a. Strong resolvent convergence does not automatically imply uniform band-edge eigenfunction convergence.

## Review conditions

Verify dimensional units, Fourier and interpolation conventions, existence of relevant solutions, quantifier/limit order and matched error estimate. A formal limiting expansion by itself is not a convergent approximation theorem.
