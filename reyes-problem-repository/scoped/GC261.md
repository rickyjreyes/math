# GC261: Random-disorder breakdown of an envelope approximation

**Model-complete research candidate v1 (2026-10-30)**. Original: [GC261](../problems/11-physical-emergence-multiscale/GC261.md). Mathematical well-definedness and prior-art novelty are separate review gates.

## Exact microscopic/continuum target

For h=ε→0, on \(\ell_h^2\) let \(iu_t=(\Delta_h+1)^2u+\varepsilon^\alpha\xi_nu_n-|u_n|^2u_n\), where ξ_n are iid symmetric ±1 random variables and α∈[0,4]. Compare with the disorder-free continuum envelope initialized from the same Schwartz carrier data as GC256, over times \(0\le t\le\varepsilon^{-2}\). Determine the set of α for which \(\sup_t\|u_h(t)-u_{app}^\varepsilon(t,nh)\|_{\ell_h^2}\to0\) in probability.

## Decisive test and caveat

The iid disorder distribution, amplitude scale, time, norm and convergence mode are frozen. Long-time random phase accumulation must be estimated; a deterministic O(ε^α) forcing bound may be too crude to locate a sharp threshold.

## Review conditions

Verify dimensional units, Fourier and interpolation conventions, existence of relevant solutions, quantifier/limit order and matched error estimate. A formal limiting expansion by itself is not a convergent approximation theorem.
