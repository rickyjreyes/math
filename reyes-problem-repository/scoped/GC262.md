# GC262: Boundary correction for a fourth-order discrete operator

**Model-complete research candidate v1 (2026-10-30)**. Original: [GC262](../problems/11-physical-emergence-multiscale/GC262.md). Mathematical well-definedness and prior-art novelty are separate review gates.

## Exact microscopic/continuum target

On (0,1), take h=1/(N+1), the N×N Dirichlet second-difference matrix \(A_h\) approximating -d²/dx², and \(H_h=(A_h-1)^2\). Let \(H=(-d^2_D/dx^2-1)^2\) on L²(0,1). For each fixed eigenvalue index j, determine the coefficient c_j in \(\lambda_j(H_h)=\lambda_j(H)+c_jh^2+O_j(h^4)\), with interpolation of eigenvectors in H¹.

## Decisive test and caveat

The fourth-order boundary choice is inherited by squaring the Dirichlet Laplacian, not clamped biharmonic data. The 1D sine eigenvalues make the coefficient exactly computable.

## Review conditions

Verify dimensional units, Fourier and interpolation conventions, existence of relevant solutions, quantifier/limit order and matched error estimate. A formal limiting expansion by itself is not a convergent approximation theorem.
