# GC263: Finite periodic-lattice negative-energy threshold

**Model-complete research candidate v1 (2026-10-30)**. Original: [GC263](../problems/11-physical-emergence-multiscale/GC263.md). Mathematical well-definedness and prior-art novelty are separate review gates.

## Exact microscopic/continuum target

For 1D cubic–quintic lattice W with h=1, let \(E_N\) be the energy from GC255 on a periodic ring of N sites, and \(E_\infty\) on \(\ell^2(\mathbb Z)\). Define \(m_c(N)=\inf\{m>0: \inf_{M(u)=m}E_N(u)<0\}\) and \(m_c(\infty)\) analogously, with inf empty=∞. Determine whether \(m_c(N)\to m_c(\infty)\) as N→∞ and the best N-dependent error.

## Decisive test and caveat

The negative-energy criterion avoids assuming attained solitons. Address finite-volume uniform states and periodic-image interactions in comparing thresholds.

## Review conditions

Verify dimensional units, Fourier and interpolation conventions, existence of relevant solutions, quantifier/limit order and matched error estimate. A formal limiting expansion by itself is not a convergent approximation theorem.
