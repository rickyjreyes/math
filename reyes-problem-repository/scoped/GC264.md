# GC264: Convergence of discrete mass and energy invariants

**Model-complete research candidate v1 (2026-10-30)**. Original: [GC264](../problems/11-physical-emergence-multiscale/GC264.md). Mathematical well-definedness and prior-art novelty are separate review gates.

## Exact microscopic/continuum target

For \(u\in C([0,T];H^6(\mathbb R))\) a smooth W solution and its sampled values \(u_n(t)=u(t,nh)\), define \(M_h=h\sum|u_n|^2\) and E_h as in GC255. Prove uniform-in-t quadrature consistency \(\sup_{t\le T}(|M_h(t)-M(u(t))|+|E_h(t)-E_W(u(t))|)\le C_T h^2\), under explicit decay/moment hypotheses ensuring summability.

## Decisive test and caveat

The samples do not solve the lattice equation, so their discrete invariants need not be exactly conserved. Distinguish consistency of sampling from conservation by exact lattice dynamics.

## Review conditions

Verify dimensional units, Fourier and interpolation conventions, existence of relevant solutions, quantifier/limit order and matched error estimate. A formal limiting expansion by itself is not a convergent approximation theorem.
