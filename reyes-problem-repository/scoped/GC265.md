# GC265: Noncommuting lattice-spacing and long-time limits

**Model-complete research candidate v1 (2026-10-30)**. Original: [GC265](../problems/11-physical-emergence-multiscale/GC265.md). Mathematical well-definedness and prior-art novelty are separate review gates.

## Exact microscopic/continuum target

For h∈(0,1), define a damped lattice zero mode \(a_h(t)\in\mathbb C\) by \(\dot a_h=-h a_h\), \(a_h(0)=1\), and observable \(Q_h(t)=|a_h(t)|^2\). Compute both iterated limits \(\lim_{h\downarrow0}\lim_{t\to\infty}Q_h(t)\) and \(\lim_{t\to\infty}\lim_{h\downarrow0}Q_h(t)\), and decide whether they commute.

## Decisive test and caveat

This is an explicit counterexample showing failure without a uniform relaxation gap. Upgrading it to a nonlinear localized lattice requires demonstrating the same mechanism for a concrete wave observable.

## Review conditions

Verify dimensional units, Fourier and interpolation conventions, existence of relevant solutions, quantifier/limit order and matched error estimate. A formal limiting expansion by itself is not a convergent approximation theorem.
