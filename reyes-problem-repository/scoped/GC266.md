# GC266: Which measurements survive weak L² convergence?

**Model-complete research candidate v1 (2026-10-30)**. Original: [GC266](../problems/11-physical-emergence-multiscale/GC266.md). Mathematical well-definedness and prior-art novelty are separate review gates.

## Exact microscopic/continuum target

Let \(u_n\rightharpoonup u\) in L²(\(\mathbb T\)). For each fixed \(g\in L²\) define interferometric measurement \(O_g(v)=|\langle v,g\rangle|^2\), and define intensity \(I(v)=\int|v|^4\), where finite. Prove \(O_g(u_n)\to O_g(u)\), but determine whether \(I(u_n)\to I(u)\) follows for every uniformly H¹-bounded sequence, and exhibit a counterexample if the uniform regularity condition is dropped.

## Decisive test and caveat

On the compact 1D torus, a uniform H¹ bound gives strong L⁴ convergence along weak-L²-convergent sequences by Rellich; this important compactness distinction must be reflected in the answer.

## Review conditions

Verify dimensional units, Fourier and interpolation conventions, existence of relevant solutions, quantifier/limit order and matched error estimate. A formal limiting expansion by itself is not a convergent approximation theorem.
