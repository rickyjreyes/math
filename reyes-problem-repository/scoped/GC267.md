# GC267: Recovering two envelope couplings from amplitude tests

**Model-complete research candidate v1 (2026-10-30)**. Original: [GC267](../problems/11-physical-emergence-multiscale/GC267.md). Mathematical well-definedness and prior-art novelty are separate review gates.

## Exact microscopic/continuum target

For complex uniform solutions \(z_A(t)=Ae^{-i\omega(A)t}\) of \(iz_t=\omega_0z-a|z|^2z+b|z|^4z\), take A1=1 and A2=2 and known ω0. Define data \(D(a,b)=(\omega(1)-\omega_0,\omega(2)-\omega_0)\). Determine whether D is globally injective on \([-2,2]^2\), derive its exact inverse, and quantify reconstruction error from bounded deterministic frequency errors.

## Decisive test and caveat

This is a fully identifiable microscopic-to-envelope coefficient benchmark, not generic scattering inversion. It requires phase/frequency unwrapping if the observable is intensity rather than frequency.

## Review conditions

Verify dimensional units, Fourier and interpolation conventions, existence of relevant solutions, quantifier/limit order and matched error estimate. A formal limiting expansion by itself is not a convergent approximation theorem.
