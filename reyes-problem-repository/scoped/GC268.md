# GC268: Convergence of discrete winding under nonvanishing interpolation

**Model-complete research candidate v1 (2026-10-30)**. Original: [GC268](../problems/11-physical-emergence-multiscale/GC268.md). Mathematical well-definedness and prior-art novelty are separate review gates.

## Exact microscopic/continuum target

For \(u\in H^2(\mathbb T^2;\mathbb C)\) with \(\inf|u|\ge c>0\), sample u on uniform periodic grids h=2π/N and interpolate edge phases by shortest circular arcs. Let \(w_h\in\mathbb Z^2\) be the two discrete cycle sums divided by 2π. Prove \(w_h=w(u)\) for all sufficiently small h, with a bound on h determined by c and \(\|u\|_{H^2}\).

## Decisive test and caveat

H²(T²) embeds in continuous functions, making nonvanishing meaningful, but a quantitative Lipschitz modulus may require higher regularity; prove an H² Hölder bound or strengthen the assumption explicitly.

## Review conditions

Verify dimensional units, Fourier and interpolation conventions, existence of relevant solutions, quantifier/limit order and matched error estimate. A formal limiting expansion by itself is not a convergent approximation theorem.
