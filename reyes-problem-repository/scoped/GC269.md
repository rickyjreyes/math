# GC269: Thin-waveguide reduction to a fourth-order 2D operator

**Model-complete research candidate v1 (2026-10-30)**. Original: [GC269](../problems/11-physical-emergence-multiscale/GC269.md). Mathematical well-definedness and prior-art novelty are separate review gates.

## Exact microscopic/continuum target

Let \(\Omega_h=\mathbb T^2\times(0,h)\), h∈(0,1), and \(A_h=-\Delta_{\Omega_h}\) with Neumann transverse boundary conditions. Define \(H_h=(A_h-1)^2\) by functional calculus. For the linear Schrödinger equation \(iu_t=H_hu\), compare its transverse constant-mode projection to the exact 2D equation \(iv_t=(-\Delta_{\mathbb T^2}-1)^2v\). Prove an H² convergence error controlled by the initial energy in nonconstant transverse modes, uniformly for t∈[0,T].

## Decisive test and caveat

The constant transverse sector is exactly invariant in this frozen linear geometry; nontrivial homogenization requires a varying cross-section or nonlinear coupling.

## Review conditions

Verify dimensional units, Fourier and interpolation conventions, existence of relevant solutions, quantifier/limit order and matched error estimate. A formal limiting expansion by itself is not a convergent approximation theorem.
