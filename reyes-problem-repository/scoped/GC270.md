# GC270: Distinct envelopes with equal leading-order predictions

**Model-complete research candidate v1 (2026-10-30)**. Original: [GC270](../problems/11-physical-emergence-multiscale/GC270.md). Mathematical well-definedness and prior-art novelty are separate review gates.

## Exact microscopic/continuum target

On one-dimensional W near carrier k=1, compare envelope laws (A) \(iA_T=-4A_{XX}-|A|^2A\) and (B) \(iA_T=-4A_{XX}-|A|^2A+\varepsilon^2|A|^4A\), both with identical initial A0∈H^4. For fixed T, determine whether \(\sup_{0\le s\le T}\|A_B(s)-A_A(s)\|_{H^1}=O(\varepsilon^2)\), and whether an O(ε²) measurement can distinguish them.

## Decisive test and caveat

The two equations produce the same ε⁰ leading envelope, so leading-order agreement alone cannot identify quintic physics. Establish a uniform nonlinear perturbation estimate.

## Review conditions

Verify dimensional units, Fourier and interpolation conventions, existence of relevant solutions, quantifier/limit order and matched error estimate. A formal limiting expansion by itself is not a convergent approximation theorem.
