# GC271: Recovering nearest- and next-neighbor stiffness

**Model-complete research candidate v1 (2026-10-30)**. Original: [GC271](../problems/11-physical-emergence-multiscale/GC271.md). Mathematical well-definedness and prior-art novelty are separate review gates.

## Exact microscopic/continuum target

For h>0 and positive rational J1,J2, let the lattice dispersion \(\omega^2(k)=\omega_0^2+4J_1h^{-2}\sin^2(kh/2)+4J_2h^{-2}\sin^2(kh)\). Compute coefficients c2,c4 in \(\omega^2(k)=\omega_0^2+c_2k^2+c_4k^4+O(h^4k^6)\). Determine whether (J1,J2)↦(c2,c4) is injective for every fixed h>0, with exact inverse and conditioning as h→0.

## Decisive test and caveat

The coefficient map is explicit: c2=J1+4J2 and c4=−h²(J1/12+4J2/3). Prove the determinant and quantify ill-conditioning when microscopic spacing shrinks.

## Review conditions

Verify dimensional units, Fourier and interpolation conventions, existence of relevant solutions, quantifier/limit order and matched error estimate. A formal limiting expansion by itself is not a convergent approximation theorem.
