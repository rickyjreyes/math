# GC272: Breakdown of heavy-mode elimination at a crossing

**Model-complete research candidate v1 (2026-10-30)**. Original: [GC272](../problems/11-physical-emergence-multiscale/GC272.md). Mathematical well-definedness and prior-art novelty are separate review gates.

## Exact microscopic/continuum target

For real Δ and g, define \(H_{\Delta,g}=\begin{pmatrix}0&g\\g&\Delta\end{pmatrix}\). For initial (1,0), let \(\psi(t)=e^{-itH}(1,0)\). The scalar elimination model uses energy shift \(-g^2/\Delta\) when Δ≠0. On t∈[0,1/|g|] (g≠0), classify ratios \(|\Delta|/|g|\) for which the scalar approximation error is uniformly small, and show a nonzero lower bound on discarded-mode amplitude at Δ=0.

## Decisive test and caveat

The exact two-level solution quantifies resonance-induced failure. A continuum multiscale theorem would additionally require uniform spectral gaps across spatial wavevectors.

## Review conditions

Verify dimensional units, Fourier and interpolation conventions, existence of relevant solutions, quantifier/limit order and matched error estimate. A formal limiting expansion by itself is not a convergent approximation theorem.
