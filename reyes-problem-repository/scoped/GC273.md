# GC273: Discrete H² bound via Shannon interpolation

**Model-complete research candidate v1 (2026-10-30)**. Original: [GC273](../problems/11-physical-emergence-multiscale/GC273.md). Mathematical well-definedness and prior-art novelty are separate review gates.

## Exact microscopic/continuum target

For 0<h≤1 on h\(\mathbb Z^2\), define \(\Delta_h\) by nearest-neighbor differences and Fourier transform on \([-\pi/h,\pi/h]^2\). Let \(I_hu\) be its band-limited Shannon interpolant on \(\mathbb R^2\). Prove \(\|I_hu\|_{H^2}^2\le C(\|u\|_{\ell_h^2}^2+\|\Delta_hu\|_{\ell_h^2}^2)\) with C independent of 0<h≤1.

## Decisive test and caveat

The piecewise-constant interpolant would not lie in H²; band limitation and a uniform comparison of discrete and continuum symbols are essential.

## Review conditions

Verify dimensional units, Fourier and interpolation conventions, existence of relevant solutions, quantifier/limit order and matched error estimate. A formal limiting expansion by itself is not a convergent approximation theorem.
