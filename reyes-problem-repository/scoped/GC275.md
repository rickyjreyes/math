# GC275: Invertible phase-to-dispersion calibration of a photonic lattice

**Model-complete research candidate v1 (2026-10-30)**. Original: [GC275](../problems/11-physical-emergence-multiscale/GC275.md). Mathematical well-definedness and prior-art novelty are separate review gates.

## Exact microscopic/continuum target

On a square photonic lattice with positive baseline coupling J0, let two programmable couplings be \(J_x=J_0\cos\phi_x\), \(J_y=J_0\cos\phi_y\), \((\phi_x,\phi_y)\in(0,\pi/2)^2\). The Bloch dispersion is \(\omega(k)=\omega_0+2J_x(1-\cos k_xa)+2J_y(1-\cos k_ya)\). Derive the continuum quadratic coefficients \(D_x=J_xa^2,D_y=J_ya^2\), prove local/global invertibility of phase↔(Dx,Dy) on the stated domain, and bound conditioning near its endpoints.

## Decisive test and caveat

No unique fourth-order curvature operator follows from these two linear couplings. Higher-order Taylor terms and nonlinear devices need a separate calibration model.

## Review conditions

Verify dimensional units, Fourier and interpolation conventions, existence of relevant solutions, quantifier/limit order and matched error estimate. A formal limiting expansion by itself is not a convergent approximation theorem.
