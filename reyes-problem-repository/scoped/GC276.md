# GC276: Short-wave instability invisible to a stable envelope

**Model-complete research candidate v1 (2026-10-30)**. Original: [GC276](../problems/11-physical-emergence-multiscale/GC276.md). Mathematical well-definedness and prior-art novelty are separate review gates.

## Exact microscopic/continuum target

For h>0 let a two-band lattice linearization have Fourier symbol \(L_h(k)=\operatorname{diag}(-(|k|-1)^2,\ 1-(|k|-\pi/h)^2)\), \(k\in[-\pi/h,\pi/h]\). Define the low-frequency envelope sector as \(|k-1|\le1/10\). Determine whether spectral stability of the first band restricted to this sector implies \(\sup_{k}\sup\operatorname{Re}\sigma(L_h(k))\le0\); exhibit the precise hidden unstable mode or add a sufficient full-band gap condition.

## Decisive test and caveat

The example contains a positive growth rate at k=π/h and refutes unconditional stability transfer. An affirmative transfer theorem needs a uniform high-frequency spectral bound and approximation of the complete generator.

## Review conditions

Verify dimensional units, Fourier and interpolation conventions, existence of relevant solutions, quantifier/limit order and matched error estimate. A formal limiting expansion by itself is not a convergent approximation theorem.
