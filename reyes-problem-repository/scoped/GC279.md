# GC279: Cascadability limit of unreplenished passive optical intensity

**Device-level mathematical candidate v1, 2026-10-30.** [Original GC279](../problems/12-photonic-wave-computing/GC279.md). The device model is idealized and **not** an experimentally validated optical implementation.

## Explicit physical abstraction and quantified mathematical target

Consider a D-stage optical path carrying bit 1 as power P₀>0 and bit 0 as zero power. Each passive stage transmits a fixed fraction 0<η<1 and introduces no gain: P_{j+1}=ηP_j. A final detector declares 1 iff P_D≥τ with 0<τ<P₀. Determine the largest integer D for which all ideal input bits are decoded correctly, and prove that no fixed P₀,η,τ permits arbitrary-depth reliable composition without energy regeneration.

## Proof or counterexample acceptance

The exact bound is η^D P₀≥τ. A detector output is not by itself a fresh optical carrier; allowing electrical re-modulation or nonlinear optical gain constitutes a distinct model.

## Scientific and formalization boundary

Prove all logical implications for the stated inputs, component transfer functions, limits and error models. Note any already elementary result rather than declaring a novel open conjecture. A mathematical truth table does not certify fabricated hardware; any Lean statement must match this model exactly.
