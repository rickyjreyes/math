# GC280: Noisy resonator-memory retention bound

**Photonic mathematical specification v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC280](../problems/12-photonic-wave-computing/GC280.md). Precise models do not automatically constitute physical implementation or a new open result.

## Exact device/logic model and quantified claim

Let phase θ_t solve \(d\theta_t=\sqrt{2D}\,dW_t\) modulo 2π with D>0. Encode stored bits as θ0∈{0,π}, and observe \(Y_t=\cos\theta_t+N\), \(N\sim N(0,\sigma^2)\) independently. Define optimal equal-prior bit-error probability P_e(t) over all measurable decoders of Y_t. Determine \(T_{err}(p)=\sup\{t:P_e(t)\le p\}\) for p∈(0,1/2), including explicit bounds for D,σ.

## Formal resolution and negative test

The stochastic phase diffusion, readout, prior and error metric are exact. A claimed nonzero infinite retention time must account for convergence of phase to uniform distribution.

## Readiness gate

Audit detector thresholds, input/output encoding, amplification and nonlinear readout, worst-case or stochastic errors, units and robustness. Separate mathematical circuit semantics from hardware measurements and novelty.
