# GC283: Photon budget for fanout into N receivers

**Photonic mathematical specification v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC283](../problems/12-photonic-wave-computing/GC283.md). Precise models do not automatically constitute physical implementation or a new open result.

## Exact device/logic model and quantified claim

A coherent optical pulse with mean photon number μ is split by a passive lossless N-port uniformly into N outputs. Each receiver performs an ideal on/off photon count with unit efficiency; logical 1 is pulse present and 0 is vacuum. Determine minimum μ such that, conditional on sending 1, all N receivers click with probability at least 1−ε, ε∈(0,1/2).

## Formal resolution and negative test

Each output is coherent with mean μ/N and independent Poisson counts. Obtain exact threshold from (1−e^{-μ/N})^N≥1−ε; distinguish this from arbitrary active copying schemes.
