# GC284: Worst-case error of a depth-D optical arithmetic cascade

**Photonic mathematical specification v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC284](../problems/12-photonic-wave-computing/GC284.md). Precise models do not automatically constitute physical implementation or a new open result.

## Exact device/logic model and quantified claim

Let intended real signals evolve by x_{j+1}=f_j(x_j), where all f_j are L-Lipschitz on [−2,2]. Let physical stages obey \(\tilde x_{j+1}=f_j(\tilde x_j)+\eta_j\), \(|\eta_j|\le\delta\), with both trajectories in [−2,2]. Determine the sharp uniform bound on \(|\tilde x_D-x_D|\) for every D≥1 and fixed initial discrepancy ε0, and construct f_j/η_j attaining the bound before saturation.

## Formal resolution and negative test

Prove ε_D≤L^Dε0+δΣ_{j=0}^{D−1}L^j, including L=1. The nonlinear device properties must justify a chosen Lipschitz L.

## Readiness gate

Audit detector thresholds, input/output encoding, amplification and nonlinear readout, worst-case or stochastic errors, units and robustness. Separate mathematical circuit semantics from hardware measurements and novelty.
