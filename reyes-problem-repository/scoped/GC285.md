# GC285: Robustness of a finite interferometer to phase offsets

**Photonic mathematical specification v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC285](../problems/12-photonic-wave-computing/GC285.md). Precise models do not automatically constitute physical implementation or a new open result.

## Exact device/logic model and quantified claim

For m≥2 define an optical circuit U(φ)=B_mD(φ_m)B_{m−1}⋯B_1D(φ_1), with fixed unitary B_j∈U(2), D(θ)=diag(1,e^{iθ}), and φ∈R^m. Determine the optimal constant C_m such that \(\|U(\phi+\delta)-U(\phi)\|_{op}\le C_m\|\delta\|_\infty\) for all φ,δ and all beam splitters, and construct worst-case configurations.

## Formal resolution and negative test

The norm and circuit architecture are specified. Telescoping products give C_m≤m; establishing sharpness requires correlated phase sensitivities.

## Readiness gate

Audit detector thresholds, input/output encoding, amplification and nonlinear readout, worst-case or stochastic errors, units and robustness. Separate mathematical circuit semantics from hardware measurements and novelty.
