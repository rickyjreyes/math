# GC285: Unitary-mesh sensitivity to fabrication phase offsets

**Device-level mathematical candidate v1, 2026-10-30.** [Original GC285](../problems/12-photonic-wave-computing/GC285.md). The device model is idealized and **not** an experimentally validated optical implementation.

## Explicit physical abstraction and quantified mathematical target

For U,V∈U(M) and programmable phases φ∈R^M, define H(φ)=U diag(e^{iφ₁},…,e^{iφ_M}) V. Given fabrication offsets |δ_j|≤ε≤π, determine the exact worst-case operator norm sup_{|δ_j|≤ε}||H(φ+δ)−H(φ)||_{2→2}, and extend to a cascade of L such meshes with independent offsets.

## Proof or counterexample acceptance

Unitary invariance reduces the single-layer result to 2 sin(ε/2). A telescoping product gives a sufficient L-dependent bound; exhibit aligned cases or qualify any claimed sharpness.

## Scientific and formalization boundary

Prove all logical implications for the stated inputs, component transfer functions, limits and error models. Note any already elementary result rather than declaring a novel open conjecture. A mathematical truth table does not certify fabricated hardware; any Lean statement must match this model exactly.
