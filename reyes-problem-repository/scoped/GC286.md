# GC286: Complete system-energy break-even criterion

**Device-level mathematical candidate v1, 2026-10-30.** [Original GC286](../problems/12-photonic-wave-computing/GC286.md). The device model is idealized and **not** an experimentally validated optical implementation.

## Explicit physical abstraction and quantified mathematical target

For one completed N-gate logical operation, define measured source energy E_src, modulator/control energy E_mod, detector energy E_det, cooling energy E_cool, memory energy E_mem and idle baseline E_idle, all nonnegative joules per completed operation. Let E_opt=sum of these six quantities, and choose an electronic reference performing the **same input/output function** with measured energy E_ref>0. Prove that optical energy advantage holds iff E_src+E_mod+E_det+E_cool+E_mem+E_idle<E_ref; when E_src=N_ph hν/η_wall, derive the maximal permitted N_ph as a function of other terms and efficiency 0<η_wall≤1.

## Proof or counterexample acceptance

This is an accounting/experimental protocol, not an open universal mathematical law. Performance numbers require actual measurements and equal workload, error tolerance and clock rate; no favorable crossover is assumed.

## Scientific and formalization boundary

Prove all logical implications for the stated inputs, component transfer functions, limits and error models. Note any already elementary result rather than declaring a novel open conjecture. A mathematical truth table does not certify fabricated hardware; any Lean statement must match this model exactly.
