# GC286: End-to-end photonic arithmetic energy comparison

**Photonic mathematical specification v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC286](../problems/12-photonic-wave-computing/GC286.md). Precise models do not automatically constitute physical implementation or a new open result.

## Exact device/logic model and quantified claim

For one operation, define \(E_{opt}=E_{source}+E_{mod}+E_{det}+E_{mem}+E_{ctrl}\) and \(E_{elec}=E_{ALU}+E_{memory}+E_{io}\), with all components nonnegative joules per identical accepted output operation and error tolerance p≤10^-9. Prove the exact crossover criterion \(E_{opt}<E_{elec}\), and derive the photon-budget lower bound \(E_{source}\ge Nhc/\lambda\) for N launched photons at wavelength λ. Determine what additional measured quantities are necessary for a nontrivial numerical comparison.

## Formal resolution and negative test

This is measurement/accounting engineering, not an unsolved mathematical theorem. No optical efficiency superiority follows from comparing optical core energy against full electronic system costs.

## Readiness gate

Audit detector thresholds, input/output encoding, amplification and nonlinear readout, worst-case or stochastic errors, units and robustness. Separate mathematical circuit semantics from hardware measurements and novelty.
