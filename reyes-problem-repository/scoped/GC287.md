# GC287: Single-wavelength attractor of two coupled laser modes

**Photonic mathematical specification v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC287](../problems/12-photonic-wave-computing/GC287.md). Precise models do not automatically constitute physical implementation or a new open result.

## Exact device/logic model and quantified claim

For \((a,b)\in\mathbb C^2\), consider \(\dot a=(p-|a|²-\chi|b|²)a\), \(\dot b=(p-|b|²-\chi|a|²)b\), p=1, χ∈[0,3]. Classify all stationary intensities and their orbital stability modulo independent phase rotations. Determine for which χ the only asymptotically stable nonzero intensity equilibria are single-mode states (1,0) and (0,1), versus a coexistence state.

## Formal resolution and negative test

The model includes permutation symmetry, so a globally **unique** selected wavelength cannot be proved without an external asymmetry; give the correct bistability or coexistence classification.
