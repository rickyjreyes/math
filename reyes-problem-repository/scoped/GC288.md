# GC288: Feedback calibration under bounded phase drift

**Photonic mathematical specification v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC288](../problems/12-photonic-wave-computing/GC288.md). Precise models do not automatically constitute physical implementation or a new open result.

## Exact device/logic model and quantified claim

Let phase error satisfy \(e_{k+1}=e_k+w_k-Ke_k\), with |w_k|≤d and 0<K<2. For an XOR gate with worst-case tolerance |e_k|≤ε from GC277, determine necessary and sufficient conditions on (K,d,ε) so the invariant interval [−ε,ε] is forward invariant for every drift sequence.

## Formal resolution and negative test

The exact condition is |1−K|ε+d≤ε; combine this with the optical truth-table margin, rather than claiming stability from finite numerical runs.

## Readiness gate

Audit detector thresholds, input/output encoding, amplification and nonlinear readout, worst-case or stochastic errors, units and robustness. Separate mathematical circuit semantics from hardware measurements and novelty.
