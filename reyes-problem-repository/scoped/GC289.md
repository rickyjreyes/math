# GC289: Sampling rate necessary for bounded optical phase drift

**Device-level mathematical candidate v1, 2026-10-30.** [Original GC289](../problems/12-photonic-wave-computing/GC289.md). The device model is idealized and **not** an experimentally validated optical implementation.

## Explicit physical abstraction and quantified mathematical target

Let a phase error e(t) satisfy |de/dt|≤v almost everywhere between ideal instantaneous reset operations at times nΔ, where e(nΔ)=0, v>0 and Δ>0. Correctness of a phase-coded detector requires |e(t)|<ε for all t, with ε∈(0,π/4). Determine the necessary and sufficient maximum Δ under arbitrary adversarial drift, hence the minimum feedback sample rate 1/Δ.

## Proof or counterexample acceptance

The maximum within one interval is vΔ. Exact noiseless resetting is a stated idealization; if noisy sensors, actuator delay or limited correction are permitted, the bound must be modified in a separate model.
