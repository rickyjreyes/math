# GC289: Minimum feedback sampling rate against phase drift

**Photonic mathematical specification v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC289](../problems/12-photonic-wave-computing/GC289.md). Precise models do not automatically constitute physical implementation or a new open result.

## Exact device/logic model and quantified claim

A controlled phase follows \(\dot\theta=w(t)\) between sample-and-reset events at times kT_s, with |w(t)|≤W, W>0. At each sample the controller can reset θ exactly to zero. For tolerance |θ|≤ε at all times and every measurable disturbance, determine largest permissible sampling period T_s and minimal rate 1/T_s.

## Formal resolution and negative test

The exact worst-case drift is WT_s, giving T_s≤ε/W. Any more realistic noisy, delayed, or bandwidth-limited controller needs its own model.
