# GC290: Output cardinality of a photon-limited passive network

**Photonic mathematical specification v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC290](../problems/12-photonic-wave-computing/GC290.md). Precise models do not automatically constitute physical implementation or a new open result.

## Exact device/logic model and quantified claim

Let input coherent amplitude z∈\mathbb C^M obey \(\|z\|_2²≤P\), and U∈U(M) be passive. Each output intensity |(Uz)_j|² is quantized into bins [0,δ),[δ,2δ),… for δ>0, yielding q(z)∈N^M. Determine the maximum number of distinct quantized patterns as z varies over the power ball and U varies over unitaries, and give upper/lower bounds in M and P/δ.

## Formal resolution and negative test

Because U is unitary, output nonnegative intensities sum ≤P, so admissible bin indices satisfy Σq_j≤floor(P/δ). Prove each such pattern realizable up to bin boundaries.

Audit detector thresholds, input/output encoding, amplification and nonlinear readout, worst-case or stochastic errors, units and robustness. Separate mathematical circuit semantics from hardware measurements and novelty.
