# GC291: Curved-path tracking of a nonlinear optical pulse

**Device-level mathematical candidate v1, 2026-10-30.** [Original GC291](../problems/12-photonic-wave-computing/GC291.md). The device model is idealized and **not** an experimentally validated optical implementation.

## Explicit physical abstraction and quantified mathematical target

For propagation distance z∈[0,L] and transverse (x,y)∈R², let i∂_z u=−(1/2)Δu+V(x,y)u−g|u|²u, where V(x,y)=Ω²(y−κx²)²/2, Ω=2, κ=1/10 and g∈[0,1]. Initialize a normalized Gaussian u(0,x,y)=C exp(−(x+2)²−(y−0.4)²) exp(2ix). Define beam center X(z)=∫x|u|²/M and Y(z)=∫y|u|²/M. Determine whether sup_{z∈[0,1]}|Y(z)−κX(z)²|≤1/10 for every g∈[0,1], or give one exact counterexample.

## Proof or counterexample acceptance

A curved refractive channel is explicit and the success metric is a measurable center-of-mass inequality. Verify well-posedness/moment evolution and do not substitute ray tracing for the full PDE estimate.

## Scientific and formalization boundary

Prove all logical implications for the stated inputs, component transfer functions, limits and error models. Note any already elementary result rather than declaring a novel open conjecture. A mathematical truth table does not certify fabricated hardware; any Lean statement must match this model exactly.
