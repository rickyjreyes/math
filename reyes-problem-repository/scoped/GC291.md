# GC291: Curved-ray tracking in a refractive potential

**Photonic mathematical specification v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC291](../problems/12-photonic-wave-computing/GC291.md). Precise models do not automatically constitute physical implementation or a new open result.

## Exact device/logic model and quantified claim

Consider \(i u_t=-\Delta u+V(x)u-|u|²u\) on R² with \(V(x)=\tfrac12\Omega²|x|²\), Ω>0, and initial data a translated smooth ground state localized near x0 with phase \(e^{ip_0\cdot x}\). Define mass center \(X(t)=M(u)^{-1}\int x|u|²dx\). Prove X''(t)=−2Ω²X(t) under sufficient moment regularity and determine the exact center trajectory and perturbation sensitivity.

## Formal resolution and negative test

This harmonic potential yields an exact Ehrenfest center law rather than an assertion that a pulse travels an arbitrary curved engineered path; shape stability is separate.
