# GC293: Soliton collision as a Boolean switching gate

**Photonic mathematical specification v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC293](../problems/12-photonic-wave-computing/GC293.md). Precise models do not automatically constitute physical implementation or a new open result.

## Exact device/logic model and quantified claim

For one-dimensional focusing cubic NLS \(iu_t=-u_{xx}-2|u|²u\), define inputs b1,b2∈{0,1} by initial data \(u(0,x)=b_1\operatorname{sech}(x+R)e^{ivx}+b_2\operatorname{sech}(x-R)e^{-ivx}\), R=10,v=1. Decode bit 1 iff \(\int_{-1}^{1}|u(T,x)|²dx≥1/2\) for T=10. Determine the exact four-input truth table and any perturbation tolerance in H¹ guaranteeing the same decoding.

## Formal resolution and negative test

The full continuum dynamics and detector region are frozen. Numerically plausible switching is not a certified gate unless strict output margins and well-posedness bounds are proved.
