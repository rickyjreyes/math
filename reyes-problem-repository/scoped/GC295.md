# GC295: Typed phase-logic compiler correctness

**Photonic mathematical specification v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC295](../problems/12-photonic-wave-computing/GC295.md). Precise models do not automatically constitute physical implementation or a new open result.

## Exact device/logic model and quantified claim

Define source Boolean language generated by variables, XOR, AND and NOT. Compile each gate into ideal real-valued detector functions \(D_{XOR}(a,b)=(1−\cos(\pi(a−b)))/2\), \(D_{AND}(a,b)=\sin²(\pi a/2)\sin²(\pi b/2)\), \(D_{NOT}(a)=1−a\), thresholding at 1/2 (with AND output 1 at exactly 1). Prove by structural induction that every compiled circuit outputs the source Boolean semantics on bits.

## Formal resolution and negative test

This is an idealized detector-level semantics proof, not a passive physical implementation; compile/runtime fidelity and finite precision are separate proof obligations.

## Readiness gate

Audit detector thresholds, input/output encoding, amplification and nonlinear readout, worst-case or stochastic errors, units and robustness. Separate mathematical circuit semantics from hardware measurements and novelty.
