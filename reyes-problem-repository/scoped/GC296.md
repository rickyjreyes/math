# GC296: Interval certificate for a fabricated XOR gate

**Photonic mathematical specification v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC296](../problems/12-photonic-wave-computing/GC296.md). Precise models do not automatically constitute physical implementation or a new open result.

## Exact device/logic model and quantified claim

For encoded bits a,b∈{0,1}, measured phase intervals \(\Theta_a=[\pi a−\varepsilon,\pi a+\varepsilon]\) and \(\Theta_b=[\pi b−\varepsilon,\pi b+\varepsilon]\), define S=(1−cos(θa−θb))/2 and detector error η∈[−ν,ν]. Construct a rational interval checker proving S+η<1/2 on equal inputs and S+η≥1/2 on unequal inputs whenever \(\cos(2\varepsilon)>2\nu\), for \(0≤\varepsilon≤\pi/4\).

## Formal resolution and negative test

Soundness needs enclosure of π, cos and measured phase intervals with certified arithmetic. The inequalities are sufficient, not automatically optimal outside the stated error ranges.

## Readiness gate

Audit detector thresholds, input/output encoding, amplification and nonlinear readout, worst-case or stochastic errors, units and robustness. Separate mathematical circuit semantics from hardware measurements and novelty.
