# GC297: Waveguide spacing versus cross-talk error

**Photonic mathematical specification v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC297](../problems/12-photonic-wave-computing/GC297.md). Precise models do not automatically constitute physical implementation or a new open result.

## Exact device/logic model and quantified claim

For two weakly coupled equal waveguides of propagation length L>0, let amplitudes solve \(i d/dz(a,b)^T=\begin{pmatrix}0&\kappa(d)\\\kappa(d)&0\end{pmatrix}(a,b)^T\), κ(d)=κ0e^{-d/ℓ}, κ0,ℓ>0, with input (a,b)=(1,0). Define cross-talk probability P(d)=|b(L)|². Determine all spacings d≥0 with P(d)≤ε for fixed 0<ε<1/2, and the least d in the weak-coupling regime κ(d)L≤π/2.

## Formal resolution and negative test

The exact solution is P(d)=sin²(κ0Le^{-d/ℓ}); outside the monotone regime interference creates additional acceptable spacing intervals.
