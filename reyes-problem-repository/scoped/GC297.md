# GC297: Spacing bound for two coupled photonic waveguides

**Device-level mathematical candidate v1, 2026-10-30.** [Original GC297](../problems/12-photonic-wave-computing/GC297.md). The device model is idealized and **not** an experimentally validated optical implementation.

## Explicit physical abstraction and quantified mathematical target

For two identical parallel waveguides at separation d>0, let amplitudes obey ż=i[[0,κ(d)],[κ(d),0]]z for propagation z∈[0,L] with unit amplitude initially in guide 1, κ(d)=κ₀exp(−d/ℓ), κ₀,ℓ,L>0. Restrict designs to κ(d)L≤π/2 (first transfer lobe). Determine the smallest d giving output cross-talk |z₂(L)|²≤p for p∈(0,1/2), and the corresponding maximum linear packing density 1/d.

## Proof or counterexample acceptance

The exact cross-talk is sin²(κ(d)L). The first-lobe restriction prevents spurious high-coupling revival points; a multi-waveguide array needs a different coupling matrix.
