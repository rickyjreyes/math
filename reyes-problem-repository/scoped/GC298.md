# GC298: Phase capture of two drifting oscillators

**Photonic mathematical specification v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC298](../problems/12-photonic-wave-computing/GC298.md). Precise models do not automatically constitute physical implementation or a new open result.

## Exact device/logic model and quantified claim

For phase difference φ∈R/2πZ, use the Adler equation \(\dot\phi=\Delta\omega−K\sin\phi+w(t)\), K>0, |w(t)|≤d. Determine for which |Δω|,d,K there exists a forward-invariant arc \(|\phi−\phi_*|≤r<\pi/2\) containing a stable locked phase φ*, and prove a perturbation bound valid for every measurable w.

## Formal resolution and negative test

A nominal fixed point requires |Δω|<K, but robust invariant arcs depend on drift margin. Derive a boundary-vector-field condition, not just the nominal condition.

## Readiness gate

Audit detector thresholds, input/output encoding, amplification and nonlinear readout, worst-case or stochastic errors, units and robustness. Separate mathematical circuit semantics from hardware measurements and novelty.
