# GC299: Minimum reset work and photon dissipation

**Photonic mathematical specification v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC299](../problems/12-photonic-wave-computing/GC299.md). Precise models do not automatically constitute physical implementation or a new open result.

## Exact device/logic model and quantified claim

For a two-state optical-memory bit in thermal equilibrium with degenerate logical energies, initial distribution (1/2,1/2), bath temperature T_b and reset error p∈[0,1/2), consider isothermal protocols with endpoint distribution (1−p,p). Compute the minimum reversible mean work from free-energy change, and compare with any assumed radiative photon-loss term N_loss hc/λ under an explicit independently specified optical-loss mechanism.

## Formal resolution and negative test

The entropic reset-work lower bound is kBT_b(ln2−h(p)) using natural-log binary entropy h. Thermodynamic work and photon loss are distinct observables; no universal photon-number lower bound follows without a coupling model.
