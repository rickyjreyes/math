# GC300: End-to-end verified two-bit optical half-adder

**Photonic mathematical specification v1 (2026-10-30).** Original: [GC300](../problems/12-photonic-wave-computing/GC300.md). Precise models do not automatically constitute physical implementation or a new open result.

## Exact device/logic model and quantified claim

Define Boolean half-adder H(a,b)=(a XOR b,a AND b) for a,b∈{0,1}. Compile the source into the ideal XOR and AND phase detector functions in GC295, with phases θ_a=πa, θ_b=πb, detector thresholds 1/2, and bounded phase errors |δ_a|,|δ_b|≤ε plus detector errors |η_X|,|η_A|≤ν. Prove the exact zero-error truth table and derive the maximal region of (ε,ν) for which every allowed physical error preserves both output bits; also show a verified interval checker accepts all safe parameter intervals and rejects unsafe ones without false acceptance.

## Formal resolution and negative test

This is a fully typed compiler-to-detection claim under explicit idealized assumptions. A hardware claim needs measured calibration certificates and independent assessment of detector noise, optical losses and nonlinear resources.

## Readiness gate

Audit detector thresholds, input/output encoding, amplification and nonlinear readout, worst-case or stochastic errors, units and robustness. Separate mathematical circuit semantics from hardware measurements and novelty.
