# GC69: Hamiltonian spectral instability index

**Version:** scoped mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30). **Readiness:** precise candidate, not literature-certified open, not proven, and not claimed Lean-verified. Original draft: [GC69](../problems/01-wave-dynamics/GC69.md). Shared definitions: [COMMON_MODELS](COMMON_MODELS.md).

## Exact mathematical target

For every real nonzero stationary \(\phi\in H^4\) of W with finite-dimensional negative eigenspaces of \(L_+\) and \(L_-\), and with \(0\) isolated whenever a kernel exists, determine the unstable eigenvalue count \(n_+(\mathcal A_\phi)=\sum_{\operatorname{Re}\lambda>0}\operatorname{algmult}(\lambda)\). Identify an exact formula or inequality in terms of the Morse index of \(\operatorname{diag}(L_+,L_-)\) and constrained neutral directions.

## What answers it

A purported index identity must state treatment of purely imaginary eigenvalues with negative Krein signature, essential spectrum and zero modes. A counterexample within this quantified class refutes an unrestricted equality.
