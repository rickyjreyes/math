# GC71: Dynamical fission versus mass-energy

**Version:** scoped mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30). **Readiness:** precise candidate, not literature-certified open, not proven, and not claimed Lean-verified. Original draft: [GC71](../problems/01-wave-dynamics/GC71.md). Shared definitions: [COMMON_MODELS](COMMON_MODELS.md).

## Exact mathematical target

For W, call a global \(H^2\) orbit a **two-profile fission orbit** if there are \(\phi_1,\phi_2\in H^4\setminus\{0\}\) and continuous centers \(a_j(t)\) with \(|a_1(t)-a_2(t)|\to\infty\), such that \(\|u(t)-\sum_{j=1}^2 e^{i\theta_j(t)}\phi_j(\cdot-a_j(t))\|_{H^2}\to0\) as \(t\to\infty\). Determine whether two initial data can have equal \((M,E_W)\) but different fission outcomes, with one fission orbit and one non-fission orbit.

## What answers it

An affirmative example proves conserved mass and energy alone cannot universally predict splitting. A negative theorem must quantify over all global H2 orbits and the stated decomposition notion.
