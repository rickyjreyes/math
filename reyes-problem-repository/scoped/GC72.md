# GC72: Radiation tail asymptotics

**Version:** scoped mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30). **Readiness:** precise candidate, not literature-certified open, not proven, and not claimed Lean-verified. Original draft: [GC72](../problems/01-wave-dynamics/GC72.md). Shared definitions: [COMMON_MODELS](COMMON_MODELS.md).

## Exact mathematical target

Let \(\phi\) be any real stationary \(H^4\) profile of W with \(\mathcal A_\phi\) having no nonzero imaginary eigenvalue and no embedded eigenvalue. Let \(\Pi_c\) be the continuous spectral projection, if defined, and \(w(t)=e^{t\mathcal A_\phi}\Pi_c w_0\). For \(w_0\in H^2\cap L^1\) lying in the projection domain, classify exponents \(\alpha\ge0\) such that \(\|w(t)\|_{L^\infty}\le C_\phi(1+t)^{-\alpha}\|w_0\|_{H^2\cap L^1}\) for all \(t\ge0\).

## What answers it

A complete answer records when the requisite projection exists, the continuous-spectrum thresholds and the supremal alpha. A single threshold counterexample disproves a proposed uniform exponent.
