## Exact mathematical target

Replace W by \(i u_t=((\Delta+1)^2-e^{-|x|^2})u-|u|^2u+|u|^4u\). Use the explicit incoming family \(u_{A,p,R}(0,x)=A\exp(-|x+Re_1|^2)\exp(ipx_1)\) with \(A,p,R>0\). For each triple with a global \(H^2\) solution define the captured mass \(C=\limsup_{t\to\infty}\int_{|x|\le2}|u(t,x)|^2dx\). Determine exactly which triples satisfy \(C>0\), and whether any satisfy \(\liminf_{t\to\infty}\int_{|x|\le2}|u|^2>0\).
