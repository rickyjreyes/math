# GC75: Phase-dependent effective interaction

**Version:** scoped mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30). **Readiness:** precise candidate, not literature-certified open, not proven, and not claimed Lean-verified. Original draft: [GC75](../problems/01-wave-dynamics/GC75.md). Shared definitions: [COMMON_MODELS](COMMON_MODELS.md).

## Exact mathematical target

For real \(\phi\in H^4\) stationary for W with \(e^{|x|/10}\phi\in H^4\), take \(U_{R,\theta}(x)=\phi(x-Re_1)+e^{i\theta}\phi(x+Re_1)\). Define interaction \(I(R,\theta)=E_W(U_{R,\theta})-2E_W(\phi)\) and force \(F(R,\theta)=-\partial_R I(R,\theta)\), with \(R>1\) and \(\theta\in[0,2\pi)\). Determine the uniform-in-phase leading asymptotic term as \(R\to\infty\) and its sharp remainder.

## What answers it

The derivative is of a precisely defined variational interaction, not an unproved physical acceleration law. First prove existence of a nonzero exponentially localized phi; zeros of the leading coefficient must be treated separately.
