# GC76: Embedded-mode persistence

**Version:** scoped mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30). **Readiness:** precise candidate, not literature-certified open, not proven, and not claimed Lean-verified. Original draft: [GC76](../problems/01-wave-dynamics/GC76.md). Shared definitions: [COMMON_MODELS](COMMON_MODELS.md).

## Exact mathematical target

Let \(V,W\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R^2;\mathbb R)\) and \(H_\varepsilon=(\Delta+1)^2+V+\varepsilon W\) on \(H^4\subset L^2\). Determine whether there exist \(V,W\), a positive embedded eigenvalue \(\lambda_0>0\) of \(H_0\), and \(\delta>0\) such that every \(|\varepsilon|<\delta\) has an \(L^2\) eigenvalue \(\lambda_\varepsilon\to\lambda_0\). If no compactly supported potential admits the starting eigenvalue, prove that stronger obstruction.

## What answers it

The perturbation class and operator are fixed, but no embedded eigenvalue is presupposed. Distinguish eigenvalue from a resonance pole; specify its analytic continuation if the latter is studied.
