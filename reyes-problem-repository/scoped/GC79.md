# GC79: Genuine three-body energy term

**Version:** scoped mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30). **Readiness:** precise candidate, not literature-certified open, not proven, and not claimed Lean-verified. Original draft: [GC79](../problems/01-wave-dynamics/GC79.md). Shared definitions: [COMMON_MODELS](COMMON_MODELS.md).

## Exact mathematical target

For any real exponentially localized stationary \(\phi\) of W, set \(\phi_j(x)=e^{i\theta_j}\phi(x-a_j)\). Define the irreducible three-body energy \(I_3=E_W(\phi_1+\phi_2+\phi_3)-\sum_{i<j}E_W(\phi_i+\phi_j)+\sum_{i=1}^3E_W(\phi_i)\). For \(|a_i-a_j|\ge R\) and \(\theta_j\in[0,2\pi)\), determine the optimal uniform decay of \(\sup|I_3|\) as \(R\to\infty\), and whether it is asymptotically smaller than all nonzero pair interaction terms.

## What answers it

This exact static inclusion–exclusion identity removes ambiguous 'not captured by pairs'; dynamical three-body forces require an additional modulation theorem.
