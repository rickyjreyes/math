# GC80: Periodic-orbit persistence under defects

**Version:** scoped mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30). **Readiness:** precise candidate, not literature-certified open, not proven, and not claimed Lean-verified. Original draft: [GC80](../problems/01-wave-dynamics/GC80.md). Shared definitions: [COMMON_MODELS](COMMON_MODELS.md).

## Exact mathematical target

Consider \(i u_t=((\Delta+1)^2+\varepsilon e^{-|x|^2})u-|u|^2u+|u|^4u\) on \(H^2(\mathbb R^2)\). A breather is a non-standing solution with \(e^{i\omega T}u(T)=u(0)\) for some \(T>0,\omega\in\mathbb R\), and \(u(t)\in H^4\) uniformly for \(t\in[0,T]\). Determine whether there exists an unperturbed breather with a nearby \(H^2\)-continuous branch of breathers for all sufficiently small \(\varepsilon\).

## What answers it

State a local slice removing phase/time symmetries when proving persistence. The unperturbed breather must itself be constructed, not presumed.
