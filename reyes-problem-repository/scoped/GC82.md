# GC82: Coercivity of regularized phase curvature

**Version:** concrete mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30). **Status:** mathematically proposed, not prior-art-certified or proved. Shared definitions: [COMMON_MODELS](COMMON_MODELS.md). Historical candidate: [GC82](../problems/02-confinement-curvature/GC82.md).

## Exact question

For \(m>0\) and \(\alpha\ge0\), define \(I_\alpha(m)=\inf\{E_{\alpha,1}(u):u\in H^2(\mathbb R^2;\mathbb C),\ M(u)=m\}\) using K and W. Determine whether for every fixed \(m>0\) and \(\alpha>0\) there are \(c_{m,\alpha}>0,C_{m,\alpha}<\infty\) such that \(E_{\alpha,1}(u)\ge c_{m,\alpha}\|u\|_{H^2}^2-C_{m,\alpha}\) for all admissible \(u\).

## Proof or counterexample acceptance

Either establish the displayed inequality, including the \(|\xi|=1\) dispersion shell, or construct fixed-mass \(H^2\) sequences with divergent norm and subquadratic energy.
