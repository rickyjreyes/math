# GC83: Phase lifting in the presence of nodal defects

**Version:** concrete mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30). **Status:** mathematically proposed, not prior-art-certified or proved. Shared definitions: [COMMON_MODELS](COMMON_MODELS.md). Historical candidate: [GC83](../problems/02-confinement-curvature/GC83.md).

## Exact question

Let \(A=\{x\in\mathbb R^2:1<|x|<2\}\). For \(u\in C^2(\overline A;\mathbb C)\) with \(|u|\ge c>0\), call a global phase a \(\theta\in C^1(A;\mathbb R)\) satisfying \(u=|u|e^{i\theta}\). Characterize existence of \(\theta\) by the integer period \(n(u)=(2\pi)^{-1}\int_{|x|=3/2}\operatorname{Im}(\bar u\nabla u)|u|^{-2}\cdot d\ell\). Determine whether an \(L^2(A)\) bound on \(K_1(u)\) forces \(n(u)=0\).

## Proof or counterexample acceptance

Prove the lifting equivalence and test \(u=e^{i\arg x}\), for which a smooth global argument fails while the divergence-based regularized curvature is zero. Treat this as a topology/counterexample question, not presumed novel.
