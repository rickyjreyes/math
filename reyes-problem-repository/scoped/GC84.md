# GC84: revised mathematically explicit candidate

**Definition revision v1.1 (2026-10-30).** Earlier scoping draft corrected for internal domain or topology inconsistency. Original candidate: [GC84](../problems/02-confinement-curvature/GC84.md).

## Exact proposed target

Let \(\mathcal U=\{u\in C^\infty(\mathbb R^2;\mathbb C):u(x)=1\text{ for }|x|\ge3,\ u\text{ has finitely many isolated simple zeros in }B_3\}\). For \(u\in\mathcal U\), define \(J_\varepsilon(u)=\operatorname{Im}(\bar u\nabla u)/(\varepsilon+|u|^2)\) and \(K_\varepsilon=\operatorname{div}J_\varepsilon\). Determine whether \(J_\varepsilon\to J_0\) in \(L^1_{loc}\) and \(K_\varepsilon\to\operatorname{div}J_0\) in \(\mathcal D'\); independently compute \(\operatorname{curl}J_0\) as a distribution and its point masses at each isolated zero.

## Acceptance and outstanding verification

The nonvanishing constant exterior fixes the earlier incompatibility between compact support and finitely many isolated zeros. Distinguish divergence from curl: vortex winding contributes to curl, not automatically to divergence. Prove local integrability at simple zeros and test renormalization where necessary.
