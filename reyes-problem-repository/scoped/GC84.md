# GC84: Zero-regularizer vortex limit

**Version:** concrete mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30). **Status:** mathematically proposed, not prior-art-certified or proved. Shared definitions: [COMMON_MODELS](COMMON_MODELS.md). Historical candidate: [GC84](../problems/02-confinement-curvature/GC84.md).

## Exact question

Let \(\mathcal U=\{u\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R^2;\mathbb C):u\text{ has finitely many isolated simple zeros}\}\). For \(u\in\mathcal U\), define \(J_\varepsilon(u)=j(u)/(\varepsilon+|u|^2)\) and \(K_\varepsilon=\operatorname{div}J_\varepsilon\). Determine whether \(J_\varepsilon\to J_0\) in \(L^1_{\mathrm{loc}}\) and \(K_\varepsilon\to\operatorname{div}J_0\) in \(\mathcal D'\); independently classify distributional \(\operatorname{curl}J_0\) at each zero.

## Proof or counterexample acceptance

Explicitly compare divergence and curl; the vortex point charge is a curl effect and must not be asserted as a delta in divergence without a calculation. Note that compact support creates non-isolated zeros outside its support; interpret simple zeros only inside the nonzero region or replace C_c∞ by nonvanishing-at-infinity fields.
