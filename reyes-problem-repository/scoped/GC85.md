# GC85: Geometry dependence of constrained minimizers

**Version:** concrete mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30). **Status:** mathematically proposed, not prior-art-certified or proved. Shared definitions: [COMMON_MODELS](COMMON_MODELS.md). Historical candidate: [GC85](../problems/02-confinement-curvature/GC85.md).

## Exact question

Let \((M_1,g_1)\) be the unit round \(S^2\), and \((M_2,g_2)\) the flat square torus of area \(4\pi\). For \(j=1,2\) define \(E_j(u)=\int_{M_j}(|(\Delta_{g_j}+1)u|^2-\tfrac12|u|^4+\tfrac13|u|^6)\,dV_{g_j}\), \(u\in H^2(M_j;\mathbb C)\). For every mass \(m>0\) define \(I_j(m)=\inf\{E_j(u):\|u\|_2^2=m\}\). Determine whether \(I_1(m)=I_2(m)\), and for which \(m\) each minimizing orbit is spatially constant.

## Proof or counterexample acceptance

The comparison involves exact equal-area manifolds, avoids undefined 'localization on a compact surface', and admits a proof or one explicit mass separating the infima.
