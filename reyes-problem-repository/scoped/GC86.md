# GC86: Anisotropic mass-threshold dependence

**Version:** concrete mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30). **Status:** mathematically proposed, not prior-art-certified or proved. Shared definitions: [COMMON_MODELS](COMMON_MODELS.md). Historical candidate: [GC86](../problems/02-confinement-curvature/GC86.md).

## Exact question

For \(0\le\varepsilon\le1/4\), let \(D_\varepsilon\) be the self-adjoint Fourier multiplier on \(L^2(\mathbb R^2)\) with symbol \(p_\varepsilon(\xi)=(|\xi|^2-1)^2+\varepsilon(\xi_1^2-\xi_2^2)^2\). On \(H^2\) define \(E_\varepsilon(u)=\langle u,D_\varepsilon u\rangle-\tfrac12\|u\|_4^4+\tfrac13\|u\|_6^6\) and \(m_*(\varepsilon)=\inf\{m>0:I_\varepsilon(m)\text{ is attained}\}\) with \(\inf\emptyset=\infty\). Determine continuity or discontinuity of \(m_*\) at \(\varepsilon=0\).

## Proof or counterexample acceptance

Prove existence of minimizers at masses used to define a finite threshold, or exhibit nonattainment; do not infer a threshold shift from the linear symbol alone.
