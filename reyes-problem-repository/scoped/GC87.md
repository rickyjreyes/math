# GC87: Bound states from a deformed waveguide

**Version:** concrete mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30). **Status:** mathematically proposed, not prior-art-certified or proved. Shared definitions: [COMMON_MODELS](COMMON_MODELS.md). Historical candidate: [GC87](../problems/02-confinement-curvature/GC87.md).

## Exact question

For \(f\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R)\) with \(f\ge0\) and \(\|f\|_\infty=1\), take \(\Omega_{\delta,f}=\{(x,y):0<y<1+\delta f(x)\}\) for \(0<\delta<1/4\). Let \(L_{\delta,f}=(\Delta+1)^2\) be the Friedrichs operator defined by the closed quadratic form \(q(u)=\| (\Delta+1)u\|_2^2\) on \(H_0^2(\Omega_{\delta,f})\). Determine for which \((\delta,f)\) there is an eigenvalue strictly below \(\inf\sigma_{\rm ess}(L_{\delta,f})\).

## Proof or counterexample acceptance

Prove the essential threshold for the asymptotically straight guide, and an eigenfunction or a no-eigenvalue theorem. The chosen form fixes the fourth-order boundary interpretation.
