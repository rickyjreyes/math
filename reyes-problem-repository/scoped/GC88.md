# GC88: Persistence under compact metric perturbations

**Version:** concrete mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30). **Status:** mathematically proposed, not prior-art-certified or proved. Shared definitions: [COMMON_MODELS](COMMON_MODELS.md). Historical candidate: [GC88](../problems/02-confinement-curvature/GC88.md).

## Exact question

For any real symmetric \(h\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R^2;\mathbb R^{2\times2})\) with \(\|h\|_{C^2}\le1\), take \(g_\varepsilon=I+\varepsilon h\), \(|\varepsilon|<1/4\). With \(H^2(g_\varepsilon)\) and fixed Riemannian mass \(M_\varepsilon(u)=\int|u|^2dV_{g_\varepsilon}=m\), minimize \(E_\varepsilon(u)=\int(|(\Delta_{g_\varepsilon}+1)u|^2-\tfrac12|u|^4+\tfrac13|u|^6)dV_{g_\varepsilon}\). Determine for each m whether minimizers exist for small \(\varepsilon\) and converge, modulo translations, as \(\varepsilon\to0\).

## Proof or counterexample acceptance

Prove tightness/compactness or give escaping-minimizer sequences; the perturbation breaks translation symmetry, so quotienting requires explicit center shifts.
