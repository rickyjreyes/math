# GC90: Quantile concentration radius of minimizers

**Version:** concrete mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30). **Status:** mathematically proposed, not prior-art-certified or proved. Shared definitions: [COMMON_MODELS](COMMON_MODELS.md). Historical candidate: [GC90](../problems/02-confinement-curvature/GC90.md).

## Exact question

For \(u\in L^2(\mathbb R^2)\), \(M(u)=m>0\), set \(r_{9/10}(u)=\inf\{r>0:\exists a\in\mathbb R^2,\ \int_{B_r(a)}|u|^2\ge0.9m\}\). Let \(\mathcal M_m=\{u\in H^2:M(u)=m,\ E_W(u)=I_0(m)\}\). For masses \(m\) with \(\mathcal M_m\ne\emptyset\), determine whether \(0<\inf_{u\in\mathcal M_m}r_{9/10}(u)\le\sup_{u\in\mathcal M_m}r_{9/10}(u)<\infty\), with explicit estimates in m.

## Proof or counterexample acceptance

Do not posit minimizers where they have not been established; failure at either endpoint is a valid counterexample.
