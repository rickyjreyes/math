# GC92: Invariant closure class under a conservation law

**Version:** concrete mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30). **Status:** mathematically proposed, not prior-art-certified or proved. Shared definitions: [COMMON_MODELS](COMMON_MODELS.md). Historical candidate: [GC92](../problems/02-confinement-curvature/GC92.md).

## Exact question

Let \(\mathcal C\) consist of scalar operators \(C(u)=K_1(u)+\operatorname{div}F(u,\nabla u)\), where \(F\) is a real polynomial vector of total degree \(\le4\) in real and imaginary components of \(u,\nabla u\), satisfying phase, translation and proper-rotation covariance, and \(\operatorname{div}F(Ae^{i\xi\cdot x})=0\) for every \(A>0,\xi\in\mathbb R^2\). Classify the real vector space of such F modulo identically divergence-free polynomial currents.

## Proof or counterexample acceptance

The class, algebraic degree, equivalence and conservation-by-divergence convention are fixed. Show a basis and dimension or prove proposed generators are incomplete; uniqueness is not presupposed.
