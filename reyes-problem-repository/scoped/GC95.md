# GC95: Sobolev continuity threshold of K

**Version:** concrete mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30). **Status:** mathematically proposed, not prior-art-certified or proved. Shared definitions: [COMMON_MODELS](COMMON_MODELS.md). Historical candidate: [GC95](../problems/02-confinement-curvature/GC95.md).

## Exact question

For \(1\le s\le3\), define \(H^s=H^s(\mathbb R^2;\mathbb C)\), and interpret \(K_1(u)=\operatorname{div}(\operatorname{Im}(\bar u\nabla u)/(1+|u|^2))\) in distributions. Classify the set \(S=\{s\in[1,3]:K_1:H^s\to L^2\text{ is locally Lipschitz on every Hs ball}\}\).

## Proof or counterexample acceptance

Give product/chain estimates for all asserted s and explicit bounded Hs sequences violating L² mapping or Lipschitz continuity at excluded s. The epsilon and target norm are fixed.
