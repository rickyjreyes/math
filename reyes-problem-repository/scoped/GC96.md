# GC96: Splitting of a double-trap eigenvalue

**Version:** concrete mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30). **Status:** mathematically proposed, not prior-art-certified or proved. Shared definitions: [COMMON_MODELS](COMMON_MODELS.md). Historical candidate: [GC96](../problems/02-confinement-curvature/GC96.md).

## Exact question

Let \(H_R=(\Delta+1)^2-2e^{-|x-Re_1|^2}-2e^{-|x+Re_1|^2}\) on \(H^4(\mathbb R^2)\), \(R>1\), and let \(H_\infty=(\Delta+1)^2-2e^{-|x|^2}\). Determine whether \(H_\infty\) has a simple negative ground eigenvalue \(\lambda_*\) and, if so, whether \(H_R\) has exactly two eigenvalues \(\lambda_1(R)\le\lambda_2(R)\) converging to \(\lambda_*\), with a sharp asymptotic for \(\lambda_2-\lambda_1\).

## Proof or counterexample acceptance

The existential one-trap eigenvalue check comes first; the splitting theorem must identify spectral multiplicity and remainder, not assume every well has a simple isolated mode.
