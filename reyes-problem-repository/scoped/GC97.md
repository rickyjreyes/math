# GC97: Vortex energy in a disk

**Version:** concrete mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30). **Status:** mathematically proposed, not prior-art-certified or proved. Shared definitions: [COMMON_MODELS](COMMON_MODELS.md). Historical candidate: [GC97](../problems/02-confinement-curvature/GC97.md).

## Exact question

Let \(B\) be the unit disk and \(n\in\mathbb Z\setminus\{0\}\). For \(0<\delta<1\), minimize \(E_{\delta,n}(u)=\int_B(|\nabla u|^2+\delta^{-2}(1-|u|^2)^2+|K_1(u)|^2)dx\) over \(u\in H^2(B;\mathbb C)\) with trace \(u(e^{i\theta})=e^{in\theta}\), where K uses distributional derivatives on B. Determine the sharp order of \(\inf E_{\delta,n}\) as \(\delta\downarrow0\).

## Proof or counterexample acceptance

Interior zeros are allowed; boundary winding is frozen. Prove a matching lower/upper bound or construct a family invalidating a proposed vortex-cost asymptotic.
