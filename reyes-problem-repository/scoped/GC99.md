# GC99: Dimension and curvature-constrained attainment

**Version:** concrete mathematical target v1 (2026-10-30). **Status:** mathematically proposed, not prior-art-certified or proved. Shared definitions: [COMMON_MODELS](COMMON_MODELS.md). Historical candidate: [GC99](../problems/02-confinement-curvature/GC99.md).

## Exact question

For \(d\in\{1,2,3,4\}\), define \(K_1^{(d)}(u)=\operatorname{div}(\operatorname{Im}(\bar u\nabla u)/(1+|u|^2))\) for \(u\in H^2(\mathbb R^d)\), and \(E_d(u)=\|(\Delta+1)u\|_2^2-\tfrac12\|u\|_4^4+\tfrac13\|u\|_6^6\). For \(m,B>0\), determine which triples \((d,m,B)\) attain \(\inf\{E_d(u):\|u\|_2^2=m,\ \|K_1^{(d)}(u)\|_2^2\le B\}\).

## Proof or counterexample acceptance

The H² products underlying K require special checking for d=4; do not extend blindly to d≥5, where the natural domain may not support L² curvature.
