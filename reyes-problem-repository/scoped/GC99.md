## Candidate mathematical question

Determine how the critical dimensions for attained constrained minimizers change when a curvature-energy constraint is added.

**Quantifiers and domain discipline:** Freeze translation/phase quotient, H² norm, domain and boundary geometry; specify whether a constant is uniform over λ and ε. If a curved manifold is essential, define its metric, Laplace–Beltrami operator and volume form anew.

**Observable to define explicitly:** coercivity, minimizer compactness, curvature norm or localization radius. Provide the exact norm, parameter ranges, and dependence of any claimed constant, bound, rate or threshold on all inputs.

## First decisive result

Establish a dimension-dependent existence threshold or nonattainment construction.

**Valid negative outcomes:** A minimizing sequence escaping after symmetry reduction, a zero-amplitude pathology or a failure of uniform coercivity.

State at least one precisely checkable theorem or counterexample on the benchmark. If the desired reference state, operator or measurement does not exist, that nonvacuity obstruction must be resolved **before** treating any conditional conclusion as substantive.
